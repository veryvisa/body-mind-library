It's OK not to finish

This book is a toolbox, not a test. You don't have to read it in order, and you don't have to read all of it. One small block today is plenty.

Pick a rhythm

RhythmWhat a day looks likeWhole book
GentleOne activity a day, 15–20 minutes, weekdays onlyabout 9–10 weeks
LightJust the map and cards of one activity, about 10 minutesas long as you like
Pick and chooseOnly the activities that fit what's hard right now. Good first picks: 1, 3, 28, 30, 34a few days

Today, one block is great. Missed a week? Start again from wherever you like — nothing is lost.

Stuck? Three steps

1Change the form. Listen, or read only the one-sentence version.
2Look at the picture. The mind map or the figure often says it faster.
3Skip it. The next activity doesn't depend on this one. You can come back.
If you often feel worthless, hopeless or unsafe — or if these thoughts get in the way of school, sleep or friends — tell an adult you trust.
