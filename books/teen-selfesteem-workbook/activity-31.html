Step 2 · The main line

In one sentence

Core idea

How you carry yourself shows your self-esteem, and acting a little more confident and friendly can help you feel that way.

What the author is getting at

Confidence shows in how you move and look, and using that look on purpose can help you feel it. Treat it as a gentle experiment, not a performance you must get right.

Step 5 · Four angles

Look at it from four sides

What the book says

Confidence shows in how you move and look, and using that look on purpose can help you feel it. Treat it as a gentle experiment, not a performance you must get right.

Common mix-up

A common misreading

'Looking confident means faking who I am.' Better: it is a way of practicing the calm you want, and it works with your authentic self rather than against it. If you feel low for a long time, a trusted adult or counselor can help; posture is not a cure.

Does it still hold today?

What research says (open when you're curious)

In a randomized trial of 74 adults, those held in an upright seated posture reported higher self-esteem and better mood during a stressful speech task than those held slumped (Nair et al., 2015). The study was small, brief and with adults, so it hints that posture can influence feelings but does not show lasting change in teens. Claims about hormonal effects of power poses have failed to replicate.

  • Nair S, Sagar M, Sollers J, Consedine N, Broadbent E. Do slumped and upright postures affect stress responses? A randomized trial. Health Psychol 2015;34(6):632-41. link
