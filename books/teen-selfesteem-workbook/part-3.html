Part 3 · Discovering yourself

Activities 12–20 · The book itself has no parts; this six-part grouping is ours, to give a map of the 46 activities.

12 · It's Normal to Not Know
Teen years are for trying ideas out, so being unsure about who you are and what comes next is normal, and you can unfold one step at a time.
13 · Discovering Your Likes and Dislikes
Your likes and dislikes, even small ones, add up to a one-of-a-kind picture of you, and noticing them builds a stronger sense of self.
14 · Discovering Your Dreams
Daydreams, night dreams, and wishes for the future hint at what you value and long for, even when they are hazy or conflicted.
15 · Discovering Your Beliefs
Many beliefs are handed to you; looking at each one lets you keep what is truly yours and rewrite what is not.
16 · Discovering Your Passions
A passion can be an idea, a thing, an activity, or a person, and noticing what moves you deeply points toward your authentic self.
17 · You in the Universe
When a decision is stressful, step back from what others will think and ask what kind of person you want to be, then let that answer steer what you do.
18 · Why You Are Here
Nobody else has your exact mix of gifts, so exploring your own purpose gives you something steady to hold on to when others pull you away.
19 · Celebrate Your Strengths, Work with Your Weaknesses
Everyone has both strengths and weaknesses, and neither changes your worth, so celebrate the first and, where it matters, work on the second.
20 · The Power of Intuition
Intuition is a deep felt sense you notice in body and mind; learning to listen to it, while checking anything risky, helps you stay true to yourself.
