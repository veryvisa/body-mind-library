Step 2 · The main line

In one sentence

Core idea

Big goals work better as long-term goals, climbed by realistic short-term steps, and each step you reach feeds healthier self-esteem.

What the author is getting at

Goals set too high drain you and usually end in quitting. Treat the big one as a long-term goal and build realistic short-term steps toward it, and you collect successes that strengthen self-esteem.

What is a realistic goal?

How are short-term and long-term goals different?
Answer in your head, then tap to flip
Short-term goals are for the near future; long-term goals take more time. Which is which depends on your age and situation.
Activity 43 · Setting Realistic Goals
Why do big goals so often fail?
Answer in your head, then tap to flip
Trying to change everything at once drains your energy and adds pressure, so most people quit early.

How do goals connect to self-esteem?
Answer in your head, then tap to flip
Reaching realistic goals gives you real successes, and those build healthier self-esteem; aiming too high and missing can leave you feeling worse.

Step 5 · Four angles

Look at it from four sides

What the book says

Goals set too high drain you and usually end in quitting. Treat the big one as a long-term goal and build realistic short-term steps toward it, and you collect successes that strengthen self-esteem.

Common mix-up

A common misreading

'Realistic means settling for less.' Not in this activity: the dream stays on top of the ladder. Realistic only means the steps are the right size for now. If missing goals leaves you feeling low a lot, a trusted adult or counselor can help.

Does it still hold today?

What research says (open when you're curious)

Locke and Latham's review of 35 years of goal-setting research summarizes the core findings, how goals work, and the limits of the theory (Locke & Latham, 2002). I only read the abstract, which does not list specific effects, so I make no claim here about how large they are; the link to teen self-esteem is the book's own idea and I did not verify it.

  • Locke EA, Latham GP. Building a practically useful theory of goal setting and task motivation: A 35-year odyssey. Am Psychol 2002;57(9):705-717. doi:10.1037/0003-066X.57.9.705 link

How it connects

Activity 14 (dreams) supplies what to aim at; Activity 41 (positive decisions) and Activity 44 (problem solving) help with each step. Activity 45 (turn things around) treats a turnaround as exactly this kind of long-term goal.
