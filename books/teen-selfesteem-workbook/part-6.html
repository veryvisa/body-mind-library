Part 6 · Conviction, responsibility, goals and asking for help

Activities 39–46 · The book itself has no parts; this six-part grouping is ours, to give a map of the 46 activities.

39 · The Power of Conviction
Conviction is a deep belief in yourself and your goals that helps you keep going through challenges without giving up on who you are.
40 · The Power of Responsibility
Pointing at others for how your life is going hands your power away; owning your thoughts, feelings and actions gets it back.
41 · The Power of Positive Decisions
A positive decision is the one most likely to bring a good outcome, even when it is not the easiest, and it also protects how you feel about yourself.
42 · The Power of Facing Challenges Head-On
Ignoring or avoiding a problem usually stacks a second problem on top of the first; facing it is what actually helps and builds self-esteem.
43 · Setting Realistic Goals
Big goals work better as long-term goals, climbed by realistic short-term steps, and each step you reach feeds healthier self-esteem.
44 · Problem-Solving Skills
A six-step plan, calm first, then define, brainstorm, choose, test and adjust, makes problems feel manageable and builds confidence.
45 · It's Never Too Late to Turn Things Around
No matter what has happened, you can start fresh at any moment, and each step of turning things around turns your self-esteem around too.
46 · Smart People Ask for Help
Asking for help is a sign of wisdom, not weakness: the right help gets you better results, and you can build a list of people you trust.
