Step 2 · The main line

In one sentence

Core idea

A healthy boundary is a limit you set to protect yourself from disrespect or harm, and acting on it supports your self-esteem.

What the author is getting at

Boundaries limit abuse of any kind and protect you. Leaving or limiting contact with someone who disrespects you is a way of respecting yourself, even when it is hard.

Step 5 · Four angles

Look at it from four sides

What the book says

Boundaries limit abuse of any kind and protect you. Leaving or limiting contact with someone who disrespects you is a way of respecting yourself, even when it is hard.

Common mix-up

A common misreading

'Setting a boundary is mean or selfish.' A better reading: it is care for yourself, and you can still be kind. If someone is hurting or threatening you, do not handle it alone; tell a parent, counselor or another trusted adult right away.

Does it still hold today?

What research says (open when you're curious)

In a study of 1,182 students across four multi-session school programs on preventing sexual violence, participants reported more positive attitudes and intended behaviors about personal boundaries and healthy dating norms after the programs than before (Clinton-Sherrod et al., 2009). This was a pre-post survey without a comparison group, so it suggests such lessons can shift attitudes but cannot show lasting behavior change.

  • Clinton-Sherrod M, Morgan-Lopez AA, Gibbs D, et al. Factors contributing to the effectiveness of four school-based sexual violence interventions. Health Promot Pract 2009;10(1 Suppl):19S-28S. link
