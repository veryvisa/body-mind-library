Step 2 · The main line

In one sentence

Core idea

Nobody else has your exact mix of gifts, so exploring your own purpose gives you something steady to hold on to when others pull you away.

What the author is getting at

Having a purpose of your own gives you confidence in your value. You do not have to know it today; exploring and staying true to yourself is how you get closer.

Step 5 · Four angles

Look at it from four sides

What the book says

Having a purpose of your own gives you confidence in your value. You do not have to know it today; exploring and staying true to yourself is how you get closer.

Common mix-up

A common misreading

'I must figure out my one big purpose right now, or I am behind.' The activity says the opposite: not knowing is okay, and a purpose can start small and change as you learn.

Does it still hold today?

What research says (open when you're curious)

In one set of three studies, young people who felt a stronger commitment to a purpose reported more positive feelings, hope and happiness, and purpose appeared to help explain why a clear sense of identity went with better well-being (Burrow & Hill, 2011). These are mostly self-reports at a point in time, so they show a link and do not prove purpose causes well-being.

  • Burrow AL, Hill PL. Purpose as a form of identity capital for positive youth adjustment. Dev Psychol 2011;47(4):1196-1206. link

How it connects

Follows Activity 17 (you in the universe) and links to Activity 16 (discovering your passions), Activity 19 (strengths and weaknesses) and Activity 20 (intuition), which all help you spot your direction.
