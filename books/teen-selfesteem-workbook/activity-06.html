Step 2 · The main line

In one sentence

Core idea

Compassion is caring about the struggles of others and yourself, and you already know how to do it, so you can turn it on yourself with the same words and actions.

What the author is getting at

Compassion for every person, yourself included, is a cornerstone of healthy self-esteem. You already know how to be compassionate to others, so you can learn to direct it inward.

Step 5 · Four angles

Look at it from four sides

What the book says

Compassion for every person, yourself included, is a cornerstone of healthy self-esteem. You already know how to be compassionate to others, so you can learn to direct it inward.

Common mix-up

A common misreading

A common wrong reading: 'Being kind to myself is weak or selfish.' A better one: care helps you get back up. Self-compassion is not a lack of effort. If you are struggling a lot, reaching out to a trusted adult is itself an act of self-compassion.

Does it still hold today?

What research says (open when you're curious)

A meta-analysis of 79 samples found that more self-compassion goes with better well-being, with a correlation around .47, and a few studies hinted at a causal effect (Zessin et al., 2015). A meta-analysis of 19 studies of adolescents found that higher self-compassion goes with lower anxiety, depression, and stress, about -.55 (Marsh et al., 2018). These are mostly correlations, so they show a link, not proof that practice alone fixes distress.

  • Zessin U, Dickhäuser O, Garbade S. The relationship between self-compassion and well-being: a meta-analysis. Appl Psychol Health Well Being 2015;7(3):340-364. doi:10.1111/aphw.12051 link
  • Marsh IC, Chan SWY, MacBeth A. Self-compassion and psychological distress in adolescents - a meta-analysis. Mindfulness 2018;9:1011-1027. doi:10.1007/s12671-017-0850-7 link

How it connects
