Step 2 · The main line

In one sentence

Core idea

Pointing at others for how your life is going hands your power away; owning your thoughts, feelings and actions gets it back.

What the author is getting at

Blaming people or circumstances gives your power away and breeds negativity. Owning your thoughts, feelings and actions returns it and helps you grow into your authentic self.

What does taking responsibility actually mean?
Answer in your head, then tap to flip

Is understanding where low self-esteem came from the same as blaming?
Answer in your head, then tap to flip
No. Understanding is just seeing what happened. After that, the repair work is yours to do.

What can you do when someone else's choice hurts you?
Answer in your head, then tap to flip
Tell them how you feel, ask what you could do differently, and decide that their choice will not run your happiness.

Step 5 · Four angles

Look at it from four sides

What the book says

Blaming people or circumstances gives your power away and breeds negativity. Owning your thoughts, feelings and actions returns it and helps you grow into your authentic self.

Common mix-up

A common misreading

'Taking responsibility means it was all my fault.' Not quite: other people's choices can really hurt, and feeling upset is fair. Responsibility is only about your own next move and your own feelings. If you carry something heavy, a trusted adult or counselor can help.
