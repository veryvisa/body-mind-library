Part 2 · Being you with family, friends and society

Activities 7–11 · The book itself has no parts; this six-part grouping is ours, to give a map of the 46 activities.

7 · The Perfection of Diversity
Nature runs on variety, you cannot become someone else, so trying to be the best version of yourself is the path that works.
8 · Your Authentic Self Is Your Best Self
Your authentic self is who you are before you bend to other people's expectations, and checking your choices against it helps you find and trust yourself.
9 · Who You Are for Your Family
Your family hands you roles and expectations, and noticing them is a first step toward telling which choices are really yours.
10 · Who You Are for Your Friends
Friends can change how you think, feel, and act; changing because you enjoy a situation is fine, changing only to fit in is a game.
11 · Who You Are for Society
Society sends messages about how to look, act, and belong; you can notice them and choose which to follow, as long as your choices stay safe.
