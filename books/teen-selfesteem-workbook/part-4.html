Part 4 · Your body, other people's opinions, and relationships

Activities 21–31 · The book itself has no parts; this six-part grouping is ours, to give a map of the 46 activities.

21 · Your Body Image: How to Fight False Facts
Beauty ideals are often edited, changeable, and used to sell products, so you can stop letting them set how you feel about your looks.
22 · Your Body Image: Loving the Body You Have
Shifting attention from how your body looks to what it does for you, and to the many qualities that make people valuable, can lift your self-esteem.
23 · About Judgment
People often judge to feel briefly better about themselves, and your worth does not depend on their judgment, so you can stop judging and stop taking it personally.
24 · Take Charge of Social Media
Social media has real benefits, but comparing, cyberbullying and fear of missing out can wear down self-esteem, so notice your own patterns and use small tools to stay in charge.
25 · How to Talk to People: Basic Social Skills
You do not need a special personality to talk to people; six plain habits, practiced often, make conversation easier and lift your confidence.
26 · Assertive Communication
Assertive communication means standing up for yourself calmly while respecting others, and it tends to bring better results than staying silent or lashing out.
27 · Don't Take Everything Personally
Other people's words and actions often come from their own day, not from you, so check before deciding something is about you.
28 · No One Is Liked by Everyone
Being liked by every single person is an impossible goal, and your worth does not rise or fall with who likes you.
29 · Peer Pressure
Peer pressure works best when standing up for yourself feels too hard, so know your own values and have a few easy ways to say no.
30 · Setting Healthy Boundaries
A healthy boundary is a limit you set to protect yourself from disrespect or harm, and acting on it supports your self-esteem.
31 · The Look of Healthy Self-Esteem
How you carry yourself shows your self-esteem, and acting a little more confident and friendly can help you feel that way.
