Part 1 · What self-esteem is, and who holds the power

Activities 1–6 · The book itself has no parts; this six-part grouping is ours, to give a map of the 46 activities.

1 · What Is Healthy Self-Esteem?
Healthy self-esteem is a quiet, steady feeling that you and everyone else matter equally, so you can own your flaws and enjoy your strengths without putting anyone down.
2 · Recognize Your Power
Other people and hard events can hurt, but the final thoughts about yourself are yours to choose, so your self-esteem can start changing at any moment.
3 · Claim Your Intrinsic Value and Worth
Your worth was there from the start, it is the same as everyone's, and mistakes or hurtful words cannot remove it; the voices saying otherwise are messages, not facts.
4 · Release Devaluing Messages
We pick up messages about our worth from family, media and culture, often indirectly, and once we spot them we can choose to drop the ones that shrink us.
5 · Choose Positive Self-Messages
You talk to yourself all day, those messages help set your self-esteem, and you can notice them, test them, and write kinder ones.
6 · The Power of Self-Compassion
Compassion is caring about the struggles of others and yourself, and you already know how to do it, so you can turn it on yourself with the same words and actions.
