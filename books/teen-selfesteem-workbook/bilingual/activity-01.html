Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Healthy self-esteem is a quiet, steady feeling that you and everyone else matter equally, so you can own your flaws and enjoy your strengths without putting anyone down.健康的自尊是一种安静而稳的感觉：你和别人一样重要，所以能坦然承认缺点、享受优点，也不必踩低别人。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Self-esteem is your inner sense of worth. Healthy self-esteem lets you accept faults, enjoy strengths, respect others, and not depend on wins or praise.自尊是内在的价值感。健康的自尊让你能接受缺点、享受长处、尊重别人，而不靠赢或夸奖来撑着。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Self-esteem is your inner sense of worth. Healthy self-esteem lets you accept faults, enjoy strengths, respect others, and not depend on wins or praise.

自尊是内在的价值感。健康的自尊让你能接受缺点、享受长处、尊重别人，而不靠赢或夸奖来撑着。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

A common wrong reading: 'Healthy self-esteem means I must feel great about myself all the time.' A better one: it means your worth stays put on bad days too. You can feel sad or embarrassed and still know you matter.

常见的误读：「健康的自尊就是时时觉得自己很棒。」更好的理解：它是指糟糕的日子里价值感依然在。你可以难过、尴尬，同时仍知道自己很重要。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Researchers have argued about this for years. A large review concluded that high self-esteem has not been shown to cause better grades or performance, since the links are modest and hard to untangle from cause and effect (Baumeister et al., 2003). A later review of many studies found that higher self-esteem goes with somewhat better outcomes in relationships, school, work and mental health, but the average effect is small (about .10) (Orth & Robins, 2022). So it helps a little and is not a magic switch.

研究者争论多年。一篇大型综述认为，高自尊并没被证明会带来更好的成绩或表现，因为关联不大，也很难分清因果（Baumeister 等，2003）。后来一篇汇总多项研究的综述发现，自尊较高的人在人际、学业、工作和心理健康上结果略好，但平均效应很小（约 .10）（Orth 与 Robins，2022）。所以它有些帮助，却不是万能开关。

  • Baumeister RF, Campbell JD, Krueger JI, Vohs KD. Does high self-esteem cause better performance, interpersonal success, happiness, or healthier lifestyles? Psychol Sci Public Interest 2003;4(1):1-44. link链接
