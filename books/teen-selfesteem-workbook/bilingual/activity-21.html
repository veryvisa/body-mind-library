Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Beauty ideals are often edited, changeable, and used to sell products, so you can stop letting them set how you feel about your looks.美丽标准常常经过修图、随年代变化，还被用来卖东西，所以你可以不再让它们决定你对自己外貌的感受。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Self-esteem takes a hit when we compare ourselves with an unreachable, edited ideal that businesses promote. Seeing how it works lets you choose not to be controlled by it.当我们拿自己去和商家推广的、不可能达到的、修过的理想比，自尊就会受损。看清它的运作方式，你就可以选择不被它控制。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Self-esteem takes a hit when we compare ourselves with an unreachable, edited ideal that businesses promote. Seeing how it works lets you choose not to be controlled by it.

当我们拿自己去和商家推广的、不可能达到的、修过的理想比，自尊就会受损。看清它的运作方式，你就可以选择不被它控制。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'If I just looked more like the ads, I would feel fine.' The ideal keeps moving and the images are edited, so there is nothing fixed to match. Feeling better comes more from changing how much power the ideal has over you. If worries about your body feel heavy, a trusted adult or counselor can help.

「只要我更像广告里的人，就会没事了。」标准总在变，图片又是修过的，根本没有固定的目标可以达到。更好受是因为你减少了这个标准对你的影响力。如果对身体的担心很沉重，可以找信任的大人或辅导员帮忙。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

A meta-analysis of 77 studies found that exposure to media showing the thin ideal was linked to more body dissatisfaction in women, with effects described as small to moderate (Grabe et al., 2008). The samples were mostly women and the work is older, so it supports the book's point about media pressure but does not speak to every teen or to newer platforms.

一项涉及 77 项研究的元分析发现，接触展示偏瘦理想身材的媒体，与女性更多的身体不满相关，效应为小到中等（Grabe 等，2008）。样本主要是女性，研究也较早，所以它支持书里关于媒体压力的观点，但不能代表每位青少年，也不涵盖较新的平台。

  • Grabe S, Ward LM, Hyde JS. The role of the media in body image concerns among women: a meta-analysis of experimental and correlational studies. Psychol Bull 2008;134(3):460-476. link链接
