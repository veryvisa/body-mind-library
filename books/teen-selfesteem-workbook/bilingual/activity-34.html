Core idea核心

Calm is not something you must go out and get; it is already in you, and simple practices help you reach it.平静不用向外去求，它本来就在你心里，一些简单的练习可以帮你找到它。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Peace is a base you can return to, and the pages ask you to recall calm and agitated times, check which calming practices you already use, and commit to trying one at a realistic time, with small goals that grow gradually.

平静是你可以回去的基地。书里请你回想平静和烦躁的时刻，勾出已经在用的放松方式，约定一个实际的时间试一个，并定逐步增加的小目标。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

Wrong reading: if I do not feel calm right away, I am doing it wrong. Better reading: calm grows with practice, and trying different methods is part of it. If you feel on edge much of the time, a trusted adult or counselor can help.

常见误读：没有马上平静就是我做错了。更好的理解：平静是练出来的，换着试本来就是过程。如果你常常觉得心绪不宁，可以找信任的大人或辅导员聊聊。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

An updated review of 66 randomized trials of mindfulness programs for people under 19 found small benefits for anxiety and stress, attention and some behaviours compared with doing nothing, but few lasting effects at follow-up. The authors called the evidence low quality and inconclusive (Dunning et al., 2022). So calming practices may help in the moment, and big promises deserve caution.

一篇更新的综述汇总了 66 项针对 19 岁以下人群的正念课程随机试验，发现与不干预相比，在焦虑与压力、注意力和部分行为上有小幅好处，但随访时多数效果没有保持。作者称证据质量低、尚无定论（Dunning 等，2022）。所以放松练习也许当下有帮助，但不宜夸大承诺。

  • Dunning D, Tudor K, Radley L, et al. Do mindfulness-based programmes improve the cognitive skills, behaviour and mental health of children and adolescents? An updated meta-analysis of randomised controlled trials. Evid Based Ment Health 2022;25(3):135-142. doi:10.1136/ebmental-2022-300464 link链接
