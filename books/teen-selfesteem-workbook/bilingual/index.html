A steadier way to see yourself更稳一点地看待自己

Self-esteem is not a prize you win. It is a way of seeing yourself that you can practise. This book gives you 46 short activities for it — and you don't need all of them.自尊不是考出来的奖状，而是一种可以练习的看待自己的方式。这本书给你 46 个小练习——你不需要每一个都做。

Read at your own speed. Nothing here is a test, and nothing is timed.按你自己的速度来。这里没有考试，也不计时。

The book in one minute一分钟读懂

  1. First, what healthy self-esteem is and who holds the controls: your worth does not depend on winning, and the messages that put you down can be let go (Activities 1–6).先弄清什么是健康的自尊、谁握着方向盘：你的价值不靠赢来，那些贬低你的声音可以放下（练习 1–6）。
  2. Then being you in a family, among friends and in society, and discovering who you are: likes, dreams, beliefs, passions, strengths, weaknesses, intuition (7–20).然后是在家人、朋友和社会面前做自己，并认识自己：喜好、梦想、信念、热情、长处与短处、直觉（7–20）。
  3. Next, your body image, other people's opinions and the skills of getting along: social media, judgment, assertiveness, boundaries, peer pressure (21–31).接着是身体形象、别人的看法和相处的本领：社交媒体、评判、坚定表达、界限、同伴压力（21–31）。
  4. Finally, inner skills and habits — feelings, discomfort, inner peace, thinking habits, mistakes, gratitude, possibility (32–38) — then conviction, responsibility, goals and asking for help (39–46).最后是内在能力与习惯——情绪、不舒服、内心平静、思维习惯、犯错、感恩、可能性（32–38）——再到信念、责任、目标和求助（39–46）。

Three ways to read — all of them count三种读法——每一种都算读完

Just listen只听Put on headphones. One episode per part or question. That counts as reading.戴上耳机，一集一个部分或一个问题。这也算读完。Just maps and cards只看导图和卡片Look at the map of each activity, flip its cards. Ten minutes a day is plenty.看每个练习的导图，翻它的卡片。一天十分钟就够了。Read it all完整读One activity at a time, one screen at a time, with the workbook open next to you.一次一个练习、一屏一件事，原书摊在旁边。
