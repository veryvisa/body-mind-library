Method 1 of 6第 1 种，共 6 种

Mind map思维导图

When to use it什么时候用Before you start an activity, to see where things go — or after, to see it all at once.开始一个练习之前，先看东西都放在哪；或者读完后，一眼看全。
How to start怎么开始Write the topic in the middle. Add three to five branches for the big ideas. Hang short words on each branch — words, not sentences.把主题写在中间。画三到五个分支放大想法。每个分支上挂几个短词——是词，不是句子。
Five-minute version五分钟版本Five-minute timer: one middle word, three branches, two leaves on each. Stop when it rings.定时 5 分钟：一个中心词、三个分支、每支两片叶子。铃一响就停。

Method 2 of 6第 2 种，共 6 种

Cornell notes康奈尔笔记

When to use it什么时候用While you read or listen, when you want notes you can quiz yourself with later.边读边听的时候，想要以后能拿来自测的笔记。
How to start怎么开始Split the page: a narrow cue column on the left, notes on the right, a summary strip at the bottom. Notes first; afterward write one question per note on the left; cover the right side and answer.把纸分三块：左边窄的提示栏、右边笔记栏、底部总结栏。先写右边的笔记；读完在左边给每条笔记写一个问题；盖住右边自己答。
Five-minute version五分钟版本Read one short section. Three notes on the right, three questions on the left, one summary sentence.读一小段。右边三条笔记，左边三个问题，底下一句总结。

Example: Activity 6示范：练习 6

Cues (questions)提示（问题）
Notes笔记
What is compassion?什么是同情关怀？
Caring about someone's struggle and wanting to help, which can include yourself.在乎某个人的难处并想帮他，也可以对象是你自己。
Why does the book start with feelings for others?为什么书先从对别人的感受入手？
Because we usually find it easy to care about a friend, a puppy, or a stranger in trouble, and that gives us words and actions to borrow.因为我们通常很容易关心朋友、小狗或遇难的陌生人，这给了我们可以借用的话和做法。
How is self-compassion different from letting yourself off the hook?自我关怀和放纵自己有什么不同？
It does not say your mistake does not matter. It says you are a person having a hard time and deserve care while you fix it.它不是说错误不重要，而是说你是一个正在难受的人，在你去改正的过程中也值得被善待。
What is a compassion letter?什么是「关怀信」？
A letter you write to yourself about a current struggle, using the tone you would use for your best friend.你写给自己的信，讲你眼下的一个难处，用你对最好的朋友会用的语气。
Summary总结: Compassion is caring about the struggles of others and yourself, and you already know how to do it, so you can turn it on yourself with the same words and actions.同情关怀是在乎别人和自己的难处，你本来就会，所以可以用同样的话和行动对准自己。

Method 3 of 6第 3 种，共 6 种

Explain it simply (Feynman)讲给别人听（费曼法）

When to use it什么时候用When you think you understand something but aren't quite sure.觉得自己懂了，但又不太确定的时候。
How to start怎么开始Explain it out loud or on paper as if to a younger friend, in plain words. Wherever you get stuck or reach for a fancy word, go back to the book. Then explain it again, shorter.用大白话，像讲给比你小的朋友那样，说出来或写下来。哪里卡住、哪里想用大词，就回去看书。然后再讲一遍，更短。
Five-minute version五分钟版本Two minutes explaining, two minutes checking the gaps, one minute saying it in a single sentence.两分钟讲、两分钟查漏洞、一分钟压成一句话。

Example: Activity 6示范：练习 6

You know how it's easy to say 'it's okay, everyone messes up' to a friend who forgot a line on stage? Self-compassion is saying that same thing to yourself. Most of us have plenty of kindness for other people and almost none left for ourselves. The book says you already know how to do it, so you borrow the words and the small actions you'd use for a friend, and point them at yourself. It isn't excusing the mistake. It's staying on your own side while you fix it.你知道吗，朋友在台上忘词，我们很容易对他说「没关系，谁都会出错」。自我关怀就是把同一句话说给自己听。多数人对别人有很多温柔，对自己却几乎没有。书里说你本来就会，所以把对朋友用的话和小动作借过来，对准自己就行。这不是给错误找借口，而是在改正的时候，仍然站在自己这一边。

Where I got stuck: is this just letting myself off the hook? I checked the cards: no. Compassion says I deserve care while I fix it. Now I can explain it.我卡住的地方：这不就是放过自己吗？回去翻了卡片：不是。关怀说的是，我在改正的同时也值得被善待。现在我讲得出来了。

Shorter, in one sentence:再短一点，一句话： If I can be kind to a friend who is struggling, I can try being kind to myself the same way.如果我能善待一个难受的朋友，我也可以试着同样善待自己。

Method 4 of 6第 4 种，共 6 种

Question chain问题链

When to use it什么时候用When an idea feels flat and you want to go deeper — or when you feel stuck.觉得一个想法很平、想往深里走，或者卡住了的时候。
How to start怎么开始Start with one question. Answer it in one line. Turn the answer into the next question — why? how? what if? Go four or five links deep.从一个问题开始。用一行回答。把答案变成下一个问题——为什么？怎么做？如果……呢？往下走四五环。
Five-minute version五分钟版本One starting question and four links, one line each.一个起始问题，四环追问，每环一行。

Example: Activity 6示范：练习 6

  1. What does it feel like when I care about a friend who is struggling?朋友遇到难处时，我是什么感觉？
    I want to sit with them, say something gentle and maybe do something small to help.我想陪着他，说点温和的话，也许做点小事帮他。
  2. Why can't I do the same for myself?为什么我不能对自己也这样？
    Often I treat myself as if I should know better. But a friend is allowed to be having a hard time, and so am I (Activity 3).常常我觉得自己「应该做得更好」。可朋友可以难受，我也可以（练习 3）。
  3. Isn't that just making excuses?这不就是给自己找借口吗？
    No. Compassion doesn't say the mistake doesn't matter; it says I deserve care while I fix it (Activity 36).不是。关怀不是说错误不重要，而是说我在改正的同时也值得被善待（练习 36）。
  4. What would I actually say to myself?我具体会对自己说什么？
    The same kind words I picked for a friend: it's hard, it happens to everyone, I'm still here for you.就是我给朋友挑的那几句：很难受，谁都会遇到，我陪着你。
  5. Where do I try it first?我从哪里开始试？
    Write one short compassion letter about one small struggle this week, in my best friend's voice.这周挑一件小小的难处，用最好朋友的口气写一封短短的关怀信。

Method 5 of 6第 5 种，共 6 种

Concept map概念地图

When to use it什么时候用When the links between ideas matter as much as the ideas themselves.当想法之间的联系和想法本身一样重要的时候。
How to start怎么开始Put five to eight key ideas in boxes. Draw arrows between them and write a linking phrase on every arrow (shows up as, leads to, helps you…). Each arrow should read as a sentence.把五到八个关键概念放进方框。在它们之间画箭头，每个箭头上写一个连接短语（表现为、导致、帮你……）。每个箭头读起来都应该是一句话。
Five-minute version五分钟版本Five ideas, four arrows, every arrow labelled.五个概念、四个箭头，每个箭头都写上字。

Method 6 of 6第 6 种，共 6 种

One-page summary一页纸摘要

When to use it什么时候用After finishing an activity, or the night before a test — so you can review it in one minute.读完一个练习，或者考试前一晚——让自己一分钟就能复习完。
How to start怎么开始Four boxes only: the main idea in one sentence, three key points, one small picture or table, one thing I'll try. If it doesn't fit, it doesn't go in — that's the point.只有四个框：一句话主旨、三个要点、一张小图或小表、一件我要试的事。放不下的就不放——这正是关键。
Five-minute version五分钟版本One sentence, three points, a tiny sketch, one action.一句话、三个要点、一个小草图、一个行动。

Example: Activity 6示范：练习 6

Main idea一句话主旨
Compassion is caring about the struggles of others and yourself, and you already know how to do it, so you can turn it on yourself with the same words and actions.同情关怀是在乎别人和自己的难处，你本来就会，所以可以用同样的话和行动对准自己。

Three key points三个要点
  • Caring about someone's struggle and wanting to help, which can include yourself.在乎某个人的难处并想帮他，也可以对象是你自己。
  • Because we usually find it easy to care about a friend, a puppy, or a stranger in trouble, and that gives us words and actions to borrow.因为我们通常很容易关心朋友、小狗或遇难的陌生人，这给了我们可以借用的话和做法。
  • It does not say your mistake does not matter. It says you are a person having a hard time and deserve care while you fix it.它不是说错误不重要，而是说你是一个正在难受的人，在你去改正的过程中也值得被善待。
