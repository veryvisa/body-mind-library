Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Ignoring or avoiding a problem usually stacks a second problem on top of the first; facing it is what actually helps and builds self-esteem.无视或躲开问题，往往是在第一个问题上再叠一个；面对它才真的有用，也能养出自尊。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

When a situation looks difficult it is tempting to ignore or dodge it, but this makes things worse. Facing challenges helps you feel better about yourself and builds healthy self-esteem.情况看着难，人就想无视或绕开，可这样只会更糟。面对挑战，让你对自己感觉更好，也养出健康的自尊。

What the book says书里怎么说

When a situation looks difficult it is tempting to ignore or dodge it, but this makes things worse. Facing challenges helps you feel better about yourself and builds healthy self-esteem.

情况看着难，人就想无视或绕开，可这样只会更糟。面对挑战，让你对自己感觉更好，也养出健康的自尊。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Facing it means doing it all alone, right now.' No. Facing a challenge can start small, like telling someone or asking for help. Some items on the book's list (hurting yourself, drugs, alcohol, not eating) are serious; if any of them are part of your life, please talk to a trusted adult or counselor soon.

「面对就是现在独自扛下来。」不是。面对可以从很小的一步开始，比如告诉某个人、请人帮忙。书里的清单有些很严重（伤害自己、药物、酒精、不吃东西）；如果其中有的出现在你的生活里，请尽快找信得过的大人或辅导老师谈谈。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

A meta-analysis of 114 studies found that using avoidance to handle emotions was linked, at a medium-to-large size, with more symptoms of anxiety, depression, eating and substance problems (Aldao, Nolen-Hoeksema & Schweizer, 2010). This is a correlation in the pooled data, so it cannot show that avoiding causes the symptoms, and it covered symptoms rather than everyday school problems.

一项汇总 114 项研究的元分析发现，用回避来应对情绪，与更多的焦虑、抑郁、进食和物质使用方面的症状有关，关联中到大（Aldao、Nolen-Hoeksema 与 Schweizer，2010）。这是汇总数据里的相关，不能证明回避导致症状，而且研究的是症状，不是日常的学校问题。

  • Aldao A, Nolen-Hoeksema S, Schweizer S. Emotion-regulation strategies across psychopathology: A meta-analytic review. Clin Psychol Rev 2010;30(2):217-237. doi:10.1016/j.cpr.2009.11.004 link链接
