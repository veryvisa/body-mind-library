Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Other people and hard events can hurt, but the final thoughts about yourself are yours to choose, so your self-esteem can start changing at any moment.别人和难事会让人受伤，但对自己的最终看法由你来选，所以自尊在任何时刻都可以开始改变。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Believing others or circumstances control your self-esteem makes you dependent on them. In truth, you choose how to think about yourself, which means you can build healthy self-esteem at any time.如果认为别人或处境掌控你的自尊，你就依赖它们。实际上你可以选择怎样想自己，所以任何时候都能开始建立健康的自尊。

What the book says书里怎么说

Believing others or circumstances control your self-esteem makes you dependent on them. In truth, you choose how to think about yourself, which means you can build healthy self-esteem at any time.

如果认为别人或处境掌控你的自尊，你就依赖它们。实际上你可以选择怎样想自己，所以任何时候都能开始建立健康的自尊。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

A common wrong reading: 'If I am in charge, then my low self-esteem is my fault.' A better one: hard things that happened to you are not your fault. Taking charge only means what you do with the thought from here on. If the past feels heavy, a trusted adult or counselor can walk through it with you.

常见的误读：「既然我说了算，那自尊低就是我的错。」更好的理解：发生在你身上的难事不是你的错；拿回主动权只是说，从现在起你怎么处理那个想法。如果过去的事让你觉得沉，可以找信任的成年人或辅导员陪你一起理一理。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Self-determination research finds that people do better in motivation and well-being when they feel some autonomy, meaning they act from their own choices rather than only from outside pressure (Ryan & Deci, 2000). That supports the idea of choosing your own stance, though it is not a study of this workbook's method itself.

自我决定理论的研究发现，人在觉得有一定自主感时，动机和幸福感更好，也就是行动出于自己的选择，而不是只受外界压力推动（Ryan 与 Deci，2000）。这支持「自己选立场」的想法，但它并不是在检验本书的这套方法。

  • Ryan RM, Deci EL. Self-determination theory and the facilitation of intrinsic motivation, social development, and well-being. Am Psychol 2000;55(1):68-78. doi:10.1037/0003-066X.55.1.68 link链接
