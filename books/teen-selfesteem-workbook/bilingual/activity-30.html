Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

A healthy boundary is a limit you set to protect yourself from disrespect or harm, and acting on it supports your self-esteem.健康的界限是你为了保护自己、不被不尊重或伤害而设下的限度，付诸行动会撑住你的自尊。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Boundaries limit abuse of any kind and protect you. Leaving or limiting contact with someone who disrespects you is a way of respecting yourself, even when it is hard.界限是对各种形式的伤害设限，并保护你。离开或减少与不尊重你的人的接触，是尊重自己的方式，即使很难。

What the book says书里怎么说

Boundaries limit abuse of any kind and protect you. Leaving or limiting contact with someone who disrespects you is a way of respecting yourself, even when it is hard.

界限是对各种形式的伤害设限，并保护你。离开或减少与不尊重你的人的接触，是尊重自己的方式，即使很难。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Setting a boundary is mean or selfish.' A better reading: it is care for yourself, and you can still be kind. If someone is hurting or threatening you, do not handle it alone; tell a parent, counselor or another trusted adult right away.

「设界限是刻薄、自私。」更好的看法：这是在照顾自己，你仍然可以友善。如果有人在伤害或威胁你，不要一个人处理，请马上告诉家长、辅导老师或其他信任的大人。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

In a study of 1,182 students across four multi-session school programs on preventing sexual violence, participants reported more positive attitudes and intended behaviors about personal boundaries and healthy dating norms after the programs than before (Clinton-Sherrod et al., 2009). This was a pre-post survey without a comparison group, so it suggests such lessons can shift attitudes but cannot show lasting behavior change.

在四个多次课程的校园性暴力预防项目中，1,182 名学生课后比课前对个人界限和健康交往规范有更正面的态度和意向（Clinton-Sherrod 等，2009）。这是没有对照组的前后问卷，只能说明这类课程可能改变态度，不能证明行为会长期改变。

  • Clinton-Sherrod M, Morgan-Lopez AA, Gibbs D, et al. Factors contributing to the effectiveness of four school-based sexual violence interventions. Health Promot Pract 2009;10(1 Suppl):19S-28S. link链接
