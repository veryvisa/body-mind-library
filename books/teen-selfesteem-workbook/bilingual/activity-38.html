Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Feeling trapped often comes from have-tos and can-nots in your thinking, and opening your mind shows more choices.觉得被困住，常常来自想法里的「必须」和「不能」，把心打开就会看到更多选择。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

A teen feeling stuck is shown that his have-tos can be questioned. The pages have you practice small changes of routine, swap thoughts that trap you for freer ones, open a door in steps as a picture of your mind, and describe a trapped situation with the mind open wider.一位觉得卡住的少年被提醒，他的「必须」可以被质疑。后面请你练习改变小习惯，把困住你的想法换成更自由的，用一步步把门打开来比喻自己的心，再用更开阔的心重新描述一个觉得被困的处境。

What does the book mean by being trapped only in thinking?书里说只被困在想法里是什么意思？

Have-tos and can-nots can feel like walls, but many are assumptions. Questioning them reveals choices.「必须」「不能」像墙一样，但很多只是假设。去问一问，就能发现选择。

It is a low-stakes warm-up for flexible thinking. The alternative need not be something you will really do.这是低风险的灵活思维热身。替代做法不一定真要去做。

What is the open-door exercise?开门练习是怎么回事？
Answer in your head, then tap to flip先在心里答，再点一下翻面
Open a door an inch, then more, and notice how much more you see. Your mind works the same way.把门开一寸、再开大些，看看多看到了多少。你的心也是这样。

Is possibility thinking the same as making a big decision right now?可能性思维等于现在就做大决定吗？
Answer in your head, then tap to flip先在心里答，再点一下翻面
No. It is opening your options so you can choose calmly and talk with someone you trust about real changes.不是。它是打开选项，让你能平静地选，真正的改变可以和信任的人商量。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

A teen feeling stuck is shown that his have-tos can be questioned. The pages have you practice small changes of routine, swap thoughts that trap you for freer ones, open a door in steps as a picture of your mind, and describe a trapped situation with the mind open wider.

一位觉得卡住的少年被提醒，他的「必须」可以被质疑。后面请你练习改变小习惯，把困住你的想法换成更自由的，用一步步把门打开来比喻自己的心，再用更开阔的心重新描述一个觉得被困的处境。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

Wrong reading: with the right attitude anything is possible, so if it is not working, I am not thinking hard enough. Better reading: widening your options helps you see real choices, though some limits are real and need support. If you feel trapped in a way that frightens you, tell a trusted adult or counselor.

常见误读：只要态度对，什么都可能，所以不行就是我想得不够。更好的理解：拓宽选项帮你看到真实的选择，但有些限制是真的，需要支持。如果你被困得让自己害怕，请告诉信任的大人或辅导员。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

A large study of nationally representative US secondary students found that a short online growth mindset lesson, which teaches that abilities can be developed, improved grades among lower-achieving students and enrolment in advanced maths, and worked best where peers supported the idea (Yeager et al., 2019). The effects were modest and depended on context, so it is not a cure-all.

一项覆盖美国中学生全国代表性样本的大型研究发现，一节不到一小时的网上成长型思维课（讲能力可以发展）提高了成绩较低学生的成绩与进阶数学课的选修，在同伴认同这一想法的环境里效果更好（Yeager 等，2019）。效果有限且取决于环境，并非万灵药。

  • Yeager DS, Hanselman P, Walton GM, et al. A national experiment reveals where a growth mindset improves achievement. Nature 2019;573(7774):364-369. doi:10.1038/s41586-019-1466-y link链接
