Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

Core idea核心

Discomfort is not a stop sign; staying with it, when it is safe, can bring you something worth having.不舒服不是停止标志；在安全的前提下留一会儿，往往能换来值得的东西。

What is the difference between discomfort and danger?不舒服和危险有什么区别？

What the book says书里怎么说

Four short stories show different responses to discomfort. The pages then ask you to rate how strong it would feel for you, picture the benefit of staying with it in several situations, and tell your own story of getting through one.

四个小故事展示了面对不舒服的不同反应。后面的页面请你评估自己会有多难受，想象在几种情境里留下来能得到什么，再讲一个自己撑过来的故事。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

Wrong reading: be tough and never admit you are uncomfortable. Better reading: notice the discomfort honestly and choose with it in view. Some situations on the pages, like a home that feels unsafe, call for talking to a trusted adult, not for putting up with it.

常见误读：要硬撑，别承认不舒服。更好的理解：诚实地看见不舒服，带着它做选择。书里有些情境，比如在家里觉得不安全，需要的是找信任的大人谈，而不是忍着。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Researchers describe distress tolerance as how well people can stay with unpleasant inner states, and a review links lower tolerance with more psychological symptoms, while noting that the concept is measured in many different ways and that most evidence comes from adults (Leyro, Zvolensky & Bernstein, 2010). So the idea is promising for teens but not firmly settled.

研究者把痛苦耐受描述为人在不愉快的内心状态下还能撑住的程度。一篇综述发现耐受较低与较多心理症状相关，同时指出这个概念的测量方式很不统一，证据也主要来自成年人（Leyro、Zvolensky 与 Bernstein，2010）。所以这个想法对青少年有前景，但还没有定论。

  • Leyro TM, Zvolensky MJ, Bernstein A. Distress tolerance and psychopathological symptoms and disorders: a review of the empirical literature among adults. Psychol Bull 2010;136(4):576-600. doi:10.1037/a0019712 link链接
