Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

No matter what has happened, you can start fresh at any moment, and each step of turning things around turns your self-esteem around too.不论发生过什么，你随时都可以重新开始；扭转局面的每一步，也在扭转你的自尊。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

It is never too late: at every moment you can start fresh, choosing to move up instead of spiraling down, and doing so turns your self-esteem around too.永远不算晚：任何时刻你都能重新开始，选择向上而不是往下滑，这样做也会扭转你的自尊。

What the book says书里怎么说

It is never too late: at every moment you can start fresh, choosing to move up instead of spiraling down, and doing so turns your self-esteem around too.

永远不算晚：任何时刻你都能重新开始，选择向上而不是往下滑，这样做也会扭转你的自尊。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'If I have done bad things, I am just a bad person.' The better reading in this activity is that no one is 'just bad', and a person's actions can change. If you feel very down about yourself, talking with a trusted adult or counselor is a good first step.

「做过坏事，我就是个坏人。」这个练习给的更好的看法是：没有人「就是坏」，一个人的行为是可以改变的。如果你对自己非常低落，找信得过的大人或辅导老师谈谈是个好的起点。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Believing that people can change has shown some practical effects: in a nationally representative US study, a short online growth-mindset lesson raised grades among lower-achieving secondary students and increased enrolment in advanced math, especially in schools where peers supported the idea (Yeager et al., 2019). That study is about school achievement, not about behavior problems or self-esteem, so it only loosely supports this activity.

相信人可以改变，确实有些实际效果：在一项美国全国代表性样本的研究里，一堂短短的线上「成长型思维」课，提升了成绩较低的中学生的成绩，也增加了进阶数学课的选修，尤其在同伴也认同这种想法的学校（Yeager 等，2019）。该研究关注的是学业，不是行为问题或自尊，所以对这个练习只是间接支持。

  • Yeager DS, Hanselman P, Walton GM, et al. A national experiment reveals where a growth mindset improves achievement. Nature 2019;573(7774):364-369. doi:10.1038/s41586-019-1466-y link链接
