Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Paying attention to what is good in your life and in yourself lifts mood and supports healthy self-esteem.留意生活里和自己身上的好，能提升心情，也支持健康的自尊。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

A teen who complained about everything is shown that a change of focus, not of life, lifts the mood. The pages ask you to circle what you are thankful for, finish sentences, list good things about yourself in three areas, then keep a nightly list of five for seven days.一位什么都抱怨的少年被告知，改变的是关注点而不是生活本身，心情就能好起来。后面请你圈出自己感谢的事、完成句子、从三个方面写出自己的好，再连续七晚每晚列出五件。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

A teen who complained about everything is shown that a change of focus, not of life, lifts the mood. The pages ask you to circle what you are thankful for, finish sentences, list good things about yourself in three areas, then keep a nightly list of five for seven days.

一位什么都抱怨的少年被告知，改变的是关注点而不是生活本身，心情就能好起来。后面请你圈出自己感谢的事、完成句子、从三个方面写出自己的好，再连续七晚每晚列出五件。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

Wrong reading: gratitude means you cannot be upset or must feel lucky compared with others. Better reading: it is a way of also seeing what is good, without denying what hurts. If things feel hard, a trusted adult or counselor can help.

常见误读：感恩就是不能难过，或者必须觉得自己比别人幸运。更好的理解：它是一种也看到好的方式，并不否认痛处。如果日子很难，可以找信任的大人或辅导员。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

In a classroom experiment with 221 early adolescents, students who counted blessings reported more gratitude, optimism and life satisfaction and less negative feeling, with a link to school satisfaction still visible three weeks later (Froh, Sefick & Emmons, 2008). It was one study with self-reported outcomes, so treat it as encouraging rather than conclusive.

在一项有 221 名低龄青少年参加的课堂实验中，数算恩典的学生自评的感恩、乐观、生活满意度更高，负面情绪更少，三周后与学校满意度的联系仍然可见（Froh、Sefick 与 Emmons，2008）。这是一项靠自评结果的研究，只能看作令人鼓舞，不能算定论。

  • Froh JJ, Sefick WJ, Emmons RA. Counting blessings in early adolescents: an experimental study of gratitude and subjective well-being. J Sch Psychol 2008;46(2):213-233. doi:10.1016/j.jsp.2007.03.005 link链接
