Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Pointing at others for how your life is going hands your power away; owning your thoughts, feelings and actions gets it back.把生活不如意都推给别人，等于把力量交出去；认领自己的想法、感受和行动，力量就回来了。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Blaming people or circumstances gives your power away and breeds negativity. Owning your thoughts, feelings and actions returns it and helps you grow into your authentic self.责怪别人或环境，会把力量交出去，只剩消极；认领自己的想法、感受和行动，力量回到手里，你才能长成真实的自己。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Blaming people or circumstances gives your power away and breeds negativity. Owning your thoughts, feelings and actions returns it and helps you grow into your authentic self.

责怪别人或环境，会把力量交出去，只剩消极；认领自己的想法、感受和行动，力量回到手里，你才能长成真实的自己。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Taking responsibility means it was all my fault.' Not quite: other people's choices can really hurt, and feeling upset is fair. Responsibility is only about your own next move and your own feelings. If you carry something heavy, a trusted adult or counselor can help.

「负责就是说一切都是我的错。」不是这样：别人的选择确实可能伤到你，难过也很正常。负责只是说你自己的下一步和你自己的感受由你来管。如果压着很重的事，可以找信得过的大人或辅导老师聊聊。
