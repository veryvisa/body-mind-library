Part 6 · Conviction, responsibility, goals and asking for help第 6 部分 · 信念、责任、目标与求助

Activities 39–46练习 39–46 · The book itself has no parts; this six-part grouping is ours, to give a map of the 46 activities.原书不分部分；这六部分是我们为了给 46 个练习画一张地图而做的分组。

39 · The Power of Conviction信念的力量
Conviction is a deep belief in yourself and your goals that helps you keep going through challenges without giving up on who you are.信念是对自己和目标的深深相信，它帮你在困难里继续走下去，不放弃自己。
40 · The Power of Responsibility负起责任的力量
Pointing at others for how your life is going hands your power away; owning your thoughts, feelings and actions gets it back.把生活不如意都推给别人，等于把力量交出去；认领自己的想法、感受和行动，力量就回来了。
41 · The Power of Positive Decisions正向决定的力量
A positive decision is the one most likely to bring a good outcome, even when it is not the easiest, and it also protects how you feel about yourself.正向决定就是最可能带来好结果的那个选择，哪怕它不是最省力的；它也守护着你对自己的感觉。
42 · The Power of Facing Challenges Head-On直面挑战的力量
Ignoring or avoiding a problem usually stacks a second problem on top of the first; facing it is what actually helps and builds self-esteem.无视或躲开问题，往往是在第一个问题上再叠一个；面对它才真的有用，也能养出自尊。
43 · Setting Realistic Goals设定现实的目标
Big goals work better as long-term goals, climbed by realistic short-term steps, and each step you reach feeds healthier self-esteem.大目标更适合当长期目标，用现实的短期小步一级级爬；每达成一步，自尊也跟着健康一点。
44 · Problem-Solving Skills解决问题的本领
A six-step plan, calm first, then define, brainstorm, choose, test and adjust, makes problems feel manageable and builds confidence.六步计划：先平静，再界定、头脑风暴、挑选、试试看、评估调整，让问题变得可处理，也建立信心。
45 · It's Never Too Late to Turn Things Around改变永远不晚
No matter what has happened, you can start fresh at any moment, and each step of turning things around turns your self-esteem around too.不论发生过什么，你随时都可以重新开始；扭转局面的每一步，也在扭转你的自尊。
46 · Smart People Ask for Help聪明人会求助
Asking for help is a sign of wisdom, not weakness: the right help gets you better results, and you can build a list of people you trust.求助是智慧，不是软弱：合适的帮助让结果更好，你也可以列一张自己信得过的人的名单。
