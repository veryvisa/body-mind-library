Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Every feeling is allowed; what protects you is naming it, accepting it, letting it out safely and then looking after yourself.每种感受都可以有；真正保护你的，是说出它的名字、接纳它、安全地放它出来，再好好照顾自己。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Feelings are not wrong. Hiding them lets them swell, so the workbook offers a simple four-step routine and a long list of safe ways to release a feeling, then asks you to rate which ones work for you.情绪没有对错。藏起来它们反而会膨胀，所以书里给了一套简单的四步做法和一长串安全的释放方式，再请你给每种方式打分，看哪种适合自己。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Feelings are not wrong. Hiding them lets them swell, so the workbook offers a simple four-step routine and a long list of safe ways to release a feeling, then asks you to rate which ones work for you.

情绪没有对错。藏起来它们反而会膨胀，所以书里给了一套简单的四步做法和一长串安全的释放方式，再请你给每种方式打分，看哪种适合自己。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

Wrong reading: managing feelings means making them go away fast. Better reading: you are learning to let them be there and move through, without hurting anyone. If a feeling stays very heavy or frightening, a trusted adult or counselor can help.

常见误读：管理情绪就是让它赶紧消失。更好的理解：是让它存在、也能走过去，而且不伤人。如果某种感受一直很沉重或让你害怕，可以找信任的大人或辅导员帮忙。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

A large review of studies found that how people habitually handle emotions matters: rumination showed the strongest link to anxiety and depression symptoms, and avoidance and suppression also showed notable links, while acceptance and reappraisal were linked more weakly (Aldao, Nolen-Hoeksema & Schweizer, 2010). It describes associations, not proof of cause, but it fits the book's point that pushing feelings away is not the helpful route.

一项综述汇总了大量研究，发现人们惯用的情绪处理方式很重要：反复琢磨与焦虑、抑郁症状的关联最强，回避和压抑也有明显关联，而接纳与重新看待的关联较弱（Aldao、Nolen-Hoeksema 与 Schweizer，2010）。这只是相关而非因果证明，但与书里「把情绪推开并不有效」的说法相呼应。

  • Aldao A, Nolen-Hoeksema S, Schweizer S. Emotion-regulation strategies across psychopathology: A meta-analytic review. Clin Psychol Rev 2010;30(2):217-237. doi:10.1016/j.cpr.2009.11.004 link链接
