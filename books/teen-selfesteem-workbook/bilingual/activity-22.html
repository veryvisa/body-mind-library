Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Shifting attention from how your body looks to what it does for you, and to the many qualities that make people valuable, can lift your self-esteem.把注意力从身体「看起来怎样」转到它「为你做了什么」，再看到让人有价值的很多品质，能提升你的自尊。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Self-esteem grows when you appreciate what your body does, accept that bodies differ, and notice that people are valued for much more than looks.当你欣赏身体能做的事、接受身体各不相同，并注意到人被看重远不止因为外貌时，自尊就会成长。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Self-esteem grows when you appreciate what your body does, accept that bodies differ, and notice that people are valued for much more than looks.

当你欣赏身体能做的事、接受身体各不相同，并注意到人被看重远不止因为外貌时，自尊就会成长。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Loving my body means I have to feel great about it all the time.' Appreciating what your body does is a gentler goal than liking every part. It is fine to have days when this is hard. If it stays hard, a trusted adult or counselor can help.

「爱我的身体，就得时时刻刻觉得它很棒。」欣赏身体能做的事，是比「喜欢每一处」更温和的目标。这有时做不到也没关系。如果一直很难，可以找信任的大人或辅导员帮忙。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

The writing exercise the book echoes, in which people write about what their body can do, has been tested as Expand Your Horizon. In a 2025 trial of 58 adolescent girls with an eating disorder, one session raised positive body image compared with a control task but did not lower negative body image, and the researchers call for longer-term tests (Glashouwer et al., 2025). It is one small study in a specific group, so treat it as a promising lead, not a proven fix.

书里这类写作练习，也就是写下身体能做什么，在研究中被称为 Expand Your Horizon。2025 年一项针对 58 名有进食障碍的青春期女孩的试验中，一次练习相比对照任务提高了积极的身体形象，但没有降低消极的身体形象，研究者也呼吁做更长期的检验（Glashouwer 等，2025）。这只是针对特定人群的一项小研究，应当视为有希望的线索，而不是已被证明的办法。

  • Glashouwer KA, Weiland L, Alleva JM, et al. Expand Your Horizon: testing a brief writing intervention focused on body functionality among adolescent girls with an eating disorder. Int J Eat Disord 2025. link链接
