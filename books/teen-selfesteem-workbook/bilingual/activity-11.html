Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Society sends messages about how to look, act, and belong; you can notice them and choose which to follow, as long as your choices stay safe.社会会传递外貌、言行和归属的信号；你可以先留意，再决定跟随哪些，只要选择是安全的。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Part of growing up is finding your own ideas and values. Healthy self-esteem includes staying true to them when they are safe, whether or not society agrees.成长的一部分是找到自己的想法和价值。健康的自尊包括：只要安全，就算和社会不一致也守住它们。

What is a social value?什么是社会价值观？

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Part of growing up is finding your own ideas and values. Healthy self-esteem includes staying true to them when they are safe, whether or not society agrees.

成长的一部分是找到自己的想法和价值。健康的自尊包括：只要安全，就算和社会不一致也守住它们。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

"Being myself means going against everything society says." Being yourself means choosing consciously. Sometimes you agree with society, and that can be authentic too. If looks or others' teasing weigh on you, if any of this feels heavy, it is okay to talk with a trusted adult or school counselor.

「做自己就是跟社会对着干。」做自己是有意识地选择。有时你和社会的看法一致，那也可以是真实的。如果外貌或别人的取笑让你很沉重，如果读到这里觉得沉重，可以找信得过的大人或学校辅导老师聊聊。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

A meta-analysis of 77 studies found that exposure to media showing a very thin ideal body was linked to more body dissatisfaction, more internalizing of that ideal, and more disordered eating beliefs in women, with small to moderate effects (Grabe et al., 2008). It studied women rather than teens specifically, and many studies were correlational, so it supports noticing media messages but not a strong cause.

一项汇总 77 项研究的元分析发现，接触展示极瘦理想身材的媒体，与女性更不满意自己的身体、更认同这种理想、更多不健康的饮食观念有关，效应为小到中等（Grabe 等，2008）。研究对象是女性而非专门的青少年，许多研究也是相关性的，所以它支持「留意媒体信息」，但不能证明强因果。

  • Grabe S, Ward LM, Hyde JS. The role of the media in body image concerns among women: a meta-analysis of experimental and correlational studies. Psychol Bull 2008;134(3). link链接
