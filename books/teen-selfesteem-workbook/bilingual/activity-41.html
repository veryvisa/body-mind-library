Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

A positive decision is the one most likely to bring a good outcome, even when it is not the easiest, and it also protects how you feel about yourself.正向决定就是最可能带来好结果的那个选择，哪怕它不是最省力的；它也守护着你对自己的感觉。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Choices have consequences. Picking the option that is most likely to turn out well, even if harder now, raises your odds of a good result and a better opinion of yourself.选择都有后果。挑最可能有好结果的那个，即使现在更难，也更有机会得到好结果，也更能喜欢自己。

What the book says书里怎么说

Choices have consequences. Picking the option that is most likely to turn out well, even if harder now, raises your odds of a good result and a better opinion of yourself.

选择都有后果。挑最可能有好结果的那个，即使现在更难，也更有机会得到好结果，也更能喜欢自己。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'A positive decision is the one that feels good right now.' Often the opposite: the quick, easy or cool-looking choice can bring the bigger problem later. The better reading is to compare the outcomes, not the moods.

「正向决定就是现在感觉好的那个。」常常相反：图快、图省事、图显得酷的选择，后面可能带来更大的麻烦。更好的看法是比较结果，而不是比较此刻的心情。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

A long study that followed about 1,000 children to age 32 found that childhood self-control, a close cousin of weighing outcomes before acting, predicted adult health, money and criminal-record outcomes, even with intelligence and social class taken into account (Moffitt et al., 2011). That is an association in one cohort, not proof that practicing decisions will change a life, and it is not specifically about teens' self-esteem.

一项追踪约 1000 名儿童到 32 岁的长期研究发现，童年的自控力（与「行动前先掂量结果」很接近）能预测成年后的健康、财务和违法记录，即使把智力和家庭社会阶层的影响拿掉也是如此（Moffitt 等，2011）。这只是一个队列里的关联，不能证明练习做决定就能改变人生，也不是专门研究青少年自尊。

  • Moffitt TE, Arseneault L, Belsky D, et al. A gradient of childhood self-control predicts health, wealth, and public safety. Proc Natl Acad Sci USA 2011;108(7):2693-2698. doi:10.1073/pnas.1010076108 link链接

Next step下一步

Pages 193-197: for five situations, write one positive and one negative decision with their outcomes; for a day, watch decisions (yours or others') and mark them plus or minus with a reason; describe one positive and one negative decision you once made and what each did to your self-esteem; imagine a world where everyone chose only positive decisions. Some situations touch cheating, pressure and risky choices; if one feels heavy, a trusted adult can help.原书第 193-197 页：为五个情境各写一个正向决定、一个负向决定及其结果；用一天时间观察自己或别人的决定，标出加号或减号并写理由；描述你做过的一个正向决定和一个负向决定，以及各自对自尊的影响；想象一个人人只做正向决定的世界。其中有些情境涉及作弊、压力和冒险，如果觉得沉重，可以找信得过的大人聊聊。
