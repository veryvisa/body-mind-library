Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Nobody else has your exact mix of gifts, so exploring your own purpose gives you something steady to hold on to when others pull you away.没有人拥有和你一模一样的天赋组合，探索自己的人生目的，能在别人拉你离开时给你一个稳住的支点。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Having a purpose of your own gives you confidence in your value. You do not have to know it today; exploring and staying true to yourself is how you get closer.有自己的人生目的，会让你对自己的价值更有信心。你不必今天就知道答案；不断探索、忠于自己，就是靠近它的方式。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Having a purpose of your own gives you confidence in your value. You do not have to know it today; exploring and staying true to yourself is how you get closer.

有自己的人生目的，会让你对自己的价值更有信心。你不必今天就知道答案；不断探索、忠于自己，就是靠近它的方式。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'I must figure out my one big purpose right now, or I am behind.' The activity says the opposite: not knowing is okay, and a purpose can start small and change as you learn.

「我必须现在就找到唯一的大目标，否则就落后了。」这个练习说的恰恰相反：不知道也没关系，目的可以从小处开始，随着了解变多而改变。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

In one set of three studies, young people who felt a stronger commitment to a purpose reported more positive feelings, hope and happiness, and purpose appeared to help explain why a clear sense of identity went with better well-being (Burrow & Hill, 2011). These are mostly self-reports at a point in time, so they show a link and do not prove purpose causes well-being.

在三项研究里，对某个目的承诺更强的年轻人，报告了更多正面情绪、希望和快乐；目的似乎也能部分解释，为什么身份认同清晰的人幸福感更高（Burrow 和 Hill，2011）。这些主要是自我报告，只能说明有关联，不能证明目的会带来幸福。

  • Burrow AL, Hill PL. Purpose as a form of identity capital for positive youth adjustment. Dev Psychol 2011;47(4):1196-1206. link链接
