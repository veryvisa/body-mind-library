Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Your authentic self is who you are before you bend to other people's expectations, and checking your choices against it helps you find and trust yourself.真实的自己，是你在向别人期待弯腰之前的那个你；拿它来对照自己的选择，能帮你找到并信任自己。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Your authentic self is who you are apart from outside pressure. The healthier your self-esteem, the better you can know, trust, and express it, and living from it feels right.真实的自己是除去外界压力后的你。自尊越健康，越能认识、信任并表达它，按它生活会觉得对劲。

What the book says书里怎么说

Your authentic self is who you are apart from outside pressure. The healthier your self-esteem, the better you can know, trust, and express it, and living from it feels right.

真实的自己是除去外界压力后的你。自尊越健康，越能认识、信任并表达它，按它生活会觉得对劲。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

A common wrong reading: 'If I am authentic, I should always do whatever I feel.' A better one: authenticity is about knowing your values and wishes, then choosing, which can still include patience, rules, and kindness. If acting on your wishes feels unsafe at home or school, a trusted adult can help you find a way.

常见的误读：「做真实的自己，就是想干嘛干嘛。」更好的理解：真实是认清自己的价值与愿望，然后做选择，这里面仍可以有耐心、规则和善意。如果按自己的意愿行动在家或在学校会觉得不安全，可以找信任的成年人一起想办法。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Self-determination research finds that people do better in motivation and well-being when their actions come from their own values and choices, supported by competence and connection, than when they are driven only by outside pressure (Ryan & Deci, 2000). That is close to what the book calls acting from the authentic self, though the book uses its own wording and this study did not test the checklist.

自我决定理论的研究发现，行动出自自己的价值与选择、并有能力感和联结感支持时，人的动机与幸福感，比只受外界压力推动时更好（Ryan 与 Deci，2000）。这与书中说的「按真实自我行动」很接近，不过书用了自己的说法，这项研究也没有检验书里的清单。

  • Ryan RM, Deci EL. Self-determination theory and the facilitation of intrinsic motivation, social development, and well-being. Am Psychol 2000;55(1):68-78. doi:10.1037/0003-066X.55.1.68 link链接
