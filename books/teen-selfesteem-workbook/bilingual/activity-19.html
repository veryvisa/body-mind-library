Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Everyone has both strengths and weaknesses, and neither changes your worth, so celebrate the first and, where it matters, work on the second.每个人都既有优点也有弱点，两者都不改变你的价值：为优点高兴，在需要时去改进弱点。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Healthy self-esteem means recognizing your strengths and accepting your weaknesses without letting either decide your worth. Work on weaknesses only where it serves your goals.健康的自尊，是认得自己的优点、接纳自己的弱点，且不让任何一方决定你的价值；只有在对你的目标有用时才去改进弱点。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Healthy self-esteem means recognizing your strengths and accepting your weaknesses without letting either decide your worth. Work on weaknesses only where it serves your goals.

健康的自尊，是认得自己的优点、接纳自己的弱点，且不让任何一方决定你的价值；只有在对你的目标有用时才去改进弱点。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Celebrating strengths is bragging, and having weaknesses means I am not good enough.' Naming what you do well is honest, not boastful, and weaknesses are ordinary, not proof of low worth.

「庆祝优点就是自夸，有弱点就是不够好。」说出自己擅长什么是诚实，不是吹嘘；弱点很平常，不能证明你价值低。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

A 2026 meta-analysis of 114 randomized trials with about 20,000 people found that exercises aimed at building strengths such as kindness and gratitude were linked to better well-being, and that the trained strengths helped explain the effect for most strengths studied (Cebolla et al., 2026). The studies mix many exercises and ages, so this supports the general idea rather than a specific recipe for teens.

2026 年一项涵盖 114 项随机试验、约 2 万人的元分析发现，培养善意、感恩等品格优点的练习，与更好的幸福感相关，而且对大多数被研究的优点来说，被训练的优点能部分解释这种效果（Cebolla 等，2026）。这些研究的练习和年龄各异，所以它支持的是大方向，而不是给青少年的具体做法。

  • Cebolla A, Fernandez-Castilla B, Lecuona O, et al. Efficacy of cultivating human strengths and virtues on well-being: a one-stage meta-analytic structural equation modeling approach. Clin Psychol Rev 2026. link链接
