Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Big goals work better as long-term goals, climbed by realistic short-term steps, and each step you reach feeds healthier self-esteem.大目标更适合当长期目标，用现实的短期小步一级级爬；每达成一步，自尊也跟着健康一点。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Goals set too high drain you and usually end in quitting. Treat the big one as a long-term goal and build realistic short-term steps toward it, and you collect successes that strengthen self-esteem.目标定得太高，会把人耗光，往往以放弃收场。把大目标当长期目标，再为它搭建现实的短期台阶，你就会攒下一个个成功，自尊随之增强。

What the book says书里怎么说

Goals set too high drain you and usually end in quitting. Treat the big one as a long-term goal and build realistic short-term steps toward it, and you collect successes that strengthen self-esteem.

目标定得太高，会把人耗光，往往以放弃收场。把大目标当长期目标，再为它搭建现实的短期台阶，你就会攒下一个个成功，自尊随之增强。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Realistic means settling for less.' Not in this activity: the dream stays on top of the ladder. Realistic only means the steps are the right size for now. If missing goals leaves you feeling low a lot, a trusted adult or counselor can help.

「现实就是降低标准。」在这个练习里不是：梦想仍在梯子顶端，现实只是说台阶的大小要适合现在的你。如果经常因为没达成目标而情绪低落，可以找信得过的大人或辅导老师聊聊。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Locke and Latham's review of 35 years of goal-setting research summarizes the core findings, how goals work, and the limits of the theory (Locke & Latham, 2002). I only read the abstract, which does not list specific effects, so I make no claim here about how large they are; the link to teen self-esteem is the book's own idea and I did not verify it.

Locke 与 Latham 回顾了 35 年的目标设定研究，总结了核心发现、目标起作用的机制以及理论的局限（Locke 与 Latham，2002）。我只读了摘要，摘要没写具体效应，所以这里不断言效应多大；「目标与青少年自尊」的联系是书自己的主张，我没有核实。

  • Locke EA, Latham GP. Building a practically useful theory of goal setting and task motivation: A 35-year odyssey. Am Psychol 2002;57(9):705-717. doi:10.1037/0003-066X.57.9.705 link链接
