Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

We pick up messages about our worth from family, media and culture, often indirectly, and once we spot them we can choose to drop the ones that shrink us.我们从家庭、媒体、文化那里接收关于自己价值的信息，常常是间接的；一旦看出来，就可以选择放下让我们变小的那些。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Messages from family and society shape how we feel. Becoming aware of them lets us choose which to release instead of carrying them unexamined.家庭和社会的信息塑造我们的感受。意识到它们，就能选择放下哪些，而不是不加检查地一直背着。

What is a devaluing message?什么是贬低性信息？
Answer in your head, then tap to flip先在心里答，再点一下翻面
A message, spoken or unspoken, that leaves you feeling less worthy or less good enough.一种信息，不论说出口还是没说出口，让你觉得自己不够好、不够有价值。

Can a message hurt even if the sender meant well?说的人是好意，信息也会伤人吗？
Answer in your head, then tap to flip先在心里答，再点一下翻面
Yes. A parent pushing for 'even better' meant encouragement, but the teen heard 'never enough'. What lands matters, even when intent was kind.会。家长说「下次再好一点」是想鼓励，孩子却听成「永远不够」。即使出发点是善意，听进去的是什么也很重要。

What are indirect messages?什么是间接的信息？
Answer in your head, then tap to flip先在心里答，再点一下翻面
Ideas absorbed from the world around you without anyone saying them to you, such as a culture valuing athletes while you are not athletic.没人对你说过、却从周围环境里吸进来的看法，比如文化看重运动员，而你并不擅长运动。

How does the book suggest letting a message go?书建议怎样放下一条信息？

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Messages from family and society shape how we feel. Becoming aware of them lets us choose which to release instead of carrying them unexamined.

家庭和社会的信息塑造我们的感受。意识到它们，就能选择放下哪些，而不是不加检查地一直背着。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

A common wrong reading: 'If I release a message, I must be saying my family is bad.' A better one: you can love your family and still decide a particular sentence is not true about you. Some family stories are heavy, and a counselor or trusted adult is a good partner for those.

常见的误读：「放下一条信息，就是说我家人不好。」更好的理解：你可以爱家人，同时认定某句话并不适用于你。有些家庭经历很沉重，辅导员或信任的成年人是很好的陪伴。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

A meta-analysis of 77 studies on media and body image found that exposure to thin-ideal images is linked to body dissatisfaction, to taking on that ideal, and to eating-related concerns, with small to moderate effects (Grabe et al., 2008). Those studies looked at women, so it only partly speaks to teen boys or to non-body messages, but it shows that messages from media are not harmless background.

一项汇总 77 项研究的元分析发现，接触「瘦才好」的形象，与对身体的不满、认同这种标准、以及饮食方面的困扰都有关联，效应小到中等（Grabe 等，2008）。这些研究的对象是女性，所以对男生或与身体无关的信息只能部分参考；但它说明，媒体传来的信息并不只是无害的背景。

  • Grabe S, Ward LM, Hyde JS. The role of the media in body image concerns among women: a meta-analysis of experimental and correlational studies. Psychol Bull 2008;134(3):460-476. doi:10.1037/0033-2909.134.3.460 link链接
