Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Being liked by every single person is an impossible goal, and your worth does not rise or fall with who likes you.被每一个人喜欢是不可能完成的目标，你的价值不会因为谁喜欢你而涨跌。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Seeking universal approval is a treadmill that exhausts you and hides who you are. Your intrinsic value is not decided by anyone's liking, so you can let go of the goal and appreciate the friends you have.追求人人认可像踩跑步机，会耗尽你、也遮住真实的你。你的内在价值不由任何人的喜欢来决定，所以可以放下这个目标，珍惜身边的朋友。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Seeking universal approval is a treadmill that exhausts you and hides who you are. Your intrinsic value is not decided by anyone's liking, so you can let go of the goal and appreciate the friends you have.

追求人人认可像踩跑步机，会耗尽你、也遮住真实的你。你的内在价值不由任何人的喜欢来决定，所以可以放下这个目标，珍惜身边的朋友。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Not caring who likes me means I should stop being kind.' No: the book keeps kindness and drops the impossible demand. If feeling disliked hurts a lot or for a long time, a trusted adult or counselor is a good person to talk to.

「不在乎谁喜欢我，就是不用对人友善了。」不是：书里保留友善，只是放下那个不可能的要求。如果被人不喜欢让你很痛、很久，信任的大人或辅导老师是很好的倾诉对象。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Psychologists Crocker and Wolfe argued that self-esteem is less a fixed level than something that rises and falls with the areas where a person stakes their worth; when approval is one of those areas, self-esteem becomes fragile (Crocker & Wolfe, 2001). This is a theoretical model rather than a test of this worksheet, but it matches the book's warning about tying worth to being liked.

心理学家 Crocker 与 Wolfe 提出：自尊与其说是一个固定水平，不如说会随一个人把价值押在哪些方面而起落；当「被认可」成了其中之一，自尊就会变得脆弱（Crocker 与 Wolfe，2001）。这是一个理论模型，并没有测试这张练习表，但与书里「别把价值系在被喜欢上」的提醒相符。

  • Crocker J, Wolfe CT. Contingencies of self-worth. Psychol Rev 2001;108(3):593-623. link链接
