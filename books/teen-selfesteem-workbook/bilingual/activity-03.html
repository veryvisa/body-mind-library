Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Your worth was there from the start, it is the same as everyone's, and mistakes or hurtful words cannot remove it; the voices saying otherwise are messages, not facts.你的价值从一开始就在，和每个人一样，错误和伤人的话都拿不走它；说你没价值的声音只是信息，不是事实。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Every person is born with equal, lasting value. Messages that say otherwise can be examined and let go, and remembering this is a foundation for healthy self-esteem.每个人生来就有同等而恒久的价值。说你没价值的信息可以拿出来检查、放下，记住这一点是健康自尊的基础。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Every person is born with equal, lasting value. Messages that say otherwise can be examined and let go, and remembering this is a foundation for healthy self-esteem.

每个人生来就有同等而恒久的价值。说你没价值的信息可以拿出来检查、放下，记住这一点是健康自尊的基础。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

A common wrong reading: 'Worth means I should feel special all the time.' A better one: worth does not need a feeling. On days you feel low, it is still there, like the ground under a building.

常见的误读：「有价值就该时时觉得自己特别。」更好的理解：价值不依赖感觉。心情低落的日子它仍在，像房子下面的地基。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Psychologists distinguish self-worth that rests on proving yourself in certain areas from a steadier kind. When worth is staked on external wins, people tend to react to threats in ways that cost them in learning, relationships, and health over time (Crocker & Park, 2004). That fits the idea of anchoring worth in something that does not need to be re-earned each day.

心理学家区分「靠在某些领域证明自己」的价值感和更稳的那种。当价值押在外部的胜负上，人面对威胁时的反应往往会拖累学习、人际和健康（Crocker 与 Park，2004）。这和「把价值锚在不必每天重新挣的东西上」的想法相合。

  • Crocker J, Park LE. The costly pursuit of self-esteem. Psychol Bull 2004;130(3):392-414. link链接
