Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Conviction is a deep belief in yourself and your goals that helps you keep going through challenges without giving up on who you are.信念是对自己和目标的深深相信，它帮你在困难里继续走下去，不放弃自己。

What the book says书里怎么说

A girl facing health problems and family strain learns from her grandmother that challenges are normal and giving up is not the answer. The pages invite you to picture yourself strong, list achievements and mark the hardest, draw an obstacle course for one past success, and another for a current goal.

一位面对健康问题和家庭压力的女孩，从外婆那里学到挑战是常态、放弃不是答案。后面请你想象一个有力量的自己，列出成就并标出最难的，为一次过去的成功画一条障碍赛道，再为一个眼前的目标画另一条。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

Wrong reading: conviction means never doubting or never resting. Better reading: you can doubt, rest and ask for help, and still keep believing you can get through. If your challenges feel too big to carry, a trusted adult or counselor can help.

常见误读：有信念就是从不怀疑、从不休息。更好的理解：你可以怀疑、可以休息、可以求助，同时仍然相信自己能走过去。如果你的挑战大到扛不动，可以找信任的大人或辅导员。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Research on grit, the idea of long-term perseverance, is a useful caution. A meta-analysis of 88 samples found grit only moderately related to performance and very strongly related to conscientiousness, with the perseverance part doing better than the consistency of interest part, and suggested that training grit may have weak effects (Credé, Tynan & Harms, 2017). Belief and effort matter, but they are not magic.

关于坚毅，也就是长期坚持的研究提供了一个有用的提醒。一项涵盖 88 个样本的元分析发现，坚毅与表现只有中等程度的关联，却与尽责性高度相关，其中「努力坚持」部分比「兴趣一致」部分更有用，并提示训练坚毅可能效果有限（Credé、Tynan 与 Harms，2017）。信念和努力很重要，但不是魔法。

  • Credé M, Tynan MC, Harms PD. Much ado about grit: A meta-analytic synthesis of the grit literature. J Pers Soc Psychol 2017;113(3):492-511. doi:10.1037/pspp0000102 link链接
