Part 3 · Discovering yourself第 3 部分 · 认识自己

Activities 12–20练习 12–20 · The book itself has no parts; this six-part grouping is ours, to give a map of the 46 activities.原书不分部分；这六部分是我们为了给 46 个练习画一张地图而做的分组。

12 · It's Normal to Not Know不知道很正常
Teen years are for trying ideas out, so being unsure about who you are and what comes next is normal, and you can unfold one step at a time.青少年时期本来就是试各种想法的阶段，对「我是谁、接下来做什么」没把握很正常，可以一步一步慢慢展开。
13 · Discovering Your Likes and Dislikes发现你的喜好
Your likes and dislikes, even small ones, add up to a one-of-a-kind picture of you, and noticing them builds a stronger sense of self.你的喜欢和不喜欢，哪怕很小，合起来就是一幅独一无二的你；留意它们会让自我感更稳。
14 · Discovering Your Dreams发现你的梦想
Daydreams, night dreams, and wishes for the future hint at what you value and long for, even when they are hazy or conflicted.白日梦、夜里的梦和对未来的愿望，都在暗示你看重和渴望什么，哪怕它们模糊甚至互相矛盾。
15 · Discovering Your Beliefs发现你的信念
Many beliefs are handed to you; looking at each one lets you keep what is truly yours and rewrite what is not.很多信念是别人递给你的；逐个看一看，你可以留下真正属于自己的，改写不属于你的。
16 · Discovering Your Passions发现你的热情
A passion can be an idea, a thing, an activity, or a person, and noticing what moves you deeply points toward your authentic self.热情可以是一个理念、一样东西、一项活动或一个人；留意什么深深打动你，会指向真实的自己。
17 · You in the Universe放眼宇宙里的你
When a decision is stressful, step back from what others will think and ask what kind of person you want to be, then let that answer steer what you do.做决定感到压力时，先退后一步，不去猜别人会怎么想，而是问自己想成为怎样的人，再让这个答案带着你行动。
18 · Why You Are Here你为什么在这里
Nobody else has your exact mix of gifts, so exploring your own purpose gives you something steady to hold on to when others pull you away.没有人拥有和你一模一样的天赋组合，探索自己的人生目的，能在别人拉你离开时给你一个稳住的支点。
19 · Celebrate Your Strengths, Work with Your Weaknesses庆祝优点，和弱点相处
Everyone has both strengths and weaknesses, and neither changes your worth, so celebrate the first and, where it matters, work on the second.每个人都既有优点也有弱点，两者都不改变你的价值：为优点高兴，在需要时去改进弱点。
20 · The Power of Intuition直觉的力量
Intuition is a deep felt sense you notice in body and mind; learning to listen to it, while checking anything risky, helps you stay true to yourself.直觉是一种在身体和头脑里都能感到的深层感觉；学会倾听它，同时对有风险的念头多核对，能帮你忠于自己。
