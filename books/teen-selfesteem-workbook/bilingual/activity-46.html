Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Asking for help is a sign of wisdom, not weakness: the right help gets you better results, and you can build a list of people you trust.求助是智慧，不是软弱：合适的帮助让结果更好，你也可以列一张自己信得过的人的名单。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Asking for help when appropriate leads to better outcomes and healthier self-esteem. Learn problem-solving for manageable tasks, but also know when help is the best solution, especially if safety is at stake.在合适的时候求助，会带来更好的结果和更健康的自尊。可管的事学着自己解决，但也要知道什么时候求助才是最好的办法，尤其是涉及安全时。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Asking for help when appropriate leads to better outcomes and healthier self-esteem. Learn problem-solving for manageable tasks, but also know when help is the best solution, especially if safety is at stake.

在合适的时候求助，会带来更好的结果和更健康的自尊。可管的事学着自己解决，但也要知道什么时候求助才是最好的办法，尤其是涉及安全时。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Asking for help means I failed.' The better reading: it is a skill, and a smart one. If you are in danger or feel very low, please reach out to a trusted adult, school counselor or a local crisis line right away.

「求助就说明我失败了。」更好的看法：这是一项技能，而且很聪明。如果你处于危险中或情绪非常低落，请马上联系信得过的大人、学校辅导老师或当地的危机热线。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

A systematic review of 22 studies found that young people often do not seek help for mental health problems; stigma, embarrassment and difficulty recognizing symptoms were among the main perceived barriers (Gulliver, Griffiths & Christensen, 2010). I read only the abstract's first part, so I do not claim more than that: it supports the idea that worries about looking weak are common, not that this activity changes help-seeking.

一项涵盖 22 项研究的系统综述发现，年轻人对心理健康问题往往不去求助；污名、难为情以及不易识别症状，是他们认为的主要障碍（Gulliver、Griffiths 与 Christensen，2010）。我只读到摘要的前半部分，所以不多说：它支持的是「担心显得软弱很普遍」，而不是这个练习能改变求助行为。

  • Gulliver A, Griffiths KM, Christensen H. Perceived barriers and facilitators to mental health help-seeking in young people: a systematic review. BMC Psychiatry 2010;10:113. doi:10.1186/1471-244X-10-113 link链接
