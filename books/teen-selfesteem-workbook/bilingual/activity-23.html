Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

People often judge to feel briefly better about themselves, and your worth does not depend on their judgment, so you can stop judging and stop taking it personally.人们常常评判别人，是为了暂时对自己感觉好一点；你的价值不取决于他们的评判，所以你可以不去评判别人，也不必把评判当成针对自己。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Judging others gives only a short, false lift, and being judged does not change your worth. Healthy self-esteem lets you let go of both.评判别人只能带来短暂而虚假的提升，被评判也不会改变你的价值。健康的自尊让你对两者都能放下。

What the book says书里怎么说

Judging others gives only a short, false lift, and being judged does not change your worth. Healthy self-esteem lets you let go of both.

评判别人只能带来短暂而虚假的提升，被评判也不会改变你的价值。健康的自尊让你对两者都能放下。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'If someone criticizes me, they must be right.' A criticism says something about the speaker's view and mood, and it can be worth listening to only when it is fair and useful. Words that keep hurting a lot are worth sharing with a trusted adult.

「有人批评我，他一定是对的。」批评首先反映的是说话者的看法和心情，只有公平、有用时才值得听。如果某些话一直让你很难受，值得告诉信任的大人。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

A review of self-affirmation research describes how a threat to one's sense of self can set off self-protective defenses, and how reflecting on core values can lessen them (Cohen & Sherman, 2014). A meta-analysis of 88 experiments found that being rejected lowers mood and self-esteem, with a moderate to large effect on self-esteem (Gerber & Wheeler, 2009). Together they fit the idea that judgments sting and that a steadier sense of self makes them easier to bear. Neither paper tested the book's exercise directly.

一篇关于自我肯定的综述指出，自我感受到威胁时会引发自我保护式的防御，而回想核心价值观能减轻这种防御（Cohen 和 Sherman，2014）。一项涉及 88 项实验的元分析发现，被拒绝会降低情绪和自尊，对自尊的影响为中到大（Gerber 和 Wheeler，2009）。两者合起来，印证了评判会让人难受、而更稳的自我感能让它更容易承受。但两篇论文都没有直接检验书中的练习。

  • Cohen GL, Sherman DK. The psychology of change: self-affirmation and social psychological intervention. Annu Rev Psychol 2014;65:333-371. link链接
  • Gerber J, Wheeler L. On being rejected: a meta-analysis of experimental research on rejection. Perspect Psychol Sci 2009;4(5):468-488. link链接
