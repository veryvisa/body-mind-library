Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Daydreams, night dreams, and wishes for the future hint at what you value and long for, even when they are hazy or conflicted.白日梦、夜里的梦和对未来的愿望，都在暗示你看重和渴望什么，哪怕它们模糊甚至互相矛盾。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Daydreams and goals are clues to who you really are and what you want. Following them with curiosity teaches you about your authentic self.白日梦和目标是你真正是谁、想要什么的线索。带着好奇去看它们，能让你更了解真实的自己。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Daydreams and goals are clues to who you really are and what you want. Following them with curiosity teaches you about your authentic self.

白日梦和目标是你真正是谁、想要什么的线索。带着好奇去看它们，能让你更了解真实的自己。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

"Dreams must turn into plans, or they are a waste." For this activity, dreams are information about you first, not promises. Planning comes later (see Activity 43), and a dream can change without being a failure.

「梦想必须变成计划，否则就是浪费。」在这个练习里，梦想首先是关于你的信息，不是承诺。计划是之后的事（见第 43 个练习），梦想改变也不算失败。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Researchers who compared two ways of thinking about the future found that expecting a good outcome to be likely went with more effort and better results, but enjoying positive fantasies about it did not, and sometimes went with less effort (Oettingen & Mayer, 2002; PubMed record year 2003). Findings come from job seekers, students, and patients, not teens specifically. A fair reading is that dreams are useful for discovering what you value, and later pairing them with realistic steps may help, which is where Activity 43 comes in.

研究者比较了两种想未来的方式，发现「预期好结果很可能发生」与更多努力和更好结果相关，而「沉浸在美好幻想里」则不然，有时还与较少努力相关（Oettingen 与 Mayer，2002；PubMed 记录年份为 2003）。样本是求职者、学生和病人，并非专门的青少年。比较公允的读法是：梦想适合用来发现你看重什么，之后再配上现实的步骤会更有帮助，这就是第 43 个练习要做的。

  • Oettingen G, Mayer D. The motivating function of thinking about the future: expectations versus fantasies. J Pers Soc Psychol 2002;83(5). link链接
