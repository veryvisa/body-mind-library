Part 1 · What self-esteem is, and who holds the power第 1 部分 · 自尊是什么，谁掌握主动权

Activities 1–6练习 1–6 · The book itself has no parts; this six-part grouping is ours, to give a map of the 46 activities.原书不分部分；这六部分是我们为了给 46 个练习画一张地图而做的分组。

1 · What Is Healthy Self-Esteem?什么是健康的自尊
Healthy self-esteem is a quiet, steady feeling that you and everyone else matter equally, so you can own your flaws and enjoy your strengths without putting anyone down.健康的自尊是一种安静而稳的感觉：你和别人一样重要，所以能坦然承认缺点、享受优点，也不必踩低别人。
2 · Recognize Your Power认清你的力量：自尊握在你自己手里
Other people and hard events can hurt, but the final thoughts about yourself are yours to choose, so your self-esteem can start changing at any moment.别人和难事会让人受伤，但对自己的最终看法由你来选，所以自尊在任何时刻都可以开始改变。
3 · Claim Your Intrinsic Value and Worth认领你与生俱来的价值
Your worth was there from the start, it is the same as everyone's, and mistakes or hurtful words cannot remove it; the voices saying otherwise are messages, not facts.你的价值从一开始就在，和每个人一样，错误和伤人的话都拿不走它；说你没价值的声音只是信息，不是事实。
4 · Release Devaluing Messages放下贬低你的信息
We pick up messages about our worth from family, media and culture, often indirectly, and once we spot them we can choose to drop the ones that shrink us.我们从家庭、媒体、文化那里接收关于自己价值的信息，常常是间接的；一旦看出来，就可以选择放下让我们变小的那些。
5 · Choose Positive Self-Messages选择正向的自我信息
You talk to yourself all day, those messages help set your self-esteem, and you can notice them, test them, and write kinder ones.你一整天都在对自己说话，这些话在帮忙塑造你的自尊，你可以去留意它们、检验它们，并写下更友善的。
6 · The Power of Self-Compassion自我关怀的力量
Compassion is caring about the struggles of others and yourself, and you already know how to do it, so you can turn it on yourself with the same words and actions.同情关怀是在乎别人和自己的难处，你本来就会，所以可以用同样的话和行动对准自己。
