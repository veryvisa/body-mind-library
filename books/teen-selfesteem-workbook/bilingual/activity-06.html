Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Compassion is caring about the struggles of others and yourself, and you already know how to do it, so you can turn it on yourself with the same words and actions.同情关怀是在乎别人和自己的难处，你本来就会，所以可以用同样的话和行动对准自己。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Compassion for every person, yourself included, is a cornerstone of healthy self-esteem. You already know how to be compassionate to others, so you can learn to direct it inward.对每个人（包括自己）的同情关怀，是健康自尊的基石。你本来就知道怎么对别人好，所以也能学会把它转向自己。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Compassion for every person, yourself included, is a cornerstone of healthy self-esteem. You already know how to be compassionate to others, so you can learn to direct it inward.

对每个人（包括自己）的同情关怀，是健康自尊的基石。你本来就知道怎么对别人好，所以也能学会把它转向自己。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

A common wrong reading: 'Being kind to myself is weak or selfish.' A better one: care helps you get back up. Self-compassion is not a lack of effort. If you are struggling a lot, reaching out to a trusted adult is itself an act of self-compassion.

常见的误读：「对自己好就是软弱或自私。」更好的理解：关怀帮你重新站起来。自我关怀不是不努力。如果你正很难受，向信任的成年人求助本身就是一种自我关怀。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

A meta-analysis of 79 samples found that more self-compassion goes with better well-being, with a correlation around .47, and a few studies hinted at a causal effect (Zessin et al., 2015). A meta-analysis of 19 studies of adolescents found that higher self-compassion goes with lower anxiety, depression, and stress, about -.55 (Marsh et al., 2018). These are mostly correlations, so they show a link, not proof that practice alone fixes distress.

一项汇总 79 个样本的元分析发现，自我关怀越高，幸福感越好，相关约为 .47，少数研究提示可能有因果作用（Zessin 等，2015）。一项汇总 19 项青少年研究的元分析发现，自我关怀越高，焦虑、抑郁和压力越低，相关约 -.55（Marsh 等，2018）。这些大多是相关，只说明有关联，并不证明单靠练习就能消除困扰。

  • Marsh IC, Chan SWY, MacBeth A. Self-compassion and psychological distress in adolescents - a meta-analysis. Mindfulness 2018;9:1011-1027. doi:10.1007/s12671-017-0850-7 link链接
