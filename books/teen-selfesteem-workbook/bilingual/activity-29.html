Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Peer pressure works best when standing up for yourself feels too hard, so know your own values and have a few easy ways to say no.同伴压力最容易得逞的时候，是你觉得替自己站出来太难；所以先清楚自己的价值，再备几句好用的拒绝话。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Peer pressure is people nudging you toward their way of thinking or acting. People with steady self-esteem neither need to push others nor need to give in, and a few ready phrases make standing firm easier.同伴压力就是别人推你走向他们的想法或做法。自尊稳的人既不需要去压别人，也不需要屈服；备几句现成的话，会让坚持更容易。

What the book says书里怎么说

Peer pressure is people nudging you toward their way of thinking or acting. People with steady self-esteem neither need to push others nor need to give in, and a few ready phrases make standing firm easier.

同伴压力就是别人推你走向他们的想法或做法。自尊稳的人既不需要去压别人，也不需要屈服；备几句现成的话，会让坚持更容易。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Resisting peer pressure means I will lose all my friends.' The book asks you to look at who is truly with you later, not only tonight. If you feel unsafe or pushed toward something risky, tell a parent, counselor or another trusted adult.

「顶住同伴压力，我就会失去所有朋友。」书里让你看的是以后谁真正在你身边，而不只是今晚。如果你觉得不安全，或被推向有风险的事，请告诉家长、辅导老师或其他信任的大人。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

In an experiment with 306 people aged 13 to adult, teens and young adults took more risks and focused more on benefits when two peers were present than when alone, and the effect was stronger in teens (Gardner & Steinberg, 2005). It shows peer pressure is real and common at your age, not a personal failing, but it does not test the book's strategies.

在一项有 306 人（13 岁到成年）参加的实验中，有两位同龄人在场时，青少年和年轻人比独自一人时冒更多风险、更看重好处，而且青少年的效应更强（Gardner 与 Steinberg，2005）。这说明同伴压力在你这个年纪是真实而常见的，并非个人缺点；但它没有检验书里的应对办法。

  • Gardner M, Steinberg L. Peer influence on risk taking, risk preference, and risky decision making in adolescence and adulthood: an experimental study. Dev Psychol 2005;41(4):625-35. link链接
