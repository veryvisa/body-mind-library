Part 4 · Your body, other people's opinions, and relationships第 4 部分 · 身体形象、别人的看法与人际关系

Activities 21–31练习 21–31 · The book itself has no parts; this six-part grouping is ours, to give a map of the 46 activities.原书不分部分；这六部分是我们为了给 46 个练习画一张地图而做的分组。

21 · Your Body Image: How to Fight False Facts身体形象：戳穿虚假的「事实」
Beauty ideals are often edited, changeable, and used to sell products, so you can stop letting them set how you feel about your looks.美丽标准常常经过修图、随年代变化，还被用来卖东西，所以你可以不再让它们决定你对自己外貌的感受。
22 · Your Body Image: Loving the Body You Have身体形象：爱你现有的身体
Shifting attention from how your body looks to what it does for you, and to the many qualities that make people valuable, can lift your self-esteem.把注意力从身体「看起来怎样」转到它「为你做了什么」，再看到让人有价值的很多品质，能提升你的自尊。
23 · About Judgment关于评判
People often judge to feel briefly better about themselves, and your worth does not depend on their judgment, so you can stop judging and stop taking it personally.人们常常评判别人，是为了暂时对自己感觉好一点；你的价值不取决于他们的评判，所以你可以不去评判别人，也不必把评判当成针对自己。
24 · Take Charge of Social Media把社交媒体握在自己手里
Social media has real benefits, but comparing, cyberbullying and fear of missing out can wear down self-esteem, so notice your own patterns and use small tools to stay in charge.社交媒体有真实的好处，但比较、网络欺凌和怕错过会消耗自尊，所以先看清自己的模式，再用一些小工具把主动权握在手里。
25 · How to Talk to People: Basic Social Skills怎样和人开口聊天：基本社交技巧
You do not need a special personality to talk to people; six plain habits, practiced often, make conversation easier and lift your confidence.聊天不需要什么特别的性格；六个朴素的习惯，多练几次，开口就会容易些，信心也跟着长。
26 · Assertive Communication果断沟通
Assertive communication means standing up for yourself calmly while respecting others, and it tends to bring better results than staying silent or lashing out.果断沟通是平静地为自己说话、同时尊重别人；比起忍着不说或冲着人发火，它通常带来更好的结果。
27 · Don't Take Everything Personally别把每件事都往自己身上揽
Other people's words and actions often come from their own day, not from you, so check before deciding something is about you.别人的言行常常来自他们自己的一天，而不是冲着你；先查证，再决定它是不是跟你有关。
28 · No One Is Liked by Everyone没有人能被所有人喜欢
Being liked by every single person is an impossible goal, and your worth does not rise or fall with who likes you.被每一个人喜欢是不可能完成的目标，你的价值不会因为谁喜欢你而涨跌。
29 · Peer Pressure同伴压力
Peer pressure works best when standing up for yourself feels too hard, so know your own values and have a few easy ways to say no.同伴压力最容易得逞的时候，是你觉得替自己站出来太难；所以先清楚自己的价值，再备几句好用的拒绝话。
30 · Setting Healthy Boundaries划定健康的界限
A healthy boundary is a limit you set to protect yourself from disrespect or harm, and acting on it supports your self-esteem.健康的界限是你为了保护自己、不被不尊重或伤害而设下的限度，付诸行动会撑住你的自尊。
31 · The Look of Healthy Self-Esteem健康自尊的样子
How you carry yourself shows your self-esteem, and acting a little more confident and friendly can help you feel that way.你怎样举止会显出你的自尊；表现得稍微自信、友好一点，也会帮你真的感觉如此。
