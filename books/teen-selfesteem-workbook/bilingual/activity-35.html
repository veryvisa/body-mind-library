Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Four thinking habits, summed up as ABOP, shape how you feel about yourself, and you can learn to notice them and choose.四种思维习惯，简称 ABOP，影响你怎么看自己；你可以学着发现它们并做选择。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

The chapter names four habits with short stories of each, then asks you to match four teens to the habit they are using and suggest a better thought, and finally to track your own statements for a few days and pick one habit to work on.本章用小故事介绍四种习惯，再请你为四位少年判断各自用的是哪种习惯并想一个更好的想法，最后追踪自己几天里说过的话，选一个想练的习惯。

Why does the book add Positivity as a habit?书里为什么把积极也算作一种习惯？

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

The chapter names four habits with short stories of each, then asks you to match four teens to the habit they are using and suggest a better thought, and finally to track your own statements for a few days and pick one habit to work on.

本章用小故事介绍四种习惯，再请你为四位少年判断各自用的是哪种习惯并想一个更好的想法，最后追踪自己几天里说过的话，选一个想练的习惯。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

Wrong reading: positive thinking means pretending everything is fine. Better reading: the aim is a more accurate, kinder view, not a fake one. If negative thoughts about yourself feel constant or very heavy, a trusted adult or counselor can help.

常见误读：积极思考就是假装一切都好。更好的理解：目标是更准确也更友善的看法，而不是假的。如果对自己的负面想法一直很强或很沉重，可以找信任的大人或辅导员。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Noticing and testing unhelpful thoughts is a core part of cognitive behavioural therapy, and a 2025 evidence update classed it as well-established for adolescent depression, though evidence for children was weaker (Weersing et al., 2025). That supports the usefulness of the skill, but a workbook is not therapy and cannot replace it.

识别并检验没有帮助的想法是认知行为疗法的核心内容之一。2025 年的一份证据更新把认知行为疗法列为青少年抑郁的「证据确立」疗法，对儿童的证据则较弱（Weersing 等，2025）。这支持了这项本领的用处，但练习册不是治疗，不能取代治疗。

  • Weersing VR, Goger P, Schwartz KTG, et al. Evidence-Base Update of Psychosocial and Combination Treatments for Child and Adolescent Depression. J Clin Child Adolesc Psychol 2025;54(1):1-51. doi:10.1080/15374416.2024.2384022 link链接
