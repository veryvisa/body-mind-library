Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Mistakes are part of being human and can be material for learning, so they do not have to shrink how you see yourself.犯错是做人的一部分，也能成为学习的材料，不必因此看低自己。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

A short story and a quote about an inventor frame mistakes as normal. The pages ask you to spot a hundred human mistakes around you, including your own, rewrite your harsh thoughts, and picture and write a replay of a recent mistake handled with compassion.一个小故事和一句发明家的话把错误说成是正常的。后面请你在身边数出一百个人类的错误，包括自己的，改写苛刻的想法，再想象并写下一次用善意面对近期错误的重演。

What the book says书里怎么说

A short story and a quote about an inventor frame mistakes as normal. The pages ask you to spot a hundred human mistakes around you, including your own, rewrite your harsh thoughts, and picture and write a replay of a recent mistake handled with compassion.

一个小故事和一句发明家的话把错误说成是正常的。后面请你在身边数出一百个人类的错误，包括自己的，改写苛刻的想法，再想象并写下一次用善意面对近期错误的重演。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

Wrong reading: accepting mistakes means not caring about doing well. Better reading: you still care, and you also stop treating one slip as proof you are a bad person. If you are very hard on yourself most days, a trusted adult or counselor can help.

常见误读：接受错误就是不在乎做得好不好。更好的理解：你仍然在乎，同时不再把一次失误当成你不好的证据。如果你几乎每天都对自己很苛刻，可以找信任的大人或辅导员。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

In four experiments with adults, people who were prompted to treat themselves kindly after a failure or weakness were more motivated to improve, for example studying longer after a failed test, than people given a self-esteem boost or a distraction (Breines & Chen, 2012). The participants were not teens, but the finding fits the idea that gentleness can help you learn.

在四项针对成年人的实验里，失败或发现自己弱点后被引导善待自己的人，比被鼓励提升自尊或转移注意的人更有改进的动力，比如考试失败后学得更久（Breines 与 Chen，2012）。参与者不是青少年，但这个发现与「温和有助于学习」的想法一致。

  • Breines JG, Chen S. Self-compassion increases self-improvement motivation. Pers Soc Psychol Bull 2012;38(9):1133-1143. doi:10.1177/0146167212445599 link链接
