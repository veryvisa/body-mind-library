Part 5 · Inner skills and habits of mind第 5 部分 · 内在能力与思维习惯

Activities 32–38练习 32–38 · The book itself has no parts; this six-part grouping is ours, to give a map of the 46 activities.原书不分部分；这六部分是我们为了给 46 个练习画一张地图而做的分组。

32 · The Power of Managing Feelings管理情绪的力量
Every feeling is allowed; what protects you is naming it, accepting it, letting it out safely and then looking after yourself.每种感受都可以有；真正保护你的，是说出它的名字、接纳它、安全地放它出来，再好好照顾自己。
33 · Tolerating Discomfort忍受不舒服
Discomfort is not a stop sign; staying with it, when it is safe, can bring you something worth having.不舒服不是停止标志；在安全的前提下留一会儿，往往能换来值得的东西。
34 · The Power of Inner Peace内心平静的力量
Calm is not something you must go out and get; it is already in you, and simple practices help you reach it.平静不用向外去求，它本来就在你心里，一些简单的练习可以帮你找到它。
35 · Thinking Habits That Help or Hinder有帮助与有妨碍的思维习惯
Four thinking habits, summed up as ABOP, shape how you feel about yourself, and you can learn to notice them and choose.四种思维习惯，简称 ABOP，影响你怎么看自己；你可以学着发现它们并做选择。
36 · Accepting Mistakes接受犯错
Mistakes are part of being human and can be material for learning, so they do not have to shrink how you see yourself.犯错是做人的一部分，也能成为学习的材料，不必因此看低自己。
37 · The Power of Gratitude感恩的力量
Paying attention to what is good in your life and in yourself lifts mood and supports healthy self-esteem.留意生活里和自己身上的好，能提升心情，也支持健康的自尊。
38 · The Power of Possibility可能性的力量
Feeling trapped often comes from have-tos and can-nots in your thinking, and opening your mind shows more choices.觉得被困住，常常来自想法里的「必须」和「不能」，把心打开就会看到更多选择。
