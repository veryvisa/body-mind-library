Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Teen years are for trying ideas out, so being unsure about who you are and what comes next is normal, and you can unfold one step at a time.青少年时期本来就是试各种想法的阶段，对「我是谁、接下来做什么」没把握很正常，可以一步一步慢慢展开。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

It is normal to be unsure about who you are and what you want. Adolescence is a time for testing ideas, and you can give yourself permission to not know everything.不确定自己是谁、想要什么很正常。青少年时期就是测试想法的时期，你可以允许自己不必什么都知道。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

It is normal to be unsure about who you are and what you want. Adolescence is a time for testing ideas, and you can give yourself permission to not know everything.

不确定自己是谁、想要什么很正常。青少年时期就是测试想法的时期，你可以允许自己不必什么都知道。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

"If I am unsure now, I will always be lost." Being unsure now says very little about later; ideas change as you learn. Not knowing is also not the same as not trying, so small experiments still help. If any of this feels heavy, it is okay to talk with a trusted adult or school counselor.

「现在不确定，就会一直迷路。」现在的不确定几乎说明不了以后；想法会随着学习而变。不知道也不等于不去试，小小的尝试仍然有用。如果读到这里觉得沉重，可以找信得过的大人或学校辅导老师聊聊。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Developmental researchers describe identity as something formed and changed over time, shaped by family, friends, and society (Crocetti et al., 2024, a review of the three-factor model). In that framework, exploring and reconsidering choices is part of how identity develops, which fits the book's message that uncertainty is normal. The review is theoretical and does not tell any one teen what to do next.

发展心理学研究者把身份认同看作随时间形成和变化的东西，受家人、朋友和社会影响（Crocetti 等，2024，对三因素模型的综述）。在这个框架里，探索和重新考虑自己的选择是身份发展的一部分，这和书里「不确定很正常」的信息一致。这篇综述偏理论，并不能告诉某个具体的孩子下一步该做什么。

  • Crocetti E, Albarello F, Meeus W. Identities: A developmental social-psychological perspective. Eur Rev Soc Psychol 2024. link链接
