Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Intuition is a deep felt sense you notice in body and mind; learning to listen to it, while checking anything risky, helps you stay true to yourself.直觉是一种在身体和头脑里都能感到的深层感觉；学会倾听它，同时对有风险的念头多核对，能帮你忠于自己。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Intuition is an inner voice felt in both body and mind. Paying attention to it helps you find your authentic self, as long as you check anything that might lead to harm.直觉是在身体和头脑里都能感到的内在声音。留意它，能帮你找到真实的自己，前提是对可能带来伤害的念头多加核对。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Intuition is an inner voice felt in both body and mind. Paying attention to it helps you find your authentic self, as long as you check anything that might lead to harm.

直觉是在身体和头脑里都能感到的内在声音。留意它，能帮你找到真实的自己，前提是对可能带来伤害的念头多加核对。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Intuition is always right, so I should just trust every feeling.' The book itself adds a check: a feeling that points toward harm needs a second look. Feelings are clues to explore, and a trusted person can help weigh them.

「直觉永远是对的，所以每种感觉都该相信。」书里自己就加了一道核对：指向伤害的感觉要再看一眼。感觉是要去探索的线索，信任的人可以帮你权衡。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

In one experiment, people who could track their own heartbeat more accurately linked their body reactions more closely to how aroused they felt, and better body awareness helped or hurt decisions depending on whether the body's signals pointed toward good or bad choices (Dunn et al., 2010). So noticing body signals can inform choices, but the signals themselves are not always right, which fits the checking step.

一项实验发现，能更准确感知自己心跳的人，身体反应与主观兴奋感之间的联系更紧；而身体觉察是帮助还是妨碍决策，要看身体信号指向的是好选择还是坏选择（Dunn 等，2010）。所以留意身体信号能为选择提供线索，但信号本身不一定对，这正对应书里的核对一步。

  • Dunn BD, Galton HC, Morgan R, et al. Listening to your heart: how interoception shapes emotion experience and intuitive decision making. Psychol Sci 2010;21(12):1835-1844. link链接
