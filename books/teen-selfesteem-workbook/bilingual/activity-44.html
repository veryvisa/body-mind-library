Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

A six-step plan, calm first, then define, brainstorm, choose, test and adjust, makes problems feel manageable and builds confidence.六步计划：先平静，再界定、头脑风暴、挑选、试试看、评估调整，让问题变得可处理，也建立信心。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Problems never disappear, but a clear method keeps them from overwhelming you. Practicing the steps builds confidence in handling life's glitches, and that confidence supports healthy self-esteem.问题不会消失，但一个清楚的方法能让你不被压垮。反复练这些步骤，会建立处理生活小麻烦的信心，而这份信心支撑着健康的自尊。

What the book says书里怎么说

Problems never disappear, but a clear method keeps them from overwhelming you. Practicing the steps builds confidence in handling life's glitches, and that confidence supports healthy self-esteem.

问题不会消失，但一个清楚的方法能让你不被压垮。反复练这些步骤，会建立处理生活小麻烦的信心，而这份信心支撑着健康的自尊。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Good problem solvers just know the answer.' They don't; they have a process and keep trying options. And asking for help is part of it. If a problem feels too big or heavy, a trusted adult or counselor can help.

「会解决问题的人一眼就知道答案。」其实不是，他们靠的是一个流程，并不断尝试不同选项；求助也是流程的一部分。如果问题大到让你觉得沉重，可以找信得过的大人或辅导老师聊聊。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

A meta-analysis of 21 samples found problem-solving therapy, which trains problem-solving attitudes and the four skills of defining the problem, generating options, deciding and checking the result, helped reduce depression symptoms about as well as other psychological treatments and medication, and better than no treatment (Bell & D'Zurilla, 2009). It also worked better when it included the whole package. That study is about a clinical program for depression, not a workbook page, so it supports the idea more than this exact six-step list.

一项涵盖 21 个样本的元分析发现，问题解决疗法（训练面对问题的态度，以及界定问题、产生选项、做决定、检验结果这四项技能）对减轻抑郁症状，效果与其他心理治疗和药物相当，好于不治疗（Bell 与 D'Zurilla，2009）；包含完整训练内容时效果更好。该研究针对的是抑郁的临床方案，不是练习册页面，所以它支持的是这个思路，而不是书里这六步本身。

  • Bell AC, D'Zurilla TJ. Problem-solving therapy for depression: a meta-analysis. Clin Psychol Rev 2009;29(4):348-353. doi:10.1016/j.cpr.2009.02.003 link链接
