Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Your likes and dislikes, even small ones, add up to a one-of-a-kind picture of you, and noticing them builds a stronger sense of self.你的喜欢和不喜欢，哪怕很小，合起来就是一幅独一无二的你；留意它们会让自我感更稳。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Looking at what you like and dislike strengthens your sense of self. No one else has your exact mix of preferences.看看自己喜欢和不喜欢什么，会让自我感更强。没有人和你的喜好组合完全一样。

Why look at likes and dislikes to know yourself?为什么看喜好能更了解自己？
Answer in your head, then tap to flip先在心里答，再点一下翻面
Every day choices are mostly steered by what you like, so patterns in them show your tastes, values, and the direction you are heading.每天的选择大多由喜好驱动，所以其中的规律能显示你的口味、价值和前进的方向。

Where do preferences come from?偏好从哪来？
Answer in your head, then tap to flip先在心里答，再点一下翻面
Partly from experience, since we repeat what felt good, and partly from biology, such as how our bodies and brains react to tastes and colors.一部分来自经验，我们会重复让自己愉快的事；一部分来自生理，比如身体和大脑对味道、颜色的反应。

Small preferences are real data about you. Paying attention to them trains you to pay attention to bigger things.小偏好也是关于你的真实信息。留意它们，就是在练习留意更大的事。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Looking at what you like and dislike strengthens your sense of self. No one else has your exact mix of preferences.

看看自己喜欢和不喜欢什么，会让自我感更强。没有人和你的喜好组合完全一样。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

"My tastes are too trivial to say anything about me." Tastes are small, but together they show a pattern. Also, tastes can change, so this is a snapshot, not a verdict.

「我的喜好太琐碎，说明不了什么。」喜好虽小，合起来会显出规律；而且喜好会变，这只是一张快照，不是定论。

    它接在第 12 个练习之后，打开发现之旅，并和第 14 个练习（梦想）、第 15 个练习（信念）、第 16 个练习（热情）配套。它也支持第 8 个练习（真实自我）和第 3 个练习（内在价值）：你的价值不取决于喜好与别人一致。
