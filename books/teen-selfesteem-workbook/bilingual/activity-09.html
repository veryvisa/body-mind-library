Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Your family hands you roles and expectations, and noticing them is a first step toward telling which choices are really yours.家人会递给你角色和期待；先看清它们，才分得出哪些选择真的出自你自己。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Family influences how you think, feel, and act, and it can pull you toward or away from your authentic self. Naming the roles you play is how you start to choose among them.家庭会影响你怎么想、怎么感受、怎么做，可能把你拉近或拉远真实的自己。说出自己在家里扮的角色，才有可能开始选择。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Family influences how you think, feel, and act, and it can pull you toward or away from your authentic self. Naming the roles you play is how you start to choose among them.

家庭会影响你怎么想、怎么感受、怎么做，可能把你拉近或拉远真实的自己。说出自己在家里扮的角色，才有可能开始选择。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

"My family role is just who I am." A role is something you play in a relationship, and it can be loosened. Also, parts of a role may be truly yours. If any of this feels heavy, it is okay to talk with a trusted adult or school counselor.

「我的家庭角色就是我。」角色是你在关系里扮演的一部分，可以慢慢松开；而且角色里也可能有真的属于你的部分。如果读到这里觉得沉重，可以找信得过的大人或学校辅导老师聊聊。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Research on adolescents suggests that acting in ways that are not the real you is common, and the reasons matter. In a study of teens, those who tried out roles for experimentation reported the most positive mood and self-worth, while those who acted falsely because they felt unworthy reported the worst outcomes (Harter et al., 1996). Another line of work found that when parents' approval depended on performance, young adults felt more inner pressure and resentment and more swings in self-esteem (Assor et al., 2004). Both are correlational, mostly from US samples, and do not prove a cause.

对青少年的研究显示，做不像自己的事相当普遍，关键在原因。一项青少年研究发现，出于尝试角色而这么做的人情绪和自我价值感最好，出于觉得自己不够好而假装的人结果最差（Harter 等，1996）。另一项研究发现，父母的认可取决于表现时，年轻人更容易有内在压力、怨气和自尊起伏（Assor 等，2004）。两者都是相关性研究，样本主要来自美国，不能证明因果。

  • Harter S, Marold DB, Whitesell NR, Cobbs G. A model of the effects of perceived parent and peer support on adolescent false self behavior. Child Dev 1996;67(2). link链接
  • Assor A, Roth G, Deci EL. The emotional costs of parents' conditional regard: a self-determination theory analysis. J Pers 2004;72(1). link链接
