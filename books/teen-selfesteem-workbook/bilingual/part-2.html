Part 2 · Being you with family, friends and society第 2 部分 · 在家人、朋友和社会面前做自己

Activities 7–11练习 7–11 · The book itself has no parts; this six-part grouping is ours, to give a map of the 46 activities.原书不分部分；这六部分是我们为了给 46 个练习画一张地图而做的分组。

7 · The Perfection of Diversity差异本身就是完美
Nature runs on variety, you cannot become someone else, so trying to be the best version of yourself is the path that works.自然靠多样运转，你没法变成别人，所以努力成为最好的自己，才是走得通的路。
8 · Your Authentic Self Is Your Best Self真实的自己就是最好的自己
Your authentic self is who you are before you bend to other people's expectations, and checking your choices against it helps you find and trust yourself.真实的自己，是你在向别人期待弯腰之前的那个你；拿它来对照自己的选择，能帮你找到并信任自己。
9 · Who You Are for Your Family在家人面前的你
Your family hands you roles and expectations, and noticing them is a first step toward telling which choices are really yours.家人会递给你角色和期待；先看清它们，才分得出哪些选择真的出自你自己。
10 · Who You Are for Your Friends在朋友面前的你
Friends can change how you think, feel, and act; changing because you enjoy a situation is fine, changing only to fit in is a game.朋友会影响你怎么想、怎么感受、怎么做；因为喜欢而改变没问题，只为合群而改变就成了演戏。
11 · Who You Are for Society在社会面前的你
Society sends messages about how to look, act, and belong; you can notice them and choose which to follow, as long as your choices stay safe.社会会传递外貌、言行和归属的信号；你可以先留意，再决定跟随哪些，只要选择是安全的。
