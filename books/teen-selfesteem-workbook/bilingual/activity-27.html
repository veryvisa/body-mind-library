Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Other people's words and actions often come from their own day, not from you, so check before deciding something is about you.别人的言行常常来自他们自己的一天，而不是冲着你；先查证，再决定它是不是跟你有关。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

People carry their own moods and problems, so what looks like a snub may have nothing to do with you. Pausing to test your guess protects self-esteem even if you never learn the real reason.每个人都带着自己的心情和难处，看起来像冷落的事可能与你无关。停下来检验自己的猜测，即使最后没弄清真相，也能保护自尊。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

People carry their own moods and problems, so what looks like a snub may have nothing to do with you. Pausing to test your guess protects self-esteem even if you never learn the real reason.

每个人都带着自己的心情和难处，看起来像冷落的事可能与你无关。停下来检验自己的猜测，即使最后没弄清真相，也能保护自尊。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'If I never find out the real reason, the exercise failed.' Not so: the value is in loosening the automatic habit of assuming the worst. If hurt feelings stay strong or last long, talking with a trusted adult or counselor can help.

「没弄清真正原因，练习就白做了。」并不是：它的价值在于松动那个自动往最坏处想的习惯。如果难受的感觉很强或持续很久，可以和信任的大人或辅导老师聊聊。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Research on hostile attribution bias, the tendency to read ambiguous behavior as meant to hurt, found that among adolescents aged 13 to 16 a belief that personality cannot change predicted more hostile readings, and a short intervention encouraging a more flexible view reduced them over eight months (Yeager et al., 2013). It studies a related habit and aggression, not this exact worksheet.

关于「敌意归因偏差」（把含糊的行为读成有意伤害）的研究发现：在 13–16 岁青少年中，认为人的性格不会改变，预测更多敌意式解读；一项鼓励更灵活看法的简短干预，在八个月里减少了这类解读（Yeager 等，2013）。它研究的是相关的习惯与攻击行为，不是这张练习表本身。

  • Yeager DS, Miu AS, Powers J, Dweck CS. Implicit theories of personality and attributions of hostile intent: a meta-analysis, an experiment, and a longitudinal intervention. Child Dev 2013;84(5):1651-67. link链接
