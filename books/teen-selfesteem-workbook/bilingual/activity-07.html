Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Nature runs on variety, you cannot become someone else, so trying to be the best version of yourself is the path that works.自然靠多样运转，你没法变成别人，所以努力成为最好的自己，才是走得通的路。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Variety is the natural state of the world. Since you are unique, success and healthy self-esteem come from being your own best self, not from copying someone you think is better.多样是世界的自然状态。你是独一无二的，成功和健康的自尊来自成为最好的自己，而不是复制你觉得更好的人。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Variety is the natural state of the world. Since you are unique, success and healthy self-esteem come from being your own best self, not from copying someone you think is better.

多样是世界的自然状态。你是独一无二的，成功和健康的自尊来自成为最好的自己，而不是复制你觉得更好的人。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

A common wrong reading: 'Being myself means I never need to change.' A better one: you can keep growing; the point is to grow as yourself rather than into a copy of someone else. Admiration can be a clue to what you value.

常见的误读：「做自己就是不用改变。」更好的理解：你可以继续成长，关键是长成你自己，而不是别人的复制品。欣赏某个人，也可以是线索，提示你看重什么。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Self-esteem research suggests that striving to prove yourself in the eyes of others, which is what copying someone often is, carries costs for learning, relationships, and well-being (Crocker & Park, 2004). That is a related finding rather than a test of this activity, but it points the same way: worth built on comparison is shaky.

自尊研究提示，努力在别人眼里证明自己（模仿别人往往就是这样）会对学习、人际和幸福感带来代价（Crocker 与 Park，2004）。这是相关的发现，并不是对本练习的检验，但方向一致：靠比较建起的价值感是不稳的。

  • Crocker J, Park LE. The costly pursuit of self-esteem. Psychol Bull 2004;130(3):392-414. link链接

How it connects和别处怎么连

Activity 3 said everyone has the same worth; this adds that worth does not mean sameness. Activity 8 (authentic self) comes next, and Activity 19 looks at strengths and weaknesses, Activity 24 at social media comparison.

第 3 个练习说每个人价值相同；这里补充：价值相同不等于相同。接下来是第 8 个练习（真实的自己），第 19 个练习看长处与短处，第 24 个练习看社交媒体上的比较。
