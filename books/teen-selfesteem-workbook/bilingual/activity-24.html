Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

Social media has real benefits, but comparing, cyberbullying and fear of missing out can wear down self-esteem, so notice your own patterns and use small tools to stay in charge.社交媒体有真实的好处，但比较、网络欺凌和怕错过会消耗自尊，所以先看清自己的模式，再用一些小工具把主动权握在手里。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Social media is neither good nor bad by itself; noticing how it affects you and using simple tools lets you keep its benefits without losing your footing.社交媒体本身无所谓好坏；留意它怎样影响你、再用一些简单的工具，就能保住它的好处，又不失去自己的立足点。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Social media is neither good nor bad by itself; noticing how it affects you and using simple tools lets you keep its benefits without losing your footing.

社交媒体本身无所谓好坏；留意它怎样影响你、再用一些简单的工具，就能保住它的好处，又不失去自己的立足点。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'If social media makes me feel bad, it is my fault, or I must quit completely.' The activity points to a middle path: notice your pattern, keep your privacy settings tight, and use limits and breaks. If you are being bullied or feel very low, tell a trusted adult or counselor.

「社交媒体让我难受，是我的错，或者我必须彻底戒掉。」练习给的是中间路线：看清自己的模式、把隐私设置收紧、用限制和休息。如果你正被欺凌或心情很低落，请告诉信任的大人或辅导员。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

A study of about 1,000 adults found that passively using Facebook, meaning scrolling without interacting, was linked to lower well-being through upward comparison, while actively using Instagram was linked to more life satisfaction through social support (Masciantonio et al., 2021). A 2026 scoping review of studies of adolescents cautions that time spent alone is a blunt measure and that what teens do online matters (Sursely et al., 2026). Most of this evidence is correlational, so it points to patterns and does not prove that social media alone causes mood changes.

一项针对约 1000 名成年人的研究发现，被动使用 Facebook（只刷不互动）通过向上比较与较低的幸福感相关，而主动使用 Instagram 通过社会支持与更高的生活满意度相关（Masciantonio 等，2021）。2026 年一篇针对青少年研究的范围综述提醒，只看使用时长是个粗糙的指标，青少年在网上做什么更重要（Sursely 等，2026）。这些证据大多是相关性的，只能说明模式，不能证明社交媒体单独导致情绪变化。

  • Masciantonio A, Bourguignon D, Bouchat P, et al. Don't put all social network sites in one basket: Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, TikTok, and their relations with well-being during the COVID-19 pandemic. PLoS One 2021;16(3):e0248384. link链接
  • Sursely J, Baran M, Bardhoshi G, et al. Time out: a scoping review of non-duration based social media use measures and adolescent mental health. J Adolesc 2026. link链接
