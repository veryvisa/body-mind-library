Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Core idea核心

You talk to yourself all day, those messages help set your self-esteem, and you can notice them, test them, and write kinder ones.你一整天都在对自己说话，这些话在帮忙塑造你的自尊，你可以去留意它们、检验它们，并写下更友善的。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

The way you talk to yourself helps build your self-esteem. By noticing and evaluating those messages, you can keep what helps and write new, loving ones to repeat.你怎么对自己说话，会帮着建起你的自尊。留意并评估这些信息之后，你可以留下有帮助的，写新的、充满爱的信息来反复使用。

What the book says书里怎么说

The way you talk to yourself helps build your self-esteem. By noticing and evaluating those messages, you can keep what helps and write new, loving ones to repeat.

你怎么对自己说话，会帮着建起你的自尊。留意并评估这些信息之后，你可以留下有帮助的，写新的、充满爱的信息来反复使用。

Common mix-up常见误读

A common misreading一个常见的误读

A common wrong reading: 'Just repeat nice sentences and I will feel great.' A better one: the sentence has to feel at least a little believable, and noticing and rewriting your messages matters as much as repeating them. If a positive line makes you feel worse, it is fine to soften it.

常见的误读：「只要反复念好听的话，我就会感觉很棒。」更好的理解：那句话至少要让你觉得有点可信，留意和重写信息，与重复它们一样重要。如果一句正面的话让你更难受，把它改得温和一些也完全可以。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

An experiment series found that people with low self-esteem who repeated 'I am a lovable person' felt worse afterwards than those who did not, while people with high self-esteem felt a little better (Wood et al., 2009). So blunt positive sentences can backfire for the very people who most want them. Choosing softer, believable wording is a reasonable response, though that experiment tested short lab tasks, not a long-term workbook habit.

一系列实验发现，低自尊的人反复说「我是个可爱的人」之后，反而比没说的人感觉更差；高自尊的人则感觉略好（Wood 等，2009）。所以生硬的正面句子，可能对最想用它的人反而起反作用。选更温和、说得过去的措辞是合理的应对；不过那些实验测的是短时间的实验室任务，不是长期的练习习惯。

  • Wood JV, Perunovic WQE, L. JW. Positive self-statements: power for some, peril for others. Psychol Sci 2009;20(7):860-866. doi:10.1111/j.1467-9280.2009.02370.x link链接
