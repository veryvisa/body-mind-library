Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

When a situation feels overwhelming, 'stop, breathe, think' turns panic into a plan: pause, calm your body, then work out what to do first, second, and third.局面让人招架不住时，「停、呼吸、想」能把慌乱变成计划：先停下，让身体平静，再想清楚第一步、第二步、第三步做什么。

What the book says书里怎么说

People often feel anxious when they face a situation they think they can't handle; problem-solving skills, remembered as 'stop, breathe, think', help reduce that anxiety.

人在面对自以为处理不了的局面时，常常会焦虑；解决问题的本领——记作「停、呼吸、想」——能帮着减轻这种焦虑。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Good problem solvers don't feel anxious.' Everyone feels the first jolt; the skill is in what comes after it. Another misreading: 'Asking for help means I failed at problem solving.' The book makes asking for help an official step for problems that are too big. The book's explanation that a deep breath sends oxygen to the brain is a simplification; what matters is that a slow breath settles the body enough to think.

「会解决问题的人不会焦虑。」每个人都会先被吓一跳，本领在于之后怎么做。另一种误读是「找人帮忙，说明我解决问题失败了」。书里把「找人帮忙」正式列为处理大问题的一步。书中说深呼吸能把氧气送到大脑，是一种简化；重要的是，一次慢呼吸能让身体安定到可以思考。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Structured problem solving is a recognized therapy in its own right. A meta-analysis of 31 studies found problem-solving therapy clearly better than no treatment or usual care, about as good as other established treatments, and more effective when it included work on attitude toward problems and homework practice (Malouff et al., 2007). The studies were mostly with adults; the book's 'stop, breathe, think' is a short, teen-friendly cousin of that approach, not the full therapy.

结构化的解决问题本身就是一种公认的疗法。一篇汇总 31 项研究的元分析发现，问题解决疗法明显优于不治疗或常规照护，和其他成熟的疗法差不多，而且在包含「对问题的态度」训练和课后练习时更有效（Malouff 等，2007）。研究对象多为成年人；书里的「停、呼吸、想」是这种做法的一个简短、适合青少年的近亲，而不是完整的疗法。

  • Malouff JM, Thorsteinsson EB, Schutte NS. The efficacy of problem solving therapy in reducing mental and physical health problems: a meta-analysis. Clin Psychol Rev 2007;27(1):46–57. doi:10.1016/j.cpr.2005.12.005 link链接
