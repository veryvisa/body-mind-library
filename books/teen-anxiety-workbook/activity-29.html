Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Self-soothing means comforting yourself; sensory items and activities, along with calm, compassionate self-talk, can quiet your body and mind when you feel anxious.

自我安抚就是安慰自己；感官上的小物件和小活动，加上平静、体贴的自我对话，能在你焦虑时让身心安静下来。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Soothing things are for little kids.' Adults use them all the time: a warm shower, a favorite song, a familiar smell. Another misreading: 'Soothing myself means I'm avoiding the problem.' Calming down first often makes it easier to deal with the problem afterward. If you notice you need comfort almost constantly, or are reaching for things that could hurt you, that's a sign to talk with a trusted adult or a professional.

「安抚的东西是给小孩子用的。」大人其实一直在用：热水澡、喜欢的歌、熟悉的气味。另一种误读是「安抚自己就是在逃避问题」。先平静下来，往往之后更容易处理问题。如果你发现自己几乎时时刻刻都需要安慰，或者开始去拿可能伤害自己的东西，那就是该和信任的大人或专业人士聊聊的信号。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

The 'kind self-talk' half of this activity connects to research on self-compassion, which means treating yourself with the kindness you would offer a friend. A meta-analysis of 19 studies with adolescents aged 10 to 19 found a strong link: teens with more self-compassion reported less anxiety, depression, and stress (Marsh, Chan & MacBeth, 2018). Those studies measured the link, not the effect of a soothing kit, so they don't prove that any particular item works; they do suggest that how you talk to yourself matters.

这个练习后半部分的「温和地跟自己说话」，和「自我关怀」的研究有关：用你会对朋友的那份善意来对待自己。一篇汇总 19 项研究、对象为 10 至 19 岁青少年的元分析发现，两者关联很强：自我关怀越多的青少年，报告的焦虑、抑郁和压力越少（Marsh、Chan 与 MacBeth，2018）。这些研究测的是关联，不是某个安抚小物件的效果，所以不能证明某样东西一定管用；但它们提示，你怎样跟自己说话是有分量的。

  • Marsh IC, Chan SWY, MacBeth A. Self-compassion and psychological distress in adolescents—a meta-analysis. Mindfulness 2018;9(4):1011–1027. doi:10.1007/s12671-017-0850-7 link链接
