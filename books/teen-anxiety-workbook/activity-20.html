Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Zooming in on one worrying detail makes it, and the anxiety, look huge; zooming out to the whole situation shows the detail at its real size.盯着一个让人担心的细节，它和焦虑都会显得很大；把镜头拉远看整个局面，细节就回到了真实的大小。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Focusing closely on one or two details can make you lose sight of the whole context, and that pushes anxiety up. Shifting your focus to the bigger picture helps you feel more peaceful.紧盯一两个细节，会让人看不见整个背景，焦虑随之升高。把焦点移到大局上，人会更平静。

What the book says书里怎么说

Focusing closely on one or two details can make you lose sight of the whole context, and that pushes anxiety up. Shifting your focus to the bigger picture helps you feel more peaceful.

紧盯一两个细节，会让人看不见整个背景，焦虑随之升高。把焦点移到大局上，人会更平静。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Looking at the bigger picture means telling myself it doesn't matter.' It does matter; it's just not the only thing that's true. Zooming out adds facts rather than erasing the worrying one. If the bigger picture really does look hard, that's useful too: it tells you where to ask for help.

「看大局，就是告诉自己这事不重要。」它重要，只是并非唯一的真相。拉远镜头是补充事实，而不是抹掉那个让人担心的事实。如果大局看起来确实难，这也有用：它告诉你该在哪里求助。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

A set of seven studies found that 'temporal distancing', picturing how you will feel about a stressful event in the future, helped people cope with negative events, mainly by reminding them that the feeling is temporary (Bruehlman-Senecal & Ayduk, 2015). The question 'will this matter as much in a month or a year?' is one simple way to do that.

一组七项研究发现，「时间上拉开距离」——想象将来的自己会怎么看待一件有压力的事——能帮人应对负面事件，主要途径是提醒人这份感受是暂时的（Bruehlman-Senecal 与 Ayduk，2015）。问自己「一个月、一年之后，它还会这么重要吗？」就是一个简单的做法。

  • Bruehlman-Senecal E, Ayduk O. This too shall pass: temporal distance and the regulation of emotional distress. J Pers Soc Psychol 2015;108(2):356–375. doi:10.1037/a0038324 link链接
