Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Notice an anxious thought, give yourself a clear signal to switch, and move your attention to a calm thought you prepared ahead, as many times as it takes.察觉一个焦虑的念头，给自己一个明确的「换台」信号，把注意力挪到事先准备好的平静念头上；需要几次，就做几次。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Thought stopping helps you let go of anxious thoughts and replace them with peaceful ones: notice the thought, tell yourself firmly to stop, swap in a calm thought you planned ahead, say it, and stay with it. Stubborn thoughts may need this many times.念头叫停帮你放下焦虑的念头、换上平静的念头：察觉它，坚定地叫自己停下，换上事先想好的平静念头，说出来，并停留在那里。顽固的念头可能要这样做很多次。

What the book says书里怎么说

Thought stopping helps you let go of anxious thoughts and replace them with peaceful ones: notice the thought, tell yourself firmly to stop, swap in a calm thought you planned ahead, say it, and stay with it. Stubborn thoughts may need this many times.

念头叫停帮你放下焦虑的念头、换上平静的念头：察觉它，坚定地叫自己停下，换上事先想好的平静念头，说出来，并停留在那里。顽固的念头可能要这样做很多次。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'If I push hard enough, the thought will disappear for good.' Pushing hard against a thought tends to keep it around. The parts of this technique that hold up best are noticing, having a calm thought ready, and gently moving your attention. One of the book's stop-signal ideas is snapping a rubber band on your wrist; you don't need anything that stings. A word, a picture of a stop sign, or a small head shake does the job.

「只要我用力压，这个念头就会永远消失。」越使劲和一个念头对着干，它往往越赖着不走。这个方法里最站得住的部分，是察觉、手边备好平静的念头、温和地挪开注意力。书里有一种停止信号是弹手腕上的橡皮筋；其实用不着任何会疼的东西，一个词、想象一个停车标志、轻轻摇一下头，都够用。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Research on 'thought suppression' adds an important caution. In the famous 'white bear' studies, people told not to think of a white bear later thought about it more than people who were allowed to think about it from the start; giving them a specific other thing to think about reduced that rebound (Wegner et al., 1987). A review of controlled studies found a small to moderate rebound effect overall (Abramowitz et al., 2001). So the parts of thought stopping that fit the evidence best are having a prepared replacement thought and gently redirecting attention, not trying to force a thought out.

关于「思维压抑」的研究提出了一个重要提醒。著名的「白熊」实验里，被要求不要想白熊的人，之后反而比一开始就可以随便想的人想得更多；给他们一个具体的替代念头，可以减轻这种反弹（Wegner 等，1987）。一篇对照研究综述发现，总体上存在小到中等程度的反弹效应（Abramowitz 等，2001）。所以念头叫停里最符合证据的部分，是备好一个替代念头、温和地挪开注意力，而不是硬把念头赶走。

  • Wegner DM, Schneider DJ, Carter SR, White TL. Paradoxical effects of thought suppression. J Pers Soc Psychol 1987;53(1):5–13. doi:10.1037/0022-3514.53.1.5 link链接
  • Abramowitz JS, Tolin DF, Street GP. Paradoxical effects of thought suppression: a meta-analysis of controlled studies. Clin Psychol Rev 2001;21(5):683–703. doi:10.1016/s0272-7358(00)00057-x link链接
