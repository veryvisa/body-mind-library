Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

What you tell yourself about a situation shapes how it feels, so the thought you choose is one of the strongest anxiety tools you have.你对一件事怎么跟自己说，决定了它给你什么感受；所以你选哪个念头，是你手里最有力的应对焦虑的工具之一。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Your thoughts shape your experience. Two people in the same situation can feel very differently because of their attitude, and since you are the one doing the thinking, you can choose a more peaceful way of looking at things.念头塑造你的经历。同一个处境里的两个人，会因为态度不同而感受截然不同；既然念头是你自己在想，你就可以选一个更平和的看法。

No. Both boys were describing the bottle accurately; it really was half full. Attitude means picking the true thought that also helps you, not making up a cheerful one.不是。两个男孩说的都是实话，那瓶水确实只有一半。态度指的是在真实的念头里挑一个对你有帮助的，而不是编一个开心的说法。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Your thoughts shape your experience. Two people in the same situation can feel very differently because of their attitude, and since you are the one doing the thinking, you can choose a more peaceful way of looking at things.

念头塑造你的经历。同一个处境里的两个人，会因为态度不同而感受截然不同；既然念头是你自己在想，你就可以选一个更平和的看法。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'So if I feel anxious, it's my fault for thinking wrong.' Not quite. Thoughts often pop up on their own; you can't always pick the first one. What you can do is notice it and try a second, more helpful one. The book's line that you control every thought is best read as encouragement, not a rule you can fail.

「那我焦虑，就是我想错了，怪我自己。」不完全是。念头常常是自己冒出来的，第一个念头不一定由你选。你能做的，是察觉它，再试一个更有帮助的念头。书里说你能掌控每一个念头，最好当作鼓励来读，而不是一条会让你「不及格」的规定。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Emotion researchers call this move 'cognitive reappraisal': changing how you interpret a situation so that its emotional impact changes. In a well-known set of studies, people who habitually reappraised reported more positive and less negative emotion and better well-being, while people who mainly bottled feelings up (suppression) did worse (Gross & John, 2003). Those studies were with adults and college students and show links, not proof that reappraisal causes the difference.

情绪研究者把这一招叫「认知重评」：改变你对一件事的解读，从而改变它带来的情绪。一组著名的研究发现，习惯重评的人报告的正面情绪更多、负面情绪更少、幸福感更好，而主要靠把感受憋住（压抑）的人情况更差（Gross 与 John，2003）。这些研究的对象是成年人和大学生，显示的是关联，不能证明是重评造成了差别。

  • Gross JJ, John OP. Individual differences in two emotion regulation processes: implications for affect, relationships, and well-being. J Pers Soc Psychol 2003;85(2):348–362. doi:10.1037/0022-3514.85.2.348 link链接

How it connects和别处怎么连

Every activity through 20 is a variation on this one: spotting a thought pattern (9–11), testing a thought (13–14), or widening the view (20). It is also the basic picture behind cognitive therapy: an event, the meaning we give it, and the feeling that follows.

到第 20 个练习为止，每一个都是这一章的变奏：识别想法的套路（9–11）、检验一个念头（13–14）、把视野放大（20）。这也是认知疗法的基本图景：一件事、我们给它的意义、随后而来的感受。
