Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Looking at a big job all at once breeds anxiety; breaking it into small steps and doing just the next one makes it manageable.一下子盯着整件大事，焦虑就会冒出来；把它拆成小步骤，只做眼前这一步，事情就应付得来了。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Trying to think about or do too much at once makes people anxious; breaking a big project into smaller steps lowers that anxiety, since a job taken in small bites feels far lighter.一下子想太多、做太多会让人焦虑；把大项目拆成小步骤能降低这种焦虑，因为一点一点来，事情就变容易了。

What the book says书里怎么说

Trying to think about or do too much at once makes people anxious; breaking a big project into smaller steps lowers that anxiety, since a job taken in small bites feels far lighter.

一下子想太多、做太多会让人焦虑；把大项目拆成小步骤能降低这种焦虑，因为一点一点来，事情就变容易了。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Breaking it up just makes a longer to-do list.' The list may be longer, but each item is something you can actually start, and starting is usually the hardest part. Another misreading: 'If I've broken it down, I should be able to do it all today.' Small steps work best when you also give them realistic times, which is where Activity 38 comes in.

「拆开只会让待办清单更长。」清单也许更长，但每一项都是你真能动手开始的事，而开始往往是最难的一步。另一种误读是「既然拆好了，今天就该全做完」。小步骤要配上实际可行的时间才最管用，这正是第 38 个练习要讲的。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Research on procrastination found that how unpleasant a task feels is one of the strongest and most consistent predictors of putting it off (Steel, 2007). Breaking a big job into small, concrete steps is one way to make each piece feel less aversive, though that review did not test task-splitting directly. A related line of research found that making specific 'if-then' plans (for example, 'when I get home at 4, I'll write the outline') had a medium-to-large effect on reaching goals across 94 tests (Gollwitzer & Sheeran, 2006). Both bodies of research are mostly with adults and students.

关于拖延的研究发现，一件事让人感觉有多讨厌，是最强、最稳定地预示人会拖延的因素之一（Steel，2007）。把一件大事拆成具体的小步骤，是让每一块显得没那么讨厌的办法之一，不过那篇综述并没有直接检验「拆小」。另一条相关的研究发现，订出具体的「如果……就……」计划（例如「四点到家，我就写提纲」），在 94 项检验中对达成目标有中等到较大的效果（Gollwitzer 与 Sheeran，2006）。这两类研究的对象多为成年人和学生。

  • Steel P. The nature of procrastination: a meta-analytic and theoretical review of quintessential self-regulatory failure. Psychol Bull 2007;133(1):65–94. doi:10.1037/0033-2909.133.1.65 link链接
  • Gollwitzer PM, Sheeran P. Implementation intentions and goal achievement: a meta-analysis of effects and processes. Adv Exp Soc Psychol 2006;38:69–119. doi:10.1016/S0065-2601(06)38002-1 link链接

How it connects和别处怎么连

It extends 'think: first, second, third' from Activity 34. Taking one step at a time also echoes centering (26), and dropping perfectionism (15) helps too, since 'one good-enough step' is easier to start than 'one perfect project'. Activities 37 and 38 build on this with organizing and scheduling.

它延伸了第 34 个练习里「想：第一、第二、第三步」。「一次只做一步」也呼应找重心（26）；放下完美主义（15）也有帮助，因为「一个够好的步骤」比「一个完美的项目」更容易开始。第 37、38 个练习在此基础上讲整理和排时间。
