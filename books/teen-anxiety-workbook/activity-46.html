Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

The goal is managing anxiety, not getting rid of it forever; new challenges will keep coming, and feeling anxious again means you're human, not that you've failed.目标是管理焦虑，而不是永远消灭它；新的挑战会一直到来，再次感到焦虑说明你是个普通人，不说明你失败了。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Working through this book has given you real skills for managing anxiety; that doesn't mean you'll never feel anxious again, but it does mean you can move through life more peacefully. Managing anxiety and eliminating it are two different things.做完这本书，你已经掌握了应对焦虑的真本领；这不代表你再也不会焦虑，但意味着你能更平和地走过人生。管理焦虑和消灭焦虑，是两回事。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Working through this book has given you real skills for managing anxiety; that doesn't mean you'll never feel anxious again, but it does mean you can move through life more peacefully. Managing anxiety and eliminating it are two different things.

做完这本书，你已经掌握了应对焦虑的真本领；这不代表你再也不会焦虑，但意味着你能更平和地走过人生。管理焦虑和消灭焦虑，是两回事。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'If I still get anxious, the book didn't work.' The book's whole last chapter is there to say the opposite. Another misreading: 'Managing anxiety is something I have to do alone.' The book points to more books, classes, and teachers, and for some people, therapy or other professional help is part of the plan. Needing that is not a failure either.

「我还会焦虑，说明这本书没用。」书的最后一整章，就是为了说相反的话。另一种误读是「管理焦虑必须靠自己一个人」。书里提到还有更多的书、课程和老师可以学；对有些人来说，心理治疗或其他专业帮助也是计划的一部分。需要这些，同样不是失败。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Long-term research backs the book's realistic message. About six years after treatment in a large US study of young people with anxiety disorders, almost half (46.5%) were in remission; those who improved during treatment were more likely to stay well, but almost half of them (48%) had a relapse at some point (Ginsburg et al., 2014). Anxiety coming back does not mean the skills failed; it is common, and it is a signal to use your tools again or to get more support.

长期研究支持书里这番实在的话。美国一项针对焦虑障碍青少年的大型研究显示，治疗后大约六年，近一半（46.5%）的人处于缓解状态；治疗期间好转的人更可能保持良好，但其中也有近一半（48%）在某个时候复发过（Ginsburg 等，2014）。焦虑回来，不代表本领失效了；这很常见，它是一个信号：再把工具用起来，或者寻求更多支持。
