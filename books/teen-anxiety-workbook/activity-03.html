Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Anxiety shows up differently in different people — in sleep, in the stomach, in restlessness — and learning your own signals helps you catch it early and pick a tool.焦虑在每个人身上表现不同——在睡眠里、在肚子里、在坐立不安里；认清自己的信号，就能早点发现它并挑一个合适的工具。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Anxiety looks different in each person. Becoming aware of your own symptoms — in body, mind, and mood — helps you recognize what is happening and choose coping techniques.焦虑在每个人身上都不一样。认清自己身体、头脑、情绪上的症状，能帮你明白正在发生什么，并选择应对办法。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Anxiety looks different in each person. Becoming aware of your own symptoms — in body, mind, and mood — helps you recognize what is happening and choose coping techniques.

焦虑在每个人身上都不一样。认清自己身体、头脑、情绪上的症状，能帮你明白正在发生什么，并选择应对办法。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'If my anxiety doesn't look like panic attacks, it isn't real anxiety.' Anxiety can be quiet — poor sleep, stomachaches, irritability, trouble focusing — and still be worth working on.

「我没有惊恐发作，就不算真的焦虑。」焦虑也可以很安静——睡不好、肚子疼、易怒、难专注——同样值得处理。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

The diagnostic criteria for generalized anxiety disorder list six associated signs: restlessness or feeling on edge, tiring easily, trouble concentrating or the mind going blank, irritability, muscle tension, and sleep problems (DSM-5 criteria as summarized in StatPearls). A consensus review on 'interoception', how we sense signals from inside the body, describes problems with it as an increasingly recognized part of anxiety disorders (Khalsa et al., 2018). Only a clinician can diagnose; the list simply shows that anxiety is often quiet and physical.

广泛性焦虑症的诊断标准列出六种伴随表现：坐立不安或紧绷、容易疲劳、难以集中注意力或脑子一片空白、易怒、肌肉紧张、睡眠问题（StatPearls 归纳的 DSM-5 标准）。一篇关于「内感受」（我们怎样感知身体内部信号）的共识综述指出，内感受出问题，正日益被认为是焦虑障碍的一个组成部分（Khalsa 等，2018）。诊断只能由专业人员做；这份清单只是说明，焦虑常常是安静的、身体上的。

  • Munir S, Takov V. Generalized Anxiety Disorder. In: StatPearls. Treasure Island (FL): StatPearls Publishing (summarizing DSM-5 criteria). link链接
  • Khalsa SS, Adolphs R, Cameron OG, et al. Interoception and mental health: a roadmap. Biol Psychiatry Cogn Neurosci Neuroimaging 2018;3(6):501–513. doi:10.1016/j.bpsc.2017.12.004 link链接
