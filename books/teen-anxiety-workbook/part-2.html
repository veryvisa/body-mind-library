Part 2 · Changing the thoughts that feed anxiety第 2 部分 · 换个想法

Activities 7–20练习 7–20 · The book itself has no parts; this six-part grouping is ours, to give a map of the 46 activities.原书不分部分；这六部分是我们为了给 46 个练习画一张地图而做的分组。

7 · The Power of Attitude态度的力量
What you tell yourself about a situation shapes how it feels, so the thought you choose is one of the strongest anxiety tools you have.你对一件事怎么跟自己说，决定了它给你什么感受；所以你选哪个念头，是你手里最有力的应对焦虑的工具之一。
8 · Worrying Is Worthless担心没有用
Worry feels like doing something, but it uses up energy without changing what happens, and seeing that for yourself makes room for something more useful.担心让人觉得自己在做点什么，其实它耗掉力气，却改变不了结果；亲眼看清这一点，就能腾出位置去做更有用的事。
9 · All-or-Nothing Thinking非黑即白
Judging yourself as either perfect or a total failure leaves no room for real life, which nearly always happens in the gray in between.把自己要么判成完美、要么判成彻底失败，就没给真实生活留位置——而真实生活几乎总是发生在中间的灰色地带。
10 · Overgeneralizing以偏概全
One bad experience is not a forecast; words like always, never, everyone, and no one turn a single event into a rule that the evidence doesn't support.一次不好的经历不是预言；「总是、从不、所有人、没有人」这类词，会把一件事变成一条证据撑不起来的规律。
11 · "Should" Statements「应该」句式
Some 'shoulds' are useful reminders, but unrealistic or unimportant ones pile on pressure; sorting them lets you keep the useful ones and drop the rest.有些「应该」是有用的提醒，但不现实或不重要的「应该」只会叠加压力；把它们分一分，留下有用的，放下其余的。
12 · Thought Stopping念头叫停
Notice an anxious thought, give yourself a clear signal to switch, and move your attention to a calm thought you prepared ahead, as many times as it takes.察觉一个焦虑的念头，给自己一个明确的「换台」信号，把注意力挪到事先准备好的平静念头上；需要几次，就做几次。
13 · What's the Worst That Could Happen?最坏会怎样？
Ask what the worst outcome would be, how likely it really is, and how you would cope if it happened; together the answers usually shrink a fear to its real size.问自己：最坏的结果是什么？它真有多大可能？真发生了我会怎么应对？三个答案合起来，通常能把恐惧缩回它真实的大小。
14 · Cost-Benefit Analysis得失清单
Listing honestly what a belief gives you and what it costs you shows whether it's worth keeping, and how you might get its benefits another way.老老实实列出一个信念给了你什么、又让你付出了什么，就能看出它值不值得留着，以及能不能换条路得到它的好处。
15 · Perfectionism完美主义
Since no one can be perfect, aiming for perfection guarantees falling short and feeling anxious; aiming for your best keeps high standards without the constant alarm.既然没有人能完美，以完美为目标就注定会落空、会焦虑；以「尽力就好」为目标，标准照样高，警报却不再一直响。
16 · Pressure to Succeed成功的压力
The pressure to get everything right eases when you widen what 'success' means, remember that most paths wind, and keep your attention on today.当你把「成功」的意思放宽、记得大多数人生路都是弯的、并把注意力放在今天，「必须样样做对」的压力就会松下来。
17 · Letting Go of Control放下掌控
Trying to control what can't be controlled keeps anxiety high; sorting what's yours to steer from what isn't lets you put your energy where it works.想掌控掌控不了的东西，焦虑就降不下来；分清哪些归你管、哪些不归，才能把力气用在起作用的地方。
18 · Positive Affirmations正面的自我对话
The messages you repeat to yourself shape your anxiety; noticing them and rewriting the negative ones into realistic, kind ones helps you feel steadier.你反复对自己说的话会塑造你的焦虑；留意它们，把消极的改写成真实又友善的话，人会更稳。
19 · Serenity, Courage, and Wisdom平静、勇气与智慧
Step back, sort what you can change from what you can't, accept the first kind calmly and act on the second with courage; one short line helps you remember how.退后一步，分清哪些能改、哪些不能改：不能改的平静接受，能改的鼓起勇气去做；一句短短的话帮你记住怎么做。
20 · Seeing the Bigger Picture看大局
Zooming in on one worrying detail makes it, and the anxiety, look huge; zooming out to the whole situation shows the detail at its real size.盯着一个让人担心的细节，它和焦虑都会显得很大；把镜头拉远看整个局面，细节就回到了真实的大小。
