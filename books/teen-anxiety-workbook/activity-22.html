Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Writing your anxious feelings down, with no rules about spelling or neatness, is a private way to get them out of your head.把焦虑的感受写下来，不管拼写、不管工整，是把它们从脑子里拿出来的一种私人办法。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Writing is an effective way to express and release anxious feelings, and it needs no special talent because you are the only reader.写作是表达和释放焦虑感受的有效办法；不需要什么写作天分，因为读者只有你自己。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Writing is an effective way to express and release anxious feelings, and it needs no special talent because you are the only reader.

写作是表达和释放焦虑感受的有效办法；不需要什么写作天分，因为读者只有你自己。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'I'm bad at writing, so this isn't for me.' Jayden thought so too, but school writing rules simply don't apply here: messy, misspelled, and half-finished all count. Another misreading: 'Once I've written it, the problem is solved.' Writing can take the edge off a feeling; it doesn't replace talking to someone when a problem is big.

「我写作不好，这个不适合我。」杰登也这么想过，但学校里的写作规矩在这里根本不适用：乱、错字、没写完，都算数。另一种误读是「写完问题就解决了」。写作能让情绪不那么尖锐，但事情很大时，它不能代替找人谈。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Writing about feelings has been tested in well over a hundred randomized studies. A meta-analysis of 146 of them found that this kind of 'expressive writing' helps on average, but the average effect is small (Frattaroli, 2006; most participants were adults and students). So it is a reasonable, low-cost thing to try, not a guaranteed fix, and how much it helps varies from person to person.

写下感受这件事，已经在一百多项随机研究里检验过。一篇汇总其中 146 项的元分析发现，这种「表达性写作」平均来说有帮助，但平均效果不大（Frattaroli，2006；受试者多为成年人和学生）。所以它是一件值得试、成本很低的事，但不是保证有效的办法，帮助多大也因人而异。

  • Frattaroli J. Experimental disclosure and its moderators: a meta-analysis. Psychol Bull 2006;132(6):823–865. doi:10.1037/0033-2909.132.6.823 link链接
