Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Step back, sort what you can change from what you can't, accept the first kind calmly and act on the second with courage; one short line helps you remember how.退后一步，分清哪些能改、哪些不能改：不能改的平静接受，能改的鼓起勇气去做；一句短短的话帮你记住怎么做。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

You can lower anxiety with serenity, courage, and wisdom: step back, work out the difference between what you can and can't change, and act on that knowledge. Repeating the short line helps.你可以用平静、勇气与智慧来降低焦虑：退后一步，弄清能改的和不能改的有什么区别，然后按这个认识去做。反复念那句短话会有帮助。

“I have the serenity to accept the things I cannot change, the courage to change the things I can, and the wisdom to know the difference.” — the book, p. 76原书第 76 页

What the book says书里怎么说

You can lower anxiety with serenity, courage, and wisdom: step back, work out the difference between what you can and can't change, and act on that knowledge. Repeating the short line helps.

你可以用平静、勇气与智慧来降低焦虑：退后一步，弄清能改的和不能改的有什么区别，然后按这个认识去做。反复念那句短话会有帮助。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Accepting means I'm giving up.' Acceptance here is only for the parts you truly can't change, and it frees energy for the parts you can. Gabriella didn't give up on her friends; she stopped trying to control their apology and started setting her own limits.

「接受就是放弃。」这里的接受只针对真的改不了的部分，而且它会把力气腾出来，用在能改的部分。加布里埃拉并没有放弃朋友，她只是不再试图左右她们道不道歉，转而划出自己的界限。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

The line Gabriella's aunt uses is adapted from the Serenity Prayer, usually credited to the theologian Reinhold Niebuhr. Its authorship was questioned in 2008, but the quotation scholar Fred Shapiro, who had raised the doubt, said in 2009 that a newly found 1937 reference made Niebuhr the most likely author. The book turns the prayer into a first-person statement anyone can use.

加布里埃拉姑妈用的那句话改编自《宁静祷文》，通常认为出自神学家莱因霍尔德·尼布尔。2008 年它的作者曾受到质疑，但提出质疑的引语学者弗雷德·夏皮罗在 2009 年表示，新发现的一条 1937 年的记载让尼布尔成为最可能的作者。书里把祷文改成了任何人都能用的第一人称句子。
