Mind map思维导图

When to use it什么时候用Before you start an activity, to see where things go — or after, to see it all at once.开始一个练习之前，先看东西都放在哪；或者读完后，一眼看全。
How to start怎么开始Write the topic in the middle. Add three to five branches for the big ideas. Hang short words on each branch — words, not sentences.把主题写在中间。画三到五个分支放大想法。每个分支上挂几个短词——是词，不是句子。

Method 2 of 6第 2 种，共 6 种

Cornell notes康奈尔笔记

When to use it什么时候用While you read or listen, when you want notes you can quiz yourself with later.边读边听的时候，想要以后能拿来自测的笔记。
How to start怎么开始Split the page: a narrow cue column on the left, notes on the right, a summary strip at the bottom. Notes first; afterward write one question per note on the left; cover the right side and answer.把纸分三块：左边窄的提示栏、右边笔记栏、底部总结栏。先写右边的笔记；读完在左边给每条笔记写一个问题；盖住右边自己答。
Five-minute version五分钟版本Read one short section. Three notes on the right, three questions on the left, one summary sentence.读一小段。右边三条笔记，左边三个问题，底下一句总结。

Example: Activity 3示范：练习 3

Cues (questions)提示（问题）
Notes笔记
Does everyone feel anxiety the same way?每个人的焦虑感受都一样吗？
No. One person can't sleep, another gets stomach knots and snaps at people, another can't sit still or focus. All are normal forms of anxiety.不一样。有人睡不着，有人胃里打结、动不动发火，有人坐不住、静不下心。都是焦虑的正常样子。
Why can irritability be a sign of anxiety?为什么易怒也可能是焦虑的信号？
When the body is tense and on alert, small things feel bigger, so you may snap without a clear reason — and then feel bad about it.身体一直绷着、处于戒备时，小事也显得大，于是可能无缘无故发火，事后又难过。
What is the point of mapping where anxiety shows up in your body?在身体图上标出焦虑出现的位置，有什么用？
Your body often signals anxiety before your mind names it. Knowing your first signals lets you step in earlier, when it is easier to calm.身体往往比头脑更早报警。认得自己的第一批信号，就能在还容易平复的时候早点出手。
If you can't remember what your anxiety feels like, what can you do?如果想不起自己焦虑时是什么感觉，可以怎么办？
Watch for it over the next week like a curious observer, then come back and fill in the picture with what you noticed.接下来一周像个好奇的观察者那样留意，再回来把观察到的填进图里。
Summary总结: Anxiety shows up differently in different people — in sleep, in the stomach, in restlessness — and learning your own signals helps you catch it early and pick a tool.焦虑在每个人身上表现不同——在睡眠里、在肚子里、在坐立不安里；认清自己的信号，就能早点发现它并挑一个合适的工具。

Method 3 of 6第 3 种，共 6 种

Explain it simply (Feynman)讲给别人听（费曼法）

When to use it什么时候用When you think you understand something but aren't quite sure.觉得自己懂了，但又不太确定的时候。
How to start怎么开始Explain it out loud or on paper as if to a younger friend, in plain words. Wherever you get stuck or reach for a fancy word, go back to the book. Then explain it again, shorter.用大白话，像讲给比你小的朋友那样，说出来或写下来。哪里卡住、哪里想用大词，就回去看书。然后再讲一遍，更短。
Five-minute version五分钟版本Two minutes explaining, two minutes checking the gaps, one minute saying it in a single sentence.两分钟讲、两分钟查漏洞、一分钟压成一句话。

Example: Activity 3示范：练习 3

You know how a smoke alarm sometimes goes off just for burnt toast? Anxiety is a bit like that alarm — and it doesn't beep the same way for everyone. For one person it's lying awake at night; for another it's a knot in the stomach or snapping at people; for someone else it's not being able to sit still. The useful part is learning your own first beep. If you know your stomach tightens before things get bad, you can do something calming right then, while it's still small.你知道烟雾报警器有时只是因为面包烤焦就响吗？焦虑有点像那个报警器——而且它在每个人身上响的方式不一样。有人是夜里睡不着；有人是胃里打结、动不动发火；也有人是坐都坐不住。有用的地方在于：认出你自己的「第一声响」。如果你知道事情变糟之前胃会先发紧，那一刻就可以做点让自己平静的事，趁它还小。

Where I got stuck: why is being irritable a sign of anxiety? I checked the cards: when the body is tense and on alert, small things feel bigger. Now I can explain it.我卡住的地方：为什么易怒也是焦虑的信号？回去翻了卡片：身体绷紧、处于戒备时，小事也显得大。现在我讲得出来了。

Shorter, in one sentence:再短一点，一句话： Anxiety doesn't look the same for everyone — for some it's bad sleep, for others it's a stomachache or snapping at people — so it helps to learn your own early signs.焦虑在每个人身上不一样——有人是睡不好，有人是肚子疼或爱发火——所以认清自己的早期信号很有帮助。

Method 4 of 6第 4 种，共 6 种

Question chain问题链

When to use it什么时候用When an idea feels flat and you want to go deeper — or when you feel stuck.觉得一个想法很平、想往深里走，或者卡住了的时候。
How to start怎么开始Start with one question. Answer it in one line. Turn the answer into the next question — why? how? what if? Go four or five links deep.从一个问题开始。用一行回答。把答案变成下一个问题——为什么？怎么做？如果……呢？往下走四五环。
Five-minute version五分钟版本One starting question and four links, one line each.一个起始问题，四环追问，每环一行。

Example: Activity 3示范：练习 3

  1. How does anxiety show up for me?我的焦虑是什么样子？
    Maybe a tight stomach before tests, and snapping at my brother afterward.可能是考试前胃发紧，考完冲家人发火。
  2. Why would anxiety show up in my stomach or my mood, not just my thoughts?为什么焦虑会出现在胃里、情绪里，而不只是脑子里？
    Because anxiety is a body alarm, not only a thought (Activity 2). A tense body makes small things feel bigger.因为焦虑是身体的警报，不只是念头（练习 2）。身体绷着，小事也显得大。
  3. If my body notices first, what does that let me do?如果身体先察觉，这让我能做什么？
    Catch it early, while it's still small and easier to calm.趁它还小、还容易平复的时候早点发现。
  4. How would I catch it early, in real life?现实里，我怎样早点发现？
    Notice my first signal — the stomach — and try one tool, like slow breathing (Activity 30).留意我的第一信号——胃——然后试一个工具，比如慢呼吸（练习 30）。
  5. What if I can't tell what my signals are?如果我说不清自己的信号呢？
    Watch myself for a week like a curious observer, then fill in the body map.像个好奇的观察者那样观察自己一周，再回来填身体图。

Method 5 of 6第 5 种，共 6 种

Concept map概念地图

When to use it什么时候用When the links between ideas matter as much as the ideas themselves.当想法之间的联系和想法本身一样重要的时候。
How to start怎么开始Put five to eight key ideas in boxes. Draw arrows between them and write a linking phrase on every arrow (shows up as, leads to, helps you…). Each arrow should read as a sentence.把五到八个关键概念放进方框。在它们之间画箭头，每个箭头上写一个连接短语（表现为、导致、帮你……）。每个箭头读起来都应该是一句话。
Five-minute version五分钟版本Five ideas, four arrows, every arrow labelled.五个概念、四个箭头，每个箭头都写上字。

Method 6 of 6第 6 种，共 6 种

One-page summary一页纸摘要

When to use it什么时候用After finishing an activity, or the night before a test — so you can review it in one minute.读完一个练习，或者考试前一晚——让自己一分钟就能复习完。
How to start怎么开始Four boxes only: the main idea in one sentence, three key points, one small picture or table, one thing I'll try. If it doesn't fit, it doesn't go in — that's the point.只有四个框：一句话主旨、三个要点、一张小图或小表、一件我要试的事。放不下的就不放——这正是关键。
Five-minute version五分钟版本One sentence, three points, a tiny sketch, one action.一句话、三个要点、一个小草图、一个行动。

Example: Activity 3示范：练习 3

Main idea一句话主旨
Anxiety shows up differently in different people — in sleep, in the stomach, in restlessness — and learning your own signals helps you catch it early and pick a tool.焦虑在每个人身上表现不同——在睡眠里、在肚子里、在坐立不安里；认清自己的信号，就能早点发现它并挑一个合适的工具。

Three key points三个要点
  • No. One person can't sleep, another gets stomach knots and snaps at people, another can't sit still or focus. All are normal forms of anxiety.不一样。有人睡不着，有人胃里打结、动不动发火，有人坐不住、静不下心。都是焦虑的正常样子。
  • When the body is tense and on alert, small things feel bigger, so you may snap without a clear reason — and then feel bad about it.身体一直绷着、处于戒备时，小事也显得大，于是可能无缘无故发火，事后又难过。
  • Your body often signals anxiety before your mind names it. Knowing your first signals lets you step in earlier, when it is easier to calm.身体往往比头脑更早报警。认得自己的第一批信号，就能在还容易平复的时候早点出手。
