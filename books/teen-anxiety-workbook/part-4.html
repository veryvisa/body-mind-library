Part 4 · Calming the body and mind第 4 部分 · 让身体和心静下来

Activities 25–33练习 25–33 · The book itself has no parts; this six-part grouping is ours, to give a map of the 46 activities.原书不分部分；这六部分是我们为了给 46 个练习画一张地图而做的分组。

25 · Progressive Relaxation渐进式放松
Anxiety tightens your muscles, so slowly moving your attention through your body and letting each area loosen is a learnable way to bring anxiety down.焦虑会让肌肉绷紧；所以让注意力慢慢走过全身、让每个部位依次松下来，是一种学得会的降低焦虑的办法。
26 · Centering Yourself找到重心
When things pile up and you feel scattered, a few seconds of attention on your body's center of gravity can steady you enough to act instead of react.事情堆在一起、人觉得七零八落时，花几秒钟把注意力放在身体的重心上，能让你稳下来，去「做」而不是被推着「反应」。
27 · Mindfulness正念
Mindfulness means keeping your attention on what is happening right now, without judging it; practiced often, it leaves anxiety less room to grow.正念就是把注意力放在此时此刻正在发生的事上，而且不评判；常常练习，焦虑就少了生长的空间。
28 · Grounding Techniques着地技巧
Grounding techniques are quick, concrete ways to pull your attention out of anxious thoughts and into what is around you right now.着地技巧是一些快速、具体的办法，把注意力从焦虑的念头里拉出来，放到此刻身边的事物上。
29 · Self-Soothing自我安抚
Self-soothing means comforting yourself, through your senses and through kind self-talk, so your body and mind can settle when you're anxious.自我安抚就是安慰自己——通过感官，也通过温和的自我对话——让身心在焦虑时安定下来。
30 · Using Your Breath用好呼吸
Anxious breathing is fast and shallow; slowing it down on purpose calms the body and clears the mind, and you can do it anywhere, as prevention or in the moment.焦虑时的呼吸又快又浅；有意识地放慢它，能让身体平静、头脑清楚；随时随地都能做，既可预防，也可当场用。
31 · Visualization想象练习
Your body reacts to the pictures in your mind, so imagining a peaceful place in rich detail can help it relax, even though nothing around you has changed.身体会对脑海里的画面作出反应；所以细细地想象一个安宁的地方，即使周围什么都没变，身体也能放松下来。
32 · Meditation冥想
Meditation is practice in bringing your attention back to something peaceful each time it wanders; done a little each day, it builds a steadier mind.冥想就是一次次练习：每当注意力飘走，就把它带回一个平静的东西上；每天做一点，心会越来越稳。
33 · A Higher Power更高的力量
If you believe in a power greater than yourself, that belief can be a source of comfort and calm; if you don't, or aren't sure, that's fine too.如果你相信有一种比自己更大的力量，这份信念可以带来安慰和平静；如果你不信，或者还不确定，也完全没关系。
