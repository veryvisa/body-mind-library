Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

One bad experience is not a forecast; words like always, never, everyone, and no one turn a single event into a rule that the evidence doesn't support.一次不好的经历不是预言；「总是、从不、所有人、没有人」这类词，会把一件事变成一条证据撑不起来的规律。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Overgeneralizing means assuming that because something went badly once, it will always go badly, without evidence. It raises anxiety in any situation that has ever gone wrong before, and it shows up in words like always, never, no one, and everyone.以偏概全，就是没有证据地认定：一件事糟过一次，就会一直糟下去。它让人在任何曾经出过岔子的场合都焦虑，常常藏在「总是、从不、没有人、所有人」这些词里。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Overgeneralizing means assuming that because something went badly once, it will always go badly, without evidence. It raises anxiety in any situation that has ever gone wrong before, and it shows up in words like always, never, no one, and everyone.

以偏概全，就是没有证据地认定：一件事糟过一次，就会一直糟下去。它让人在任何曾经出过岔子的场合都焦虑，常常藏在「总是、从不、没有人、所有人」这些词里。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'But it really did happen more than once.' Sometimes a pattern is real. The point isn't to deny it but to describe it accurately: 'This has happened three times when I didn't prepare' is a pattern you can work on; 'I always fail' is not.

「可它确实不止发生过一次。」有时规律是真的。重点不是否认，而是准确地描述它：「我没准备的那三次都出了问题」是一个可以改进的规律；「我总是失败」则不是。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Overgeneralization is one of the ten cognitive distortions on D. Burns's widely used list, which builds on Aaron Beck's cognitive therapy (Beck, 1963). Lauren's choice to turn down the next babysitting job also matches a well-studied idea: avoiding feared situations is usually seen as one of the things that keeps anxiety going, because the fear never gets tested (Hofmann & Hay, 2018).

以偏概全是大卫·伯恩斯那份广为使用的十种认知扭曲清单里的一条，这份清单建立在亚伦·贝克的认知疗法之上（Beck，1963）。劳伦推掉下一次照看孩子的机会，也符合一个研究很多的观点：回避害怕的场合，通常被看作让焦虑持续下去的原因之一，因为恐惧永远得不到检验（Hofmann 与 Hay，2018）。
