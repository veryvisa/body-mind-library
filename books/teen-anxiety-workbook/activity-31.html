Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Your body reacts to the pictures in your mind, so imagining a peaceful place in rich detail can help it relax, even though nothing around you has changed.身体会对脑海里的画面作出反应；所以细细地想象一个安宁的地方，即使周围什么都没变，身体也能放松下来。

What the book says书里怎么说

Your body responds to what your mind tells it: imagine a tense situation and it tightens; imagine a peaceful one and it relaxes. Visualizing a calm place, even when it isn't real, can help you release anxiety.

身体会照着头脑告诉它的去反应：想象紧张的场面，它就绷紧；想象平静的场面，它就放松。想象一个安宁的地方——哪怕不是真的——也能帮你放下焦虑。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'My body can't think for itself, so it believes anything I imagine.' That's a simplified way of putting it. What research does support is that mental images stir up feelings strongly, in both directions. That is useful to know: the same power that makes a peaceful image calming makes an imagined disaster scary, so choosing which picture to rehearse matters. Another misreading: visualization is pretending problems don't exist. It is a short break that lets you come back steadier.

「身体不会自己思考，所以我想象什么它都信。」这是一种简化的说法。研究真正支持的是：脑海里的画面会强烈地牵动感受，而且是双向的。知道这一点很有用：让安宁画面带来平静的那股力量，也会让想象中的灾难变得吓人，所以选择预演哪一幅画面很重要。另一种误读是把想象当作假装问题不存在。它是一次短暂的休息，让你回来时更稳。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Researchers have found that mental pictures stir up emotion more strongly than the same idea put into words (Holmes & Mathews, 2010, a review that includes new experiments). That cuts both ways: it helps explain why picturing a peaceful place can calm you, and also why vivid imagined disasters can feel so frightening. The review is about how imagery works, not a trial showing that peaceful-place visualization lowers anxiety in teens.

研究者发现，脑海里的画面比同一个意思的文字说法更能牵动情绪（Holmes 与 Mathews，2010，一篇含有新实验的综述）。这一点是双向的：它有助于解释为什么想象一个安宁的地方能让人平静，也解释了为什么生动想象出来的灾难会那么吓人。这篇综述讲的是想象如何起作用，并不是一项证明「想象安宁之地」能降低青少年焦虑的试验。

  • Holmes EA, Mathews A. Mental imagery in emotion and emotional disorders. Clin Psychol Rev 2010;30(3):349–362. doi:10.1016/j.cpr.2010.01.001 link链接
