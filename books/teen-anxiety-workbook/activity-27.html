Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Mindfulness means keeping your attention on what is happening right now, without judging it; practiced often, it leaves anxiety less room to grow.正念就是把注意力放在此时此刻正在发生的事上，而且不评判；常常练习，焦虑就少了生长的空间。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Mindfulness is keeping your focus on the present moment, accepting it without judgment; practiced regularly it keeps anxiety lower, and used in a high-anxiety moment it brings you back to peace.正念就是把专注留在当下，接纳它而不评判；规律地练能让焦虑保持在较低水平，焦虑很高时用它能把你带回平静。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Mindfulness is keeping your focus on the present moment, accepting it without judgment; practiced regularly it keeps anxiety lower, and used in a high-anxiety moment it brings you back to peace.

正念就是把专注留在当下，接纳它而不评判；规律地练能让焦虑保持在较低水平，焦虑很高时用它能把你带回平静。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Mindfulness means emptying your mind.' It doesn't; it means noticing where your mind is and gently bringing it back, again and again. Another misreading is that mindfulness is a quick cure: the research with young people shows real but modest benefits, and some people find sitting still with their thoughts uncomfortable at first. If it ever makes you feel much worse, stop and talk with someone you trust.

「正念就是把脑子清空。」不是；它是留意心在哪里，再温和地把它带回来，一次又一次。另一种误读是把正念当成速效药：针对年轻人的研究显示它有真实但不大的益处，而且有些人一开始会觉得和自己的念头静坐很不舒服。如果它让你感觉糟糕很多，就停下来，和信任的人聊聊。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

The research on mindfulness for young people is encouraging but modest. A meta-analysis of 33 randomized trials with children and teens found small benefits; when the comparison group did some other active activity, the benefit for anxiety and stress was small (Dunning et al., 2019). And a very large UK trial with more than 8,000 students aged 11 to 14 found that a ten-lesson school mindfulness course was no better than usual school teaching at one year (Kuyken et al., 2022). Mindfulness can help some people, especially those who choose to practice, but a short class for everyone is not a guaranteed fix.

关于正念对年轻人的研究，结果令人鼓舞但幅度有限。一篇汇总 33 项儿童与青少年随机试验的元分析发现益处较小；当对照组也在做别的积极活动时，对焦虑与压力的益处很小（Dunning 等，2019）。英国一项有 8,000 多名 11 至 14 岁学生参加的大型试验发现，学校里十节课的正念课程，一年后的效果并不比学校平常的教学更好（Kuyken 等，2022）。正念能帮到一些人，尤其是自己愿意练的人；但给所有人上一门短课，不能保证有效。

How it connects和别处怎么连

Grounding (28) is mindfulness in quick, concrete form, and meditation (32) is its longer, daily version. The future-focused worry it interrupts is the subject of Activity 8 (worrying is worthless), and Activity 36 brings back the idea of the joy of being present.

着地技巧（28）是正念的快速、具体版，冥想（32）是它更长的日常版。它所打断的「面向未来的担心」，正是第 8 个练习（担心没有用）的主题；第 36 个练习还会再提到「活在当下的乐趣」。
