Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

When your things are sorted into categories, you can find what you need quickly, and that removes a lot of the rushing and scrambling that feeds anxiety.东西分好类，要用时很快就能找到；这能去掉很多慌忙翻找的时刻，而这些时刻正是焦虑的养料。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Keeping your surroundings in order helps you find what you need more quickly and easily, which keeps your anxiety level down.让身边环境保持有序，能帮你更快更容易找到要用的东西，从而让焦虑保持在较低水平。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Keeping your surroundings in order helps you find what you need more quickly and easily, which keeps your anxiety level down.

让身边环境保持有序，能帮你更快更容易找到要用的东西，从而让焦虑保持在较低水平。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Being organized means everything has to be perfectly neat.' It only needs to be findable. A simple system you'll keep up beats a beautiful one you'll abandon. Another misreading: 'I'm just a messy person.' Organizing is a skill with steps, which is why Eric could learn it from his sister.

「有条理就是一切都得整整齐齐。」只要找得到就行。一个你坚持得了的简单办法，胜过一个你会放弃的漂亮系统。另一种误读是「我天生就是个乱的人」。整理是一项有步骤的本领，所以埃里克才能跟姐姐学会。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

There is little research that tests whether organizing your belongings lowers anxiety. One small study of 60 working couples found that wives who described their homes using more words about clutter and unfinished projects had a daily stress-hormone (cortisol) pattern linked with poorer health, and their mood worsened over the day; the same link was not reported for husbands (Saxbe & Repetti, 2010). That study is about adults and shows a link, not proof that tidying helps. Treat the book's advice as practical common sense and see whether it lowers your own rushing and searching.

检验「整理东西能不能降低焦虑」的研究很少。一项针对 60 对双职工夫妻的小研究发现，描述自己家时用了更多「杂乱」「没弄完」字眼的妻子，每日压力激素（皮质醇）的变化曲线属于与健康较差相关的那一种，情绪也在一天中变差；丈夫身上没有报告同样的关联（Saxbe 与 Repetti，2010）。这项研究的对象是成年人，显示的是关联，不能证明整理有用。把书里的建议当作实用的常识，看看它是否减少了你自己慌忙翻找的时候。

  • Saxbe DE, Repetti R. No place like home: home tours correlate with daily patterns of mood and cortisol. Pers Soc Psychol Bull 2010;36(1):71–81. doi:10.1177/0146167209352864 link链接

It applies the small-steps idea from Activity 35 to your space, and it pairs with Activity 38, which organizes your time instead of your things. Both help with the last-minute rush that also comes up in test anxiety (43).
