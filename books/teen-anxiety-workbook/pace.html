It's OK not to finish读不完也没关系

This book is a toolbox, not a test. You don't have to read it in order, and you don't have to read all of it. One small block today is plenty.这本书是工具箱，不是考试。不必按顺序读，也不必全读完。今天做一小块，就很好。

Pick a rhythm挑一个节奏

Rhythm节奏What a day looks like一天做什么Whole book全书大约
Gentle慢慢来One activity a day, 15–20 minutes, weekdays only每天一个练习，15–20 分钟，只在工作日about 9–10 weeks大约 9–10 周
Light轻量Just the map and cards of one activity, about 10 minutes只看一个练习的导图和卡片，大约 10 分钟as long as you like多久都行
Pick and choose挑着读Only the activities that fit what's hard right now. Good first picks: 1, 3, 28, 30, 34只读和眼下难处有关的。适合先读的：1、3、28、30、34a few days几天

Today, one block is great. Missed a week? Start again from wherever you like — nothing is lost.今天做一块就很好。一周没碰？从任何地方重新开始都行——什么也没丢。

Stuck? Three steps卡住了？三步

1Change the form. Listen, or read only the one-sentence version.换个形式。听一听，或者只读一句话版本。
2Look at the picture. The mind map or the figure often says it faster.看图。导图或那张图常常说得更快。
3Skip it. The next activity doesn't depend on this one. You can come back.跳过。下一个练习不依赖这一个，以后可以回来。
