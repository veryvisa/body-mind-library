Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Calm is a natural state you already have; anxious thoughts cover it up like layers, and you can uncover it rather than having to create it.平静是你本来就有的状态；焦虑的念头像一层层东西把它盖住，你要做的是把它找回来，而不是从头造一个。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

People often think peace must be found outside or built with great effort. In fact calm is a natural state already inside us, hidden by the stress we take in and focus on; we uncover it by peeling away anxious layers.人们常以为平静得到外面去找，或者费很大力气造出来。其实平静是我们本来就有的自然状态，只是被我们吸收和盯着的压力盖住了；剥掉焦虑的层层覆盖，它就露出来。

What the book says书里怎么说

People often think peace must be found outside or built with great effort. In fact calm is a natural state already inside us, hidden by the stress we take in and focus on; we uncover it by peeling away anxious layers.

人们常以为平静得到外面去找，或者费很大力气造出来。其实平静是我们本来就有的自然状态，只是被我们吸收和盯着的压力盖住了；剥掉焦虑的层层覆盖，它就露出来。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'If I feel anxious, my calm is gone and I have to start over.' The chapter's point is that a stressful thought sits on top of calm, it doesn't replace it. Another misreading: 'so I should just be calm' — the chapter isn't asking you to force calm, only to notice what is covering it.

「我一焦虑，平静就没了，得从头再来。」这一章的意思是，有压力的念头压在平静上面，并没有取代它。另一种误读是「那我就应该平静」——这一章并不要求你硬把自己变平静，只是让你看见是什么盖住了它。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

This chapter is a perspective rather than a lab finding, best read as a helpful image. It sits close to Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), an approach whose model and outcome research has been reviewed with promising results (Hayes et al., 2006) and which teaches people to notice thoughts and feelings without being ruled by them. The statue line is widely attributed to Michelangelo, but quotation collections list it as disputed, with no documented source from Michelangelo himself, so it is safer to treat it as a sculptor's saying.

这一章讲的是一种看法，不是实验结论，当作一个有用的意象来读最合适。它和接纳承诺疗法（ACT）很接近：这种疗法的模型与疗效研究经综述认为前景良好（Hayes 等，2006），它教人察觉自己的念头和感受，而不被它们牵着走。雕像那句话常被归于米开朗基罗，但名言辑录把它列为「存疑」，找不到出自他本人的文献，稳妥的说法是「一位雕刻家的话」。

  • Hayes SC, Luoma JB, Bond FW, Masuda A, Lillis J. Acceptance and commitment therapy: model, processes and outcomes. Behav Res Ther 2006;44(1):1–25. doi:10.1016/j.brat.2005.06.006 link链接
  • Wikiquote, 'Michelangelo' — Disputed: 'I saw the angel in the marble and carved until I set him free' (earliest cited printings 1916, 1928; an 1884 version credits only 'a sculptor'). link链接

How it connects和别处怎么连

This chapter bridges Part 1 and Part 2: Activity 7 (the power of attitude) picks up the idea that what you focus on shapes how you feel. It also prepares the ground for mindfulness (27) and meditation (32), which are practical ways of 'uncovering' calm.

这一章连接第一、二部分：第 7 个练习（态度的力量）接着讲「你盯着什么，就会有什么感受」。它也为正念（27）和冥想（32）打底——那两个练习就是「把平静找回来」的具体做法。
