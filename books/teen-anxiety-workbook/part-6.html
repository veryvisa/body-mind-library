Part 6 · Hard moments and the road ahead第 6 部分 · 难关时刻与往后

Activities 40–46练习 40–46 · The book itself has no parts; this six-part grouping is ours, to give a map of the 46 activities.原书不分部分；这六部分是我们为了给 46 个练习画一张地图而做的分组。

40 · Unexpressed Anger没说出口的愤怒
Anger you ignore or swallow doesn't vanish and can turn up later as anxiety, so noticing your anger and expressing it safely can lower anxiety too.被忽略或吞下去的愤怒不会消失，过后可能变成焦虑冒出来；所以察觉自己的愤怒、用安全的方式表达，也能让焦虑降下来。
41 · Managing Panic应对惊恐
A panic attack is a short burst of very intense anxiety that feels frightening but is not dangerous, and reminding yourself of that while you slow your body down helps it pass.惊恐发作是一阵短暂而极强的焦虑，感觉很吓人，但并不危险；一边提醒自己这一点，一边让身体慢下来，它就会过去。
42 · Managing Fears and Phobias应对恐惧与恐惧症
Avoiding what you fear keeps the fear strong, while facing it in small, planned steps, starting with the easiest and staying calm at each one, can shrink it.躲开害怕的东西，恐惧就一直强大；按计划一小步一小步地面对它——从最容易的开始，每一步都保持平静——就能把它缩小。
43 · Test Anxiety考试焦虑
Test anxiety is learned, which means it can be unlearned: a plan for the days before the test and a few tools for the test itself make you feel far less helpless.考试焦虑是学来的，也就是说可以「改掉」：考前几天有一个计划，考场上有几样工具，人就不会那么无助。
44 · Managing Stressful Public Events面对令人不安的公共事件
When frightening public events happen, strong reactions are normal, and small, concrete steps such as talking, limiting news, keeping routines, and having a plan help you feel safer again.可怕的公共事件发生时，反应强烈是正常的；一些具体的小步骤——说出来、少看新闻、保持日常作息、准备好计划——能帮你重新感到安全。
45 · Help Yourself by Helping Others帮别人，也帮了自己
Helping other people takes your mind off your own worries and gives you a sense of connection and purpose, which can lift your mood and ease anxiety.帮助别人能让你暂时放下自己的担心，还能带来与人相连、做有意义之事的感觉，从而让心情变好、焦虑减轻。
46 · Future Challenges往后的挑战
The goal is managing anxiety, not getting rid of it forever; new challenges will keep coming, and feeling anxious again means you're human, not that you've failed.目标是管理焦虑，而不是永远消灭它；新的挑战会一直到来，再次感到焦虑说明你是个普通人，不说明你失败了。
