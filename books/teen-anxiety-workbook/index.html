The book in one minute一分钟读懂

  1. First you get to know the alarm: what anxiety is, how the fight-flight-freeze response works, and how it shows up in your own body, mind and mood (Activities 1–6).先认识这个警报：焦虑是什么，「战斗—逃跑—僵住」反应怎么运作，它在你自己的身体、头脑和情绪里是什么样子（练习 1–6）。
  2. Then the biggest part: noticing the thoughts that feed anxiety — all-or-nothing, 'should', worst-case — and swapping in calmer, truer ones (7–20).接着是最大的一部分：察觉那些喂大焦虑的念头——非黑即白、「应该」、往最坏处想——换成更平静、也更真实的想法（7–20）。
  3. Next, letting it out and looking after your body: talking, writing, food, movement (21–24); and calming tools you can use anywhere — relaxation, breathing, grounding, mindfulness (25–33).然后是说出来、写出来、照顾身体：倾诉、写作、饮食、运动（21–24）；以及随时随地能用的平静工具——放松、呼吸、着陆、正念（25–33）。
  4. Finally, putting days in order — problem solving, small steps, tech breaks, time (34–39) — and handling the hard moments: anger, panic, fears, tests, big events, and the road ahead (40–46).最后，把日子理顺——解决问题、拆小步骤、放下手机、安排时间（34–39）——再应对难关：愤怒、惊恐、害怕、考试、大场合，以及往后的路（40–46）。
