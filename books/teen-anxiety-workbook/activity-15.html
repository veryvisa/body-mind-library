Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Since no one can be perfect, aiming for perfection guarantees falling short and feeling anxious; aiming for your best keeps high standards without the constant alarm.既然没有人能完美，以完美为目标就注定会落空、会焦虑；以「尽力就好」为目标，标准照样高，警报却不再一直响。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Perfection is impossible, so trying to be perfect means constantly falling short and feeling anxious. Every pencil has an eraser because everyone makes mistakes. Perfectionists also tend to get less done, because they struggle to finish tasks and make decisions.完美不可能做到，所以追求完美就意味着总在落空、总在焦虑。每支铅笔都带橡皮，因为人人都会出错。完美主义者往往还做得更少，因为他们很难把事情收尾、很难下决定。

Michael was good at school, sports, and music. Why was he anxious all the time?迈克尔学习、运动、音乐都不错，为什么还一直焦虑？

Because his goal was perfection, and every ordinary mistake, a strikeout or not being number one, counted as falling short. The gap never closed, so the anxiety never stopped.因为他的目标是完美，每一个寻常的失误——一次三振、没考第一——都算没达标。这个差距永远补不上，焦虑也就停不下来。

What does the pencil eraser show?铅笔上的橡皮说明了什么？

What did Michael change, and what happened?迈克尔改了什么？结果怎样？
Answer in your head, then tap to flip先在心里答，再点一下翻面
He changed his goal from being perfect to doing the best he could. His constant anxiety eased, his headaches went away, and he started enjoying his activities again.他把目标从「完美」改成「尽我所能」。一直挂着的焦虑缓下来，头疼没了，他又开始享受原来做的那些事。
Activity 15 · Perfectionism练习 15 · 完美主义
Does dropping perfectionism mean caring less about doing well?放下完美主义，是不是就不在乎做得好不好了？
Answer in your head, then tap to flip先在心里答，再点一下翻面
No. You can still care and work hard. The difference is how you treat mistakes: as normal information, not as proof that you've failed.不是。你照样可以在乎、可以努力。区别在于怎么看待错误：把它当成正常的信息，而不是你失败了的证据。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Perfection is impossible, so trying to be perfect means constantly falling short and feeling anxious. Every pencil has an eraser because everyone makes mistakes. Perfectionists also tend to get less done, because they struggle to finish tasks and make decisions.

完美不可能做到，所以追求完美就意味着总在落空、总在焦虑。每支铅笔都带橡皮，因为人人都会出错。完美主义者往往还做得更少，因为他们很难把事情收尾、很难下决定。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Without perfectionism I'd stop trying.' Michael didn't stop trying; he kept his activities and enjoyed them more. A related misreading is that every high achiever is a perfectionist. Wanting to do well and fearing any mistake are different things, and it is mostly the fear that brings the anxiety.

「没了完美主义，我就不努力了。」迈克尔并没有不努力，他照样做那些事，而且更享受了。另一个相关的误读是：成绩好的人都是完美主义者。想做好，和害怕任何差错，是两回事；带来焦虑的主要是后者。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Perfectionism seems to be on the rise. A large analysis of 164 samples (41,641 college students in the US, Canada, and the UK, 1989–2016) found that all three kinds it measured increased steadily over time: demanding perfection of yourself, of others, and feeling that others demand it of you (Curran & Hill, 2019). The data are from college students, not teens, and describe trends across groups, not any one person.

完美主义似乎在上升。一项涵盖 164 个样本（1989–2016 年美国、加拿大、英国的 41,641 名大学生）的大型分析发现，它测量的三种完美主义都随时间稳步上升：对自己要求完美、对别人要求完美，以及觉得别人要求自己完美（Curran 与 Hill，2019）。数据来自大学生而非青少年，描述的是群体趋势，不是某一个人。

  • Curran T, Hill AP. Perfectionism is increasing over time: a meta-analysis of birth cohort differences from 1989 to 2016. Psychol Bull 2019;145(4):410–429. doi:10.1037/bul0000138 link链接

How it connects和别处怎么连

Perfectionism is all-or-nothing thinking (9) aimed at your own performance, and it is often fed by should statements (11). Activity 16 (pressure to succeed) looks at the outside pressure that can grow it.

完美主义是对准自己表现的非黑即白（9），常由「应该」句式（11）喂大。第 16 个练习（成功压力）会看那些让它长大的外部压力。
