Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Calming skills work best when you practice them on ordinary days (prevention), so they are ready to use when anxiety spikes (intervention).平静的本领要在平常日子里练（预防），焦虑冲高时才拿得出来（干预）。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

There are two times to work on anxiety: before (prevention, regular practice to keep the baseline low) and during (intervention, a technique to calm the moment). Many techniques work for both, and practicing them as prevention makes them stronger as intervention.处理焦虑有两个时机：之前（预防，规律练习，压低基线）和当下（干预，用一个方法平复这一刻）。很多方法两头都能用，平时当预防练，当干预时就更有力。

What is the difference between prevention and intervention?预防和干预有什么区别？
Answer in your head, then tap to flip先在心里答，再点一下翻面
Prevention is what you do regularly before you feel anxious, to keep your everyday level low. Intervention is what you do when anxiety is rising, to calm that moment.预防是在焦虑之前、平常规律地做，让日常焦虑保持在低水平；干预是在焦虑升起时做，用来平复那一刻。

Why practice a calming technique on a day you feel fine?明明今天心情不错，为什么还要练放松？

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

There are two times to work on anxiety: before (prevention, regular practice to keep the baseline low) and during (intervention, a technique to calm the moment). Many techniques work for both, and practicing them as prevention makes them stronger as intervention.

处理焦虑有两个时机：之前（预防，规律练习，压低基线）和当下（干预，用一个方法平复这一刻）。很多方法两头都能用，平时当预防练，当干预时就更有力。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Relaxation is only for when I'm stressed.' If you only try a technique for the first time in the middle of a hard moment, it often feels useless — not because it doesn't work, but because it hasn't been practiced.

「放松是紧张的时候才用的。」如果第一次尝试某个方法就是在最难受的时候，往往会觉得没用——不是方法不行，而是还没练过。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Applied relaxation, described by psychologist Lars-Göran Öst as a coping technique, teaches relaxation as a skill and then puts it to use against anxiety (Öst, 1987). It is now counted as an empirically supported treatment for generalized anxiety disorder; in one trial of 16 sessions, most clients (about 61% to 79% at different time points) showed clinically significant change (Hayes-Skelton, Roemer & Orsillo, 2013). That fits the book's idea: practice first, so the skill is there when it counts.

应用放松训练由心理学家拉斯-约兰·厄斯特作为一种应对技术提出：先把放松当作本领学会，再用它对付焦虑（Öst，1987）。如今它被视为广泛性焦虑症的有实证支持的疗法；在一项 16 次治疗的试验中，大多数来访者（不同时间点约 61% 至 79%）出现了有临床意义的改变（Hayes-Skelton、Roemer 与 Orsillo，2013）。这和书里的想法一致：先练，到要紧时本领才在。

  • Öst LG. Applied relaxation: description of a coping technique and review of controlled studies. Behav Res Ther 1987;25(5):397–409. doi:10.1016/0005-7967(87)90017-9 link链接
  • Hayes-Skelton SA, Roemer L, Orsillo SM. A randomized clinical trial comparing an acceptance-based behavior therapy to applied relaxation for generalized anxiety disorder. J Consult Clin Psychol 2013;81(5):761–773. doi:10.1037/a0032871 link链接

How it connects和别处怎么连

This is the instruction manual for the rest of the book: relaxation (25), centering (26), mindfulness (27), breath (30), and meditation (32) are mostly prevention-and-intervention tools. The same logic underlies habit-building books — a small daily routine beats a big rescue effort.

这是全书其余部分的「使用说明」：放松（25）、定心（26）、正念（27）、呼吸（30）、冥想（32）大多是两头都能用的工具。养成习惯类的书讲的也是这个道理——每天一点小功夫，胜过临时大抢救。
