Part 3 · Letting it out and caring for your body第 3 部分 · 说出来、写出来、照顾身体

Activities 21–24练习 21–24 · The book itself has no parts; this six-part grouping is ours, to give a map of the 46 activities.原书不分部分；这六部分是我们为了给 46 个练习画一张地图而做的分组。

21 · Talking It Out说出来
Anxiety you keep bottled up tends to grow, and saying it out loud to someone you trust, even in one short sentence, lets some of the pressure out.憋在心里的焦虑往往越憋越大；对一个你信任的人把它说出来，哪怕只是一句短话，也能放掉一部分压力。
22 · Writing It Out写出来
Writing your anxious feelings down, with no rules about spelling or neatness, is a private way to get them out of your head.把焦虑的感受写下来，不管拼写、不管工整，是把它们从脑子里拿出来的一种私人办法。
23 · Eating and Anxiety饮食与焦虑
Food and drink can nudge how anxious you feel, and a week of simply noticing can show what matters for your body, without strict rules.饮食会轻轻影响你有多焦虑；花一周只是留意一下，就能看出哪些对你的身体有影响，不需要什么严格的规定。
24 · More Exercise, Less Anxiety多动一动，少一点焦虑
Moving your body, in almost any way you enjoy, can bring anxiety down in the moment and, done regularly, keep it lower from day to day.活动身体——几乎任何你喜欢的方式——都能当场把焦虑降下来；规律地做，还能让平日的焦虑保持在较低水平。
