Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Anger you ignore or swallow doesn't vanish and can turn up later as anxiety, so noticing your anger and expressing it safely can lower anxiety too.被忽略或吞下去的愤怒不会消失，过后可能变成焦虑冒出来；所以察觉自己的愤怒、用安全的方式表达，也能让焦虑降下来。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Anger stays with you until it is expressed in some way; anger that goes unrecognized or unexpressed doesn't disappear and may later surface as anxiety, so looking at your own experience of anger can help relieve anxiety. Safe ways include words, exercise, writing, drawing, and music.愤怒会一直跟着你，直到以某种方式表达出来；没被察觉或没被表达的愤怒不会消失，之后可能以焦虑的样子冒出来；所以审视自己和愤怒的经历，有助于缓解焦虑。安全的方式包括说话、运动、写、画和音乐。

What the book says书里怎么说

Anger stays with you until it is expressed in some way; anger that goes unrecognized or unexpressed doesn't disappear and may later surface as anxiety, so looking at your own experience of anger can help relieve anxiety. Safe ways include words, exercise, writing, drawing, and music.

愤怒会一直跟着你，直到以某种方式表达出来；没被察觉或没被表达的愤怒不会消失，之后可能以焦虑的样子冒出来；所以审视自己和愤怒的经历，有助于缓解焦虑。安全的方式包括说话、运动、写、画和音乐。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Getting anger out of my system means venting it hard.' The book says anger fades once it is expressed, but research on venting points the other way: revving yourself up with hitting or shouting tends to keep anger going, and calming down first works better. The useful part of the book's advice is expressing anger safely and in words. Another misreading: 'Feeling angry means I'm a bad person.' Anger is a normal signal that something matters to you; what counts is what you do with it. If anger at home ever feels unsafe, tell a trusted adult.

「把愤怒排出体外，就是要使劲发泄。」书里说愤怒一旦表达就会消散，但关于发泄的研究指向另一边：靠打、喊让自己更激动，往往让愤怒延续下去，先平静下来效果更好。书中建议真正有用的部分，是安全地、用语言表达愤怒。另一种误读是「生气说明我是个坏人」。愤怒是一个正常的信号，说明某件事对你很重要；关键在于你拿它怎么办。如果家里的愤怒让你感到不安全，请告诉一个信任的大人。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Research gives an important twist here. In an experiment with 600 college students, people who hit a punching bag while thinking about the person who angered them ended up angrier and more aggressive than people who did nothing at all (Bushman, 2002). A 2024 meta-analysis of 154 studies found that activities that calm the body down, such as slow breathing, mindfulness, and meditation, reduced anger and aggression, while activities that rev the body up, such as hitting a bag, jogging, or cycling, were on average not effective (Kjærvik & Bushman, 2024). So 'expressing' anger works best as noticing it, naming it, and calming down enough to say or do something useful, not as blowing off steam. The book's suggestions of writing, talking, and drawing fit that better than physical venting does.

研究在这里给出了一个重要的转折。一项有 600 名大学生参加的实验中，一边打沙袋、一边想着惹自己生气的人，结果比什么都不做的人更生气、更有攻击性（Bushman，2002）。2024 年一篇汇总 154 项研究的元分析发现，让身体平静下来的活动——比如慢呼吸、正念、冥想——能减少愤怒与攻击，而让身体更兴奋的活动——比如打沙袋、慢跑、骑车——平均来说没有效果（Kjærvik 与 Bushman，2024）。所以「表达」愤怒，最好的做法是察觉它、说出它的名字、先平静到能说或做点有用的事，而不是「发泄掉」。书里提到的写、说、画，比身体上的发泄更符合这一点。

  • Kjærvik SL, Bushman BJ. A meta-analytic review of anger management activities that increase or decrease arousal: what fuels or douses rage? Clin Psychol Rev 2024;109:102414. doi:10.1016/j.cpr.2024.102414 link链接

How it connects和别处怎么连

Talking it out (21) and writing it out (22) are the safest outlets the book offers. Breath (30) and progressive relaxation (25) are the calming activities that research favors for anger, while exercise (24) is better used for general anxiety than for blowing off steam in the moment.

说出来（21）和写出来（22）是书里最安全的出口。呼吸（30）和渐进式放松（25）正是研究认为对愤怒更有效的平静活动；运动（24）则更适合用来应对日常焦虑，而不是在气头上「出气」。
