Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Too many activities in too little time leads to rushing and running late, both of which raise anxiety, so balancing commitments with real down time keeps it lower.时间太少、事情太多，人就会慌忙、迟到，这两样都会让焦虑升高；把各种安排和真正的休息时间平衡好，焦虑就能低一些。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Cramming too many activities into too little time leaves you overwhelmed and late, both of which raise anxiety; learning to balance and manage your time helps keep it down.把太多活动塞进太少的时间，会让人招架不住、频频迟到，这两样都会让焦虑升高；学会平衡和安排时间，有助于把它压下来。

What the book says书里怎么说

Cramming too many activities into too little time leaves you overwhelmed and late, both of which raise anxiety; learning to balance and manage your time helps keep it down.

把太多活动塞进太少的时间，会让人招架不住、频频迟到，这两样都会让焦虑升高；学会平衡和安排时间，有助于把它压下来。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Time management means fitting more in.' The book's first step is the opposite: protect down time, and commit only to what fits. Another misreading: 'Saying no or moving plans will make people upset.' In the story, moving the movie to another night worked fine for both friends. If your schedule is overloaded by things you can't drop, that is worth talking through with a parent, teacher, or counselor.

「时间管理就是塞进更多事。」书里的第一步恰恰相反：守住休息时间，只答应放得下的事。另一种误读是「拒绝或改期会让别人不高兴」。故事里，把电影改到另一个晚上，两个朋友都觉得没问题。如果让你日程超载的是一些推不掉的事，值得和家长、老师或辅导员一起理一理。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

A meta-analysis of time management research found it moderately linked with better well-being and less distress, and with better academic and job performance; the benefit to well-being, especially life satisfaction, looked larger than the benefit to performance (Aeon, Faber & Panaccio, 2021). Most of the studies measured links rather than testing training, and most were with working adults and college students. That fits the book's emphasis on balance and down time, not just squeezing in more.

一篇关于时间管理的元分析发现，它与更好的幸福感、更少的苦恼有中等程度的关联，也与更好的学业和工作表现有关；对幸福感（尤其是生活满意度）的益处，看起来比对表现的益处更大（Aeon、Faber 与 Panaccio，2021）。其中多数研究测的是关联而不是训练效果，对象多是在职成年人和大学生。这和书里强调平衡与休息时间、而不只是塞进更多事情的思路一致。

  • Aeon B, Faber A, Panaccio A. Does time management work? A meta-analysis. PLoS One 2021;16(1):e0245066. doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0245066 link链接
