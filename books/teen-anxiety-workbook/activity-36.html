Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Technology helps in many ways, but some patterns of use can drive anxiety up; noticing how your own use makes you feel lets you choose when and how to plug in.科技在很多方面帮了我们，但某些使用方式会把焦虑推高；留意自己的使用让你有什么感受，就能自己决定什么时候、怎样接上线。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Technology brings many benefits, but used in unhealthy ways it can send anxiety soaring, through comparison, cyberbullying, fear of missing out, fear of being without a phone, and isolation; recognizing how it affects you lets you make smart choices.

科技带来很多好处，但用得不健康时会让焦虑飙升：比较、网络欺凌、害怕错过、离了手机就慌、与人隔绝。认清它怎样影响你，你就能做出明智的选择。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'All screen time is bad for me.' The research on the average teen finds only a small link between screen use and well-being, and phones also connect people. What matters more is how you use them and how you feel afterward. The book's comparison of scrolling to a chemical addiction is stronger than the evidence supports, so read it as a warning, not a diagnosis. And if something online frightens or threatens you, that is not something to handle alone.

「所有屏幕时间都对我有害。」针对普通青少年的研究发现，屏幕使用和幸福感之间只有很小的关联，而且手机也把人连在一起。更重要的是怎么用、用完感觉如何。书里把刷手机比作化学成瘾，说得比证据更重，读作提醒就好，不是诊断。如果网上有什么东西吓到你、威胁你，不要一个人扛。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

The research is more mixed than the headlines. Across three large surveys of more than 350,000 adolescents, digital technology use had a negative but small link with well-being, explaining at most 0.4% of the differences between teens (Orben & Przybylski, 2019). In an experiment with 143 college students, limiting three social media apps to 10 minutes each per day for three weeks reduced loneliness and depression; anxiety went down in both the limited group and the comparison group, which suggests that simply tracking your use may help (Hunt et al., 2018). So the average harm of screen time is small, but how you use it, and how it makes you feel, can matter a lot for an individual.

研究结果比新闻标题复杂。三项涵盖 35 万多名青少年的大型调查显示，数字科技的使用与幸福感之间有负向但很小的关联，最多只能解释青少年之间 0.4% 的差别（Orben 与 Przybylski，2019）。一项有 143 名大学生参加的实验中，把三个社交应用每天各限在 10 分钟、坚持三周，孤独感和抑郁都减少了；焦虑则在限用组和对照组都下降了，提示单是记录自己的使用情况可能就有帮助（Hunt 等，2018）。所以屏幕时间的平均伤害不大，但对某一个人来说，怎么用、用完感觉如何，可能关系很大。

  • Hunt MG, Marx R, Lipson C, Young J. No more FOMO: limiting social media decreases loneliness and depression. J Soc Clin Psychol 2018;37(10):751–768. doi:10.1521/jscp.2018.37.10.751 link链接
