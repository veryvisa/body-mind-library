Part 5 · Putting daily life in order第 5 部分 · 把日子理顺

Activities 34–39练习 34–39 · The book itself has no parts; this six-part grouping is ours, to give a map of the 46 activities.原书不分部分；这六部分是我们为了给 46 个练习画一张地图而做的分组。

34 · Problem Solving解决问题
When a situation feels overwhelming, 'stop, breathe, think' turns panic into a plan: pause, calm your body, then work out what to do first, second, and third.局面让人招架不住时，「停、呼吸、想」能把慌乱变成计划：先停下，让身体平静，再想清楚第一步、第二步、第三步做什么。
35 · It's a Cinch by the Inch一寸一寸就容易
Looking at a big job all at once breeds anxiety; breaking it into small steps and doing just the next one makes it manageable.一下子盯着整件大事，焦虑就会冒出来；把它拆成小步骤，只做眼前这一步，事情就应付得来了。
36 · Take a Tech Break给科技放个假
Technology helps in many ways, but some patterns of use can drive anxiety up; noticing how your own use makes you feel lets you choose when and how to plug in.科技在很多方面帮了我们，但某些使用方式会把焦虑推高；留意自己的使用让你有什么感受，就能自己决定什么时候、怎样接上线。
37 · Staying Organized保持有条理
When your things are sorted into categories, you can find what you need quickly, and that removes a lot of the rushing and scrambling that feeds anxiety.东西分好类，要用时很快就能找到；这能去掉很多慌忙翻找的时刻，而这些时刻正是焦虑的养料。
38 · Balancing and Managing Time平衡与安排时间
Too many activities in too little time leads to rushing and running late, both of which raise anxiety, so balancing commitments with real down time keeps it lower.时间太少、事情太多，人就会慌忙、迟到，这两样都会让焦虑升高；把各种安排和真正的休息时间平衡好，焦虑就能低一些。
39 · Separating Yourself from Other People's Problems把别人的问题留给别人
You can care about someone's problem without taking it on as your own; holding their worry doesn't help them and can wear you out.你可以关心别人的问题，而不把它变成自己的；替别人背着担心帮不了他们，反而会把你拖累。
