Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

The pressure to get everything right eases when you widen what 'success' means, remember that most paths wind, and keep your attention on today.当你把「成功」的意思放宽、记得大多数人生路都是弯的、并把注意力放在今天，「必须样样做对」的压力就会松下来。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Teens often feel pressure to succeed, with grades, adult expectations, awards, and life choices. You can ease it by redefining success to include enjoying each day and feeling peaceful, by recognizing that life is a winding path, and by focusing on the present moment.青少年常常感到成功的压力：成绩、大人的期待、奖项、人生选择。你可以这样松绑：把成功重新定义成也包括享受每一天、内心平静；认识到人生是一条弯路；把注意力放回当下。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Teens often feel pressure to succeed, with grades, adult expectations, awards, and life choices. You can ease it by redefining success to include enjoying each day and feeling peaceful, by recognizing that life is a winding path, and by focusing on the present moment.

青少年常常感到成功的压力：成绩、大人的期待、奖项、人生选择。你可以这样松绑：把成功重新定义成也包括享受每一天、内心平静；认识到人生是一条弯路；把注意力放回当下。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Easing the pressure means giving up on ambition.' The chapter isn't against goals; it is against letting one narrow scoreboard decide your worth and your sleep. You can still aim for a good school and also count friendship, health, and calm as part of doing well. (The book mentions a story about Walt Disney being fired for lacking imagination; it is widely repeated, but we couldn't confirm the details, so we leave it out.)

「减压就是放弃上进心。」这一章并不反对目标，它反对的是让一张窄窄的记分牌决定你的价值和你的睡眠。你照样可以想上好学校，同时也把友谊、健康和平静算进「过得好」里。（书里提到华特·迪士尼因「缺乏想象力」被解雇的故事，流传很广，但我们没能核实细节，这里不用。）

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Researchers now take achievement pressure seriously. A review of students in high-achieving schools notes that excessive pressure to excel has been listed among the top four high-risk factors for adolescents' well-being, alongside poverty, trauma, and discrimination (Luthar, Kumar & Zillmer, 2020). That doesn't mean ambition is harmful; the concern is pressure that crowds out sleep, friendships, and a sense of worth apart from results.

研究者如今很重视成就压力。一篇关于高成就学校学生的综述指出，「过度的出类拔萃压力」已被列为影响青少年身心健康的四大高风险因素之一，与贫困、创伤、歧视并列（Luthar、Kumar 与 Zillmer，2020）。这并不是说上进心有害；值得担心的是那种挤掉睡眠、友谊、以及「成绩之外的自我价值」的压力。
