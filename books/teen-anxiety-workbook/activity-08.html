Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Worry feels like doing something, but it uses up energy without changing what happens, and seeing that for yourself makes room for something more useful.担心让人觉得自己在做点什么，其实它耗掉力气，却改变不了结果；亲眼看清这一点，就能腾出位置去做更有用的事。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Worry is a common response to anxiety, but it never improves the outcome and it drains you emotionally and physically, which leaves less energy for the real situation and makes you feel even more helpless.担心是对焦虑的常见反应，但它从不改善结果，还在情绪和身体上消耗你，让你面对真实处境时更没力气，也更觉得无能为力。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Worry is a common response to anxiety, but it never improves the outcome and it drains you emotionally and physically, which leaves less energy for the real situation and makes you feel even more helpless.

担心是对焦虑的常见反应，但它从不改善结果，还在情绪和身体上消耗你，让你面对真实处境时更没力气，也更觉得无能为力。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'If I stop worrying, it means I don't care.' Caring and worrying are different. You can care a lot and still put that care into a plan, a message, or a rest, instead of into a loop of what-ifs. Another misreading: 'I should be able to just stop.' Worry often feels automatic; the next few activities give tools, so it's fine if it doesn't stop on command.

「不担心，就说明我不在乎。」在乎和担心是两回事。你可以非常在乎，同时把这份在乎放进一个计划、一条消息或一次休息里，而不是放进「万一」的循环。另一种误读是「我应该说停就停」。担心常常像是自动的；后面几个练习会给工具，做不到说停就停也没关系。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Research backs the book's point that most worries don't come true. In one study, adults with generalized anxiety disorder logged their worries and later checked them: about 91% of their worried predictions did not happen (LaFreniere & Newman, 2020; 29 participants, so treat it as a striking example, not a law). A related technique, 'worry postponement' or stimulus control, sets aside a short daily worry time and moves worry there; in a small two-week study it reduced worry, anxiety, and sleep problems more than a free-worry comparison (McGowan & Behar, 2013).

研究支持书里「大多数担心不会成真」的说法。一项研究里，患广泛性焦虑症的成年人把担心记下来、事后核对：约 91% 的担忧预测没有发生（LaFreniere 与 Newman，2020；只有 29 人，宜当作一个醒目的例子，而不是定律）。相关的办法叫「推迟担心」（刺激控制）：每天留一小段固定的担心时间，把担心挪到那时；一项为期两周的小型研究中，它比「想担心就担心」的对照组更能减少担心、焦虑和失眠（McGowan 与 Behar，2013）。

  • LaFreniere LS, Newman MG. Exposing worry's deceit: percentage of untrue worries in generalized anxiety disorder treatment. Behav Ther 2020;51(3):413–423. doi:10.1016/j.beth.2019.07.003 link链接
  • McGowan SK, Behar E. A preliminary investigation of stimulus control training for worry: effects on anxiety and insomnia. Behav Modif 2013;37(1):90–112. doi:10.1177/0145445512455661 link链接

How it connects和别处怎么连

Activity 12 (thought stopping) and 13 (what's the worst that could happen?) give ways out of the worry loop; Activity 34 turns the energy into problem-solving. The 'rate before, rate after' habit comes back often, and it is the same self-monitoring idea as the log in Activity 4.

第 12 个练习（念头叫停）和第 13 个（最坏会怎样）给出跳出担心循环的办法；第 34 个把这股劲变成解决问题。「前后各打一次分」的习惯会反复出现，和第 4 个练习的记录表是同一种自我监测的思路。
