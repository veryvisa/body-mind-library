Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Ask what the worst outcome would be, how likely it really is, and how you would cope if it happened; together the answers usually shrink a fear to its real size.问自己：最坏的结果是什么？它真有多大可能？真发生了我会怎么应对？三个答案合起来，通常能把恐惧缩回它真实的大小。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Asking 'What is the worst that could happen?' and thinking realistically about the odds and about how you would handle it lowers anxiety: usually the worst is unlikely, and even if it happened, you could cope.问「最坏会怎样」，再现实地想想概率、想想自己会怎么应对，焦虑就会降下来：最坏的情况通常不太可能发生，就算发生了，你也扛得住。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Asking 'What is the worst that could happen?' and thinking realistically about the odds and about how you would handle it lowers anxiety: usually the worst is unlikely, and even if it happened, you could cope.

问「最坏会怎样」，再现实地想想概率、想想自己会怎么应对，焦虑就会降下来：最坏的情况通常不太可能发生，就算发生了，你也扛得住。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Thinking about the worst will just make me more scared.' Imagining the worst and stopping there can do that. This activity is different because it doesn't stop at the scary picture; it adds the odds and the coping plan, and those two steps are what bring the fear down.

「想最坏的情况只会让我更害怕。」如果只想最坏、想完就停，确实可能这样。这个练习不同，它不停在吓人的画面上，而是接着算概率、想应对；正是这两步让恐惧降下来。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Checking a fear against what actually happens is backed by research. When adults with generalized anxiety disorder recorded their worries and later checked the results, about 91% of the worried predictions did not come true (LaFreniere & Newman, 2020). The study was small (29 people) and adults only, but it fits the book's two discoveries: the worst is usually unlikely, and people often cope better than they expected.

把恐惧拿去和实际结果对一对，这个做法有研究支持。患广泛性焦虑症的成年人记录自己的担心、事后核对，约 91% 的担忧预测没有成真（LaFreniere 与 Newman，2020）。这项研究样本小（29 人）且都是成年人，但和书里的两个发现吻合：最坏的情况通常不太可能发生，而人往往比自己预想的更能应对。

  • LaFreniere LS, Newman MG. Exposing worry's deceit: percentage of untrue worries in generalized anxiety disorder treatment. Behav Ther 2020;51(3):413–423. doi:10.1016/j.beth.2019.07.003 link链接
