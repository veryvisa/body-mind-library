Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Keeping a short log of when you feel anxious, what was happening, and what you were thinking reveals patterns you can't see from inside a single bad moment.把「什么时候焦虑、当时发生了什么、心里在想什么」简单记下来，就能看到身处某个难受时刻时看不见的规律。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Knowing which thoughts and situations trigger anxiety helps you prevent and manage it. A behavior log, kept for at least a week, shows patterns — often that anxiety is narrower than it feels.知道哪些想法和情境会触发焦虑，有助于预防和应对。行为记录至少记一周，就会显出规律——焦虑的范围往往比感觉上窄。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Knowing which thoughts and situations trigger anxiety helps you prevent and manage it. A behavior log, kept for at least a week, shows patterns — often that anxiety is narrower than it feels.

知道哪些想法和情境会触发焦虑，有助于预防和应对。行为记录至少记一周，就会显出规律——焦虑的范围往往比感觉上窄。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'I'm anxious all the time, so there's no pattern.' When people actually write it down, they usually find calm stretches and specific triggers. The word 'always' rarely survives a week of data.

「我一直都焦虑，哪有什么规律。」真正记下来之后，大多数人会发现平静的时段和特定的触发点。「总是」这个词，很少能撑过一周的记录。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Self-monitoring is a long-standing tool in behavior therapy, used both to understand a problem and to track change during treatment (Korotitsch & Nelson-G., 1999). Researchers have also noticed 'reactivity': the act of recording a behavior can itself change it. In one experiment with college students, the biggest drop in the tracked behavior came when self-monitoring was combined with self-reward (Mace & Kratochwill, 1985).

自我监测是行为治疗里由来已久的工具，既用来弄清问题，也用来追踪治疗中的变化（Korotitsch 与 Nelson-G.，1999）。研究者还注意到「反应性」：记录一种行为这个动作本身，就可能改变它。一项以大学生为对象的实验中，自我监测再加上自我奖励时，被记录的行为下降得最多（Mace 与 Kratochwill，1985）。

How it connects和别处怎么连

The 'what I'm thinking' column is the bridge to Part 2 (Activities 7–20), where you learn to spot and rewrite anxious thoughts such as all-or-nothing thinking (9) and overgeneralizing (10). Keeping a log is also a habit that shows up in journaling (22) and in Cornell-style or question-chain notes on our notes page.

「当时在想什么」那一栏，是通往第二部分（第 7–20 个练习）的桥：那里会教你识别和改写焦虑的想法，比如非黑即白（9）和以偏概全（10）。记录的习惯也会出现在写出来（22）里，以及我们笔记方法页上的康奈尔笔记、问题链。
