Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Test anxiety is learned, which means it can be unlearned: a plan for the days before the test and a few tools for the test itself make you feel far less helpless.考试焦虑是学来的，也就是说可以「改掉」：考前几天有一个计划，考场上有几样工具，人就不会那么无助。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Worrying about an upcoming exam is a common trigger, with symptoms like trouble sleeping, a racing heart, sweating, dizziness, nausea, or heavy worry; it is a learned behavior that can be changed with the right techniques for before and during the test.担心即将到来的考试是一种常见的焦虑诱因，症状包括睡不好、心跳加快、出汗、头晕、恶心或担心过度；它是一种学来的行为，可以靠考前和考试中的合适技巧来改变。

How does the chair exercise work?椅子上的那个练习怎么做？
Answer in your head, then tap to flip先在心里答，再点一下翻面
Sit with feet flat, grip the underside of your chair, pull up with your hands while pushing down with your feet for about five seconds, then relax for five to ten seconds. Repeat two or three times.双脚平放坐好，双手抓住椅子底面，手往上拉、脚往下踩，坚持约五秒，然后放松五到十秒。重复两三次。

What is 'palming'?「掌心捂眼」是什么？
Answer in your head, then tap to flip先在心里答，再点一下翻面
Close your eyes and cup your palms over them without touching your eyes, resting the heels of your hands on your cheekbones, then picture a relaxing scene for a minute or two.闭上眼睛，用掌心轻轻盖住双眼但不碰到眼睛，掌根搭在颧骨上，然后想象一个放松的画面一两分钟。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Worrying about an upcoming exam is a common trigger, with symptoms like trouble sleeping, a racing heart, sweating, dizziness, nausea, or heavy worry; it is a learned behavior that can be changed with the right techniques for before and during the test.

担心即将到来的考试是一种常见的焦虑诱因，症状包括睡不好、心跳加快、出汗、头晕、恶心或担心过度；它是一种学来的行为，可以靠考前和考试中的合适技巧来改变。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'If I picture doing well, I'll do well.' The book says visualization 'sets up new neural pathways' and 'the reality will follow'; that overstates it. Picturing yourself calm can lower anxiety, but it works alongside studying, not instead of it. Another misreading: 'Test anxiety means I'm not smart enough.' Research links test anxiety with lower scores, and in experiments easing it before an exam raised scores, so calming down lets more of what you actually know show up. If your anxiety is tied to fear of a parent's reaction, that is worth talking about with a trusted adult or school counselor.

「只要想象自己考得好，就会考得好。」书里说想象能「建立新的神经通路」、「现实就会随之而来」，这说过头了。想象自己平静可以降低焦虑，但它是和复习一起起作用，不是代替复习。另一种误读是「考试焦虑说明我不够聪明」。研究发现考试焦虑与较低的分数相伴，实验中考前缓解焦虑也提高了分数；所以平静下来，你真正掌握的东西就能更多地发挥出来。如果你的焦虑和害怕家长的反应有关，值得和一个信任的大人或学校辅导员聊聊。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Two findings fit this activity well. A meta-analysis covering 238 studies found that test anxiety has a small-to-moderate negative link with performance on tests and grades, strongest in the middle grades (von der Embse et al., 2018). And in laboratory and classroom experiments, a short writing exercise right before an important test, where students wrote about their worries, raised exam scores, especially for students who usually feel anxious about tests (Ramirez & Beilock, 2011). Writing worries down for a few minutes is something you can add to Valeria's list.

有两项发现很贴合这个练习。一篇涵盖 238 项研究的元分析发现，考试焦虑与考试成绩和平时成绩之间有小到中等的负向关联，在中间年级（大致是初中）最明显（von der Embse 等，2018）。在实验室和课堂实验里，大考前花几分钟把担心写下来，能提高考试分数，尤其是那些平常就容易考试焦虑的学生（Ramirez 与 Beilock，2011）。考前写几分钟担心，可以加到瓦莱里娅的清单上。

  • von der Embse N, Jester D, Roy D, Post J. Test anxiety effects, predictors, and correlates: a 30-year meta-analytic review. J Affect Disord 2018;227:483–493. doi:10.1016/j.jad.2017.11.048 link链接
  • Ramirez G, Beilock SL. Writing about testing worries boosts exam performance in the classroom. Science 2011;331(6014):211–213. doi:10.1126/science.1199427 link链接
