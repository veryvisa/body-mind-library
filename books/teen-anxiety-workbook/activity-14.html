Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Listing honestly what a belief gives you and what it costs you shows whether it's worth keeping, and how you might get its benefits another way.老老实实列出一个信念给了你什么、又让你付出了什么，就能看出它值不值得留着，以及能不能换条路得到它的好处。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

This activity helps you see how a belief affects your anxiety and your life. By listing its advantages and disadvantages, finding other ways to get the advantages, and naming the belief you'd rather have, you can choose to keep it or change it.这个练习帮你看清一个信念怎样影响你的焦虑和生活。列出它的好处与坏处，想办法用别的方式得到那些好处，再写下你更想要的信念，你就能决定是留着它还是改掉它。

Why does the analysis ask you to admit the benefits of an anxious belief?为什么得失清单要你承认一个焦虑信念的好处？
Answer in your head, then tap to flip先在心里答，再点一下翻面
Beliefs that hang on usually protect us from something. Madison's belief spared her from worrying about her looks and from awkward small talk. Seeing that makes changing it less scary.赖着不走的信念，通常在替我们挡着什么。麦迪逊的信念让她不用为打扮发愁，也不用硬着头皮和不熟的人聊天。看清这一点，改起来就没那么可怕。

Is the decision just which side has more items?最后的决定只看哪边条目多吗？

What makes a good replacement belief?什么样的新信念算好？
Answer in your head, then tap to flip先在心里答，再点一下翻面
One you can actually believe. Madison's new belief still admits a little anxiety but adds that she will have fun once she's there. Believable beats perfect.你真能信的那种。麦迪逊的新信念仍然承认会有点紧张，只是补上一句「到了那儿我会玩得开心」。可信，比完美更重要。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

This activity helps you see how a belief affects your anxiety and your life. By listing its advantages and disadvantages, finding other ways to get the advantages, and naming the belief you'd rather have, you can choose to keep it or change it.

这个练习帮你看清一个信念怎样影响你的焦虑和生活。列出它的好处与坏处，想办法用别的方式得到那些好处，再写下你更想要的信念，你就能决定是留着它还是改掉它。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'If a belief has benefits, it must be right.' Benefits explain why a belief sticks, not whether it's true or good for you. Madison's belief really did protect her from some discomfort, and it was also costing her friendships and fun.

「一个信念有好处，就说明它是对的。」好处只解释了它为什么赖着不走，不代表它是真的、或对你有益。麦迪逊的信念确实帮她躲开了一些不自在，同时也在让她失去朋友和快乐。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Madison's story fits how researchers describe avoidance: steering clear of feared situations is usually seen as a key reason anxiety disorders persist. Hofmann and Hay (2018) also argue for a more balanced view, in which some avoidance can be sensible. That matches step 3 of this activity, which asks you to admit a belief's real benefits before deciding what to keep.

麦迪逊的故事符合研究者对回避的描述：避开害怕的场合，通常被看作焦虑障碍持续存在的关键原因。Hofmann 与 Hay（2018）同时主张一种更平衡的看法：有些回避是合理的。这和本练习第 3 步一致——先承认一个信念真实的好处，再决定留下什么。

  • Hofmann SG, Hay AC. Rethinking avoidance: toward a balanced approach to avoidance in treating anxiety disorders. J Anxiety Disord 2018;55:14–21. doi:10.1016/j.janxdis.2018.03.004 link链接

How it connects和别处怎么连

Madison's story is a classic avoidance loop: skipping the dance kept the fear from ever being tested. Gradually facing feared situations comes up again in later parts of the book. The 'keep the benefit another way' step echoes the 'change what you can' half of Activity 19, and the habit of weighing choices returns in problem-solving (34).

麦迪逊的故事是典型的回避循环：不去舞会，恐惧就永远得不到检验。循序渐进地面对害怕的场合，在书的后面几部分还会出现。「换条路留住好处」这一步，呼应第 19 个练习里「改变能改变的」那一半；掂量选择的习惯，在解决问题（34）里还会再用到。
