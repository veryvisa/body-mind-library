Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Many people believe in a power greater than human power and call it by different names; if you hold such a belief, you can draw on it both to prevent anxiety and to manage it.

很多人相信有一种比人更大的力量，并用不同的名字称呼它；如果你有这样的信念，就可以借助它来预防焦虑，也可以用它来应对焦虑。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'I should believe in a higher power to beat anxiety.' This activity is an option, not a requirement; plenty of people manage anxiety well without religious beliefs. Another misreading: 'Faith means I don't need other help.' Belief and practical help work fine together, and if spiritual ideas ever feel frightening or guilt-heavy, that is a good moment to talk with a trusted adult or counselor. This site doesn't promote any particular belief.

「要战胜焦虑，我就应该相信更高的力量。」这个练习是一个选项，不是要求；很多人没有宗教信仰，也把焦虑应对得很好。另一种误读是「有了信仰，就不需要其他帮助」。信念和实际的帮助可以并行不悖；如果灵性的想法让你感到害怕或满是内疚，那正是和信任的大人或辅导员聊聊的好时机。本站不推崇任何特定信仰。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Researchers who study 'religious coping' find that it can go both ways. A meta-analysis of 49 studies found that positive forms, such as feeling supported or finding meaning, were linked with better adjustment to stress, while negative forms, such as feeling punished or abandoned, were linked with worse adjustment (Ano & Vasconcelles, 2005). The studies measured links rather than causes and were not specific to teens. This matches the book's advice: if your beliefs bring peace, lean on them; if they bring fear, talk with an adult you trust.

研究「宗教应对」的学者发现，它有两个方向。一篇汇总 49 项研究的元分析发现，正向的形式（比如感到被支持、找到意义）与更好地适应压力有关，负向的形式（比如觉得被惩罚、被抛弃）与更差的适应有关（Ano 与 Vasconcelles，2005）。这些研究测的是关联而不是因果，也不是专门针对青少年。这和书里的建议一致：如果你的信念带来平静，就依靠它；如果它带来恐惧，就和一个你信任的大人聊聊。

  • Ano GG, Vasconcelles EB. Religious coping and psychological adjustment to stress: a meta-analysis. J Clin Psychol 2005;61(4):461–480. doi:10.1002/jclp.20049 link链接
