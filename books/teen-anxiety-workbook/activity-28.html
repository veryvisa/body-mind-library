Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Grounding techniques are quick, concrete ways to pull your attention out of anxious thoughts and into what is around you right now.着地技巧是一些快速、具体的办法，把注意力从焦虑的念头里拉出来，放到此刻身边的事物上。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Grounding tools ease anxiety by plugging you straight into what is happening right now; borrowing from mindfulness, they put a little space between you and the distress so your mind can refocus and settle.着地技巧通过立刻把你和当下连接起来来缓解焦虑；它们借助正念，让你和痛苦之间拉开一点距离，帮头脑重新聚焦、平静下来。

What goes on a 'five fingers' card?「五根手指」卡片上写什么？
Answer in your head, then tap to flip先在心里答，再点一下翻面
Trace your hand and label each finger with a sense; next to each, write one thing that calms you through that sense, like a soft hoodie for touch. When anxious, go finger by finger.描出自己的手，每根手指标一种感官；在旁边写一样通过这种感官让你平静的东西，比如触觉写「软软的连帽衫」。焦虑时，一根手指一根手指地过。

“Got it” brings a card back in 1, then 3, then 7 days. “Look again” keeps it close. Nothing is scored.点「会了」，卡片会在 1 天、3 天、7 天后再出现；点「再看」就留在手边。不打分。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Grounding tools ease anxiety by plugging you straight into what is happening right now; borrowing from mindfulness, they put a little space between you and the distress so your mind can refocus and settle.

着地技巧通过立刻把你和当下连接起来来缓解焦虑；它们借助正念，让你和痛苦之间拉开一点距离，帮头脑重新聚焦、平静下来。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'If a technique doesn't work the first time, grounding isn't for me.' Rina's class showed the opposite: each person found a different favorite, so try several. Another misreading: grounding is a way to never face things. It is a short reset that helps you get steady enough to deal with what is in front of you, not a replacement for doing so. A small safety note: if you use ice or cold water, keep it brief and gentle.

「一个技巧第一次没用，着地就不适合我。」莉娜的课堂恰好说明了相反的情况：每个人找到的心头好都不一样，所以多试几种。另一种误读是把着地当作永远不用面对事情的办法。它是一次短暂的「重启」，帮你稳到能处理眼前的事，而不是替代处理。一个小小的安全提醒：用冰块或冷水时，时间短一点、动作轻一点。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

We found no trial that tests the 5-4-3-2-1 game or the other grounding techniques on this list one by one, so for these specific techniques there is no reliable source yet (暂无可靠来源). Grounding borrows from mindfulness, which in randomized trials with children and teens has shown small benefits (Dunning et al., 2019). The honest summary: grounding is a widely taught, low-risk way to steady yourself in the moment, and your own ratings are the best guide to which one suits you.

我们没有找到逐一检验 5-4-3-2-1 游戏或清单上其他着地技巧的试验，所以对这些具体技巧，暂无可靠来源。着地技巧借用了正念的做法，而正念在儿童与青少年的随机试验里显示出较小的益处（Dunning 等，2019）。如实地说：着地是一种被广泛教授、风险很低、能在当下稳住自己的办法；哪一种适合你，你自己的打分是最好的参考。

  • Dunning DL, Griffiths K, Kuyken W, et al. Research Review: The effects of mindfulness-based interventions on cognition and mental health in children and adolescents – a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. J Child Psychol Psychiatry 2019;60(3):244–258. doi:10.1111/jcpp.12980 link链接

How it connects和别处怎么连

Grounding is mindfulness (27) in pocket size. The 'anchor yourself' item echoes centering (26), the five-fingers card leads into self-soothing (29), and counting with a breath on each number previews Activity 30. These are also the tools to reach for during panic (41).

着地就是口袋版的正念（27）。「给自己下锚」呼应找重心（26），五根手指卡片引出自我安抚（29），每数一个数配一次呼吸则预告了第 30 个练习。惊恐来袭时（41），这些也是可以伸手去拿的工具。
