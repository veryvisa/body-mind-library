Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Some 'shoulds' are useful reminders, but unrealistic or unimportant ones pile on pressure; sorting them lets you keep the useful ones and drop the rest.有些「应该」是有用的提醒，但不现实或不重要的「应该」只会叠加压力；把它们分一分，留下有用的，放下其余的。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

'Should' is fine when it points to something responsible or courteous, but shoulds get out of hand and raise anxiety when they are unrealistic or unimportant. Looking at each one carefully shows which to keep and which to drop.当「应该」指向尽责或礼貌时，它没问题；可一旦不现实或不重要，「应该」就会失控、推高焦虑。逐条仔细看，就知道哪些留下、哪些放下。

Are all 'should' statements bad?所有的「应该」都不好吗？
Answer in your head, then tap to flip先在心里答，再点一下翻面
No. 'I should have studied more for that test' was realistic for Brandon and pointed to something he could change. The trouble is with shoulds that are unrealistic or don't matter to you.不是。「那次考试我该多复习」对布兰登来说很现实，也指向他能改的地方。麻烦的是那些不现实、或对你并不重要的「应该」。

What two questions help you sort a 'should'?用哪两个问题来给「应该」分类？
Answer in your head, then tap to flip先在心里答，再点一下翻面
Is it realistic? Is it important to me, not just to someone else? If either answer is no, you can probably let it go.它现实吗？它对我本人重要吗，而不只是对别人重要？任何一个答案是否定的，多半就可以放下。
Activity 11 · "Should" Statements练习 11 · 「应该」句式
Where do many of our shoulds come from?我们的很多「应该」是从哪里来的？
Answer in your head, then tap to flip先在心里答，再点一下翻面
Often from other people's voices: what classmates think is cool, what we picture adults expect, what we see online. Brandon's 'I should be in a sport' came from what some kids thought, not from what he enjoyed.常常来自别人的声音：同学觉得什么酷、我们以为大人期待什么、网上看到的东西。布兰登那句「我应该去搞体育」来自一些同学的看法，而不是他自己喜欢什么。
Activity 11 · "Should" Statements练习 11 · 「应该」句式
What can you do with a 'should' that passes both tests?两关都过了的「应该」，可以怎么处理？
Answer in your head, then tap to flip先在心里答，再点一下翻面
Turn it into a plan. 'I should have studied harder' becomes 'For the next test I'll start two days earlier.' A plan lowers anxiety; a bare should mostly scolds.把它变成计划。「我该多复习」变成「下次考试提前两天开始」。计划会降低焦虑，光秃秃的「应该」大多只是在责备。

“Got it” brings a card back in 1, then 3, then 7 days. “Look again” keeps it close. Nothing is scored.点「会了」，卡片会在 1 天、3 天、7 天后再出现；点「再看」就留在手边。不打分。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

'Should' is fine when it points to something responsible or courteous, but shoulds get out of hand and raise anxiety when they are unrealistic or unimportant. Looking at each one carefully shows which to keep and which to drop.

当「应该」指向尽责或礼貌时，它没问题；可一旦不现实或不重要，「应该」就会失控、推高焦虑。逐条仔细看，就知道哪些留下、哪些放下。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'If I drop my shoulds, I'll get lazy.' The chapter keeps the shoulds that matter and are realistic; it only removes the ones that add guilt without helping. Brandon still studies hours each night. He just stops scolding himself for resting on the bus.

「把『应该』都放下，我会变懒。」这一章留下的是重要又现实的「应该」，去掉的只是那些只添内疚、不帮忙的。布兰登每晚照样学习好几个小时，他只是不再因为坐车时歇一歇而责怪自己。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Psychologist Albert Ellis, who founded rational emotive behavior therapy (REBT), put rigid 'musts, shoulds, ought tos, have tos' at the center of emotional distress, and taught people to turn them into flexible preferences ('I'd like to…' instead of 'I must…'). 'Should statements' are also one of the ten cognitive distortions on D. Burns's CBT list. Both traditions keep reasonable goals and target only the demanding, absolute version.

创立理性情绪行为疗法（REBT）的心理学家阿尔伯特·埃利斯，把僵硬的「必须、应该、非得」放在情绪困扰的核心，并教人把它们变成有弹性的偏好（「我希望……」而不是「我必须……」）。「应该」句式也是大卫·伯恩斯 CBT 清单上十种认知扭曲之一。两派都保留合理的目标，只针对那种苛刻、绝对的说法。

  • Burns DD. Secrets of self-esteem #2: negative and positive distortions (Feeling Good blog, 2014), listing his ten cognitive distortions first published in Feeling Good (1980). link链接

How it connects和别处怎么连

Should statements often feed perfectionism (15) and pressure to succeed (16). Asking 'is it realistic?' is the same move as the gray scale in Activity 9, and 'whose should is this?' leads into knowing your authentic self in 16.

「应该」句式常常给完美主义（15）和成功压力（16）添柴。问「它现实吗」，和第 9 个练习的灰度尺是同一招；问「这是谁的应该」，则通向第 16 个练习里的「认识真实的自己」。
