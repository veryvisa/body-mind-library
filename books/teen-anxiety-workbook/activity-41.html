Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

A panic attack is a short burst of very intense anxiety that feels frightening but is not dangerous, and reminding yourself of that while you slow your body down helps it pass.惊恐发作是一阵短暂而极强的焦虑，感觉很吓人，但并不危险；一边提醒自己这一点，一边让身体慢下来，它就会过去。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

A panic attack is a brief spell of very intense, uncomfortable anxiety; people can learn to manage it by following simple steps for the mind and body, and anyone who has panic attacks regularly should tell an adult and a doctor.惊恐发作是一阵短暂、极其强烈、让人很难受的焦虑；人可以按照一些针对头脑和身体的简单步骤学会应对它；经常发作的人，应该告诉大人和医生。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

A panic attack is a brief spell of very intense, uncomfortable anxiety; people can learn to manage it by following simple steps for the mind and body, and anyone who has panic attacks regularly should tell an adult and a doctor.

惊恐发作是一阵短暂、极其强烈、让人很难受的焦虑；人可以按照一些针对头脑和身体的简单步骤学会应对它；经常发作的人，应该告诉大人和医生。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'A panic attack means something is seriously wrong with my body.' The sensations are real and scary, but in a panic attack they are the body's alarm going off too loudly, not a sign of danger; a doctor's check the first time can confirm that and give peace of mind. Another misreading is in the book's last question, which asks how to 'avoid' future attacks. Spotting patterns helps you prepare, but steering clear of every place where panic happened can make the fear grow, so it's worth working through with a counselor if it keeps happening.

「惊恐发作说明我身体出了大问题。」那些感觉是真实的、吓人的，但在惊恐发作时，它们是身体的警报响得太大声，而不是危险的信号；第一次发作时让医生检查一下，可以确认这一点，也让人安心。另一种误读藏在书的最后一个问题里：它问怎样「避免」以后的发作。找出规律能帮你做好准备，但如果躲开所有发作过的地方，恐惧反而可能变大；如果反复发作，值得和辅导员一起处理。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

The leading psychological explanation of panic, proposed by D. Clark, says panic attacks come from misreading normal anxiety sensations, such as a racing heart or dizziness, as far more dangerous than they are, for example taking palpitations as a sign of a heart attack (Clark, 1986). That is why the book's first step, reminding yourself you are not in danger, matters. A Cochrane review of 54 trials in adults found cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) the most studied treatment and often better than other therapies, though the evidence quality was low (Pompoli et al., 2016). We did not find an equally solid review for teens, so these results are for adults.

关于惊恐，最有影响的心理学解释由大卫·克拉克提出：惊恐发作来自把正常的焦虑感觉——比如心跳加快、头晕——误读成比实际危险得多的东西，例如把心悸当成心脏病发作的征兆（Clark，1986）。这就是为什么书里第一步「提醒自己没有危险」很重要。一篇汇总 54 项成人试验的考科蓝（Cochrane）综述发现，认知行为疗法（CBT）是研究最多的疗法，常常优于其他疗法，但证据质量偏低（Pompoli 等，2016）。我们没有找到同样扎实的青少年综述，所以这些结果来自成年人。

  • Pompoli A, Furukawa TA, Imai H, Tajika A, Efthimiou O, Salanti G. Psychological therapies for panic disorder with or without agoraphobia in adults: a network meta-analysis. Cochrane Database Syst Rev 2016;4:CD011004. doi:10.1002/14651858.CD011004.pub2 link链接

How it connects和别处怎么连

Panic is the fight-flight-freeze response from Activity 2 at full volume. The breathing tools in Activity 30 and the grounding tools in Activity 28 are the body half of this plan, and the gentle facing of fears in Activity 42 is the usual next step if panic starts to make you avoid places.

惊恐就是第 2 个练习里「战斗—逃跑—僵住」反应开到最大音量。第 30 个练习的呼吸工具、第 28 个练习的着地工具，就是这个方案里「用身体」的那一半；如果惊恐开始让你回避某些地方，第 42 个练习里温和地面对恐惧，通常就是下一步。

Step 7 · Tell a friend in one sentence第 7 步 · 用一句话讲给朋友

Example示例
A panic attack is your body's smoke alarm going off when there's no fire: loud and scary, but it passes faster if you sit, breathe slowly, and tell yourself you're safe.惊恐发作就像没有着火时烟雾警报响了：又响又吓人；但坐下来、慢慢呼吸、告诉自己是安全的，它会过去得更快。
