Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Helping other people takes your mind off your own worries and gives you a sense of connection and purpose, which can lift your mood and ease anxiety.帮助别人能让你暂时放下自己的担心，还能带来与人相连、做有意义之事的感觉，从而让心情变好、焦虑减轻。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Planning to help someone, or actually helping, pulls your attention away from your own troubles and can brighten your mood, so lending a hand where it's needed can ease anxiety.想着帮助别人、或真的去做，能让你从自己的问题里分一下心，也能让心情变好；向需要帮助的人伸出手，可以降低你的焦虑。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Planning to help someone, or actually helping, pulls your attention away from your own troubles and can brighten your mood, so lending a hand where it's needed can ease anxiety.

想着帮助别人、或真的去做，能让你从自己的问题里分一下心，也能让心情变好；向需要帮助的人伸出手，可以降低你的焦虑。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Helping others means ignoring my own problems.' Neel still ran, worked, and looked after himself; helping was one tool among several. Another misreading: the book says helping 'releases endorphins'. That is a popular explanation, but the research we found measures well-being and connection, not brain chemicals. And helping shouldn't become one more pressure: one small act counts.

「帮助别人，就是不管自己的问题。」尼尔照样跑步、打工、照顾自己；帮助别人是几样工具之一。另一种误读来自书里「帮助别人会释放内啡肽」的说法。这是一种流行的解释，但我们找到的研究测的是幸福感和人际连结，不是大脑化学物质。另外，帮助别人不该变成又一份压力：一件小事就算数。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

A meta-analysis of 27 studies found that doing acts of kindness gives the person doing them a small-to-medium boost in well-being (Curry et al., 2018). In a classroom experiment in Vancouver, 9- to 11-year-olds who did three kind acts a week for four weeks became more accepted by their classmates; well-being rose in both the kindness group and the comparison group (Layous et al., 2012). The research is about well-being and connection rather than anxiety directly, and it doesn't settle the book's claim about endorphins.

一篇汇总 27 项研究的元分析发现，做善事能让做的人自己的幸福感有小到中等的提升（Curry 等，2018）。温哥华的一项课堂实验中，9 至 11 岁的孩子连续四周每周做三件善事后，更受同学接纳；幸福感则在善事组和对照组都有上升（Layous 等，2012）。这些研究讲的是幸福感与人际连结，而不是直接讲焦虑，也不能证实书里关于内啡肽的说法。

  • Curry OS, Rowland LA, Van Lissa CJ, Zlotowitz S, McAlaney J, Whitehouse H. Happy to help? A systematic review and meta-analysis of the effects of performing acts of kindness on the well-being of the actor. J Exp Soc Psychol 2018;76:320–329. doi:10.1016/j.jesp.2018.02.014 link链接
  • Layous K, Nelson SK, Oberle E, Schonert-Reichl KA, Lyubomirsky S. Kindness counts: prompting prosocial behavior in preadolescents boosts peer acceptance and well-being. PLoS One 2012;7(12):e51380. doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0051380 link链接

How it connects和别处怎么连

Being fully present with the kids is mindfulness (27) in action. 'Opt for helping others' was one of the steps in Activity 44, and Activity 39 sets the healthy limit: care without carrying.

全心陪着孩子们，就是正念（27）的实践。「去帮助别人」是第 44 个练习的步骤之一；第 39 个练习则划出了健康的边界：关心，不背负。
