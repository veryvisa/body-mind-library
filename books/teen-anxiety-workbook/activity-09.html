Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Judging yourself as either perfect or a total failure leaves no room for real life, which nearly always happens in the gray in between.把自己要么判成完美、要么判成彻底失败，就没给真实生活留位置——而真实生活几乎总是发生在中间的灰色地带。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

All-or-nothing thinking judges things in extreme, black-and-white categories. It is irrational because real people and situations are always a mix, and it raises anxiety because you fear that missing perfection makes you a complete failure.非黑即白的想法用极端的两分法来评判事情。它不理性，因为真实的人和事总是好坏混杂；它让人更焦虑，因为你会害怕：达不到完美，就等于彻底失败。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

All-or-nothing thinking judges things in extreme, black-and-white categories. It is irrational because real people and situations are always a mix, and it raises anxiety because you fear that missing perfection makes you a complete failure.

非黑即白的想法用极端的两分法来评判事情。它不理性，因为真实的人和事总是好坏混杂；它让人更焦虑，因为你会害怕：达不到完美，就等于彻底失败。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Seeing the gray means accepting I'm average.' The gray area is not a grade of 'mediocre'; it is a more detailed description. 'Strong at soccer, still learning to cook' is more specific than either 'excellent' or 'zero', and specific is easier to work with.

「看到灰色，就是承认自己很普通。」灰色地带不是「平庸」这个分数，而是一份更细的描述。「足球很强，做饭还在学」比「优秀」或「零分」都更具体，而具体的东西更好下手。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

The idea of 'thinking traps' comes from Aaron Beck's cognitive therapy, which began by describing the distorted thinking he saw in depressed patients (Beck, 1963). The psychiatrist D. Burns later popularized a list of ten such 'cognitive distortions', with all-or-nothing thinking first on the list; it is still widely used in cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT). The labels are teaching tools rather than diagnoses: everyone falls into these traps sometimes.

「思维陷阱」这个说法源自亚伦·贝克的认知疗法，它最早描述的是贝克在抑郁患者身上看到的扭曲想法（Beck，1963）。后来精神科医生大卫·伯恩斯把这类「认知扭曲」整理成十条并推广开来，非黑即白排在第一条；这份清单至今在认知行为疗法（CBT）里广泛使用。这些标签是教学工具，不是诊断：每个人都会时不时掉进去。

  • Beck AT. Thinking and depression. I. Idiosyncratic content and cognitive distortions. Arch Gen Psychiatry 1963;9:324–333. link链接
  • Burns DD. Secrets of self-esteem #2: negative and positive distortions (Feeling Good blog, 2014), listing his ten cognitive distortions first published in Feeling Good (1980). link链接
