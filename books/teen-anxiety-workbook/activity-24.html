Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Moving your body, in almost any way you enjoy, can bring anxiety down in the moment and, done regularly, keep it lower from day to day.活动身体——几乎任何你喜欢的方式——都能当场把焦虑降下来；规律地做，还能让平日的焦虑保持在较低水平。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Almost any kind of exercise lowers anxiety: doing it regularly helps prevent anxiety, and doing it when you feel anxious releases tension right then.几乎任何运动都能降低焦虑：规律地做有助于预防，焦虑时做能当场释放紧张。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Almost any kind of exercise lowers anxiety: doing it regularly helps prevent anxiety, and doing it when you feel anxious releases tension right then.

几乎任何运动都能降低焦虑：规律地做有助于预防，焦虑时做能当场释放紧张。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Exercise only counts if it's intense, or a sport I'm good at.' Gentle movement counts, and so does something you're bad at but enjoy. Another misreading is to treat exercise as a punishment or something to overdo; it works best as something that feels good. If you have a health condition, or you're starting something very new and strenuous, check with a doctor first.

「只有激烈的运动、或者我擅长的项目才算数。」温和的活动也算，你不擅长但喜欢的也算。另一种误读是把运动当成惩罚，或者练过头；它在让人感觉舒服时最管用。如果你有健康方面的问题，或者要开始一项很陌生、很吃力的运动，先问问医生。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Research on young people supports the book's main point. A review of randomized trials in children and young people (up to age 25) found that physical activity produced a moderate improvement in short-term anxiety compared with doing little or nothing (Carter et al., 2021). The authors also note that the studies were few and mostly of low quality, so the size of the benefit is uncertain. The book's explanation that exercise 'gets rid of adrenaline' is a simplified picture; the research shows that exercise helps without settling exactly why.

针对年轻人的研究支持书里的主要观点。一篇汇总儿童与年轻人（至 25 岁）随机试验的综述发现，与几乎不做什么相比，体育活动让短时的焦虑有中等程度的改善（Carter 等，2021）。作者同时指出，研究数量少、质量大多偏低，所以益处到底有多大还不确定。书里说运动能「排掉肾上腺素」，是一种简化的说法；研究显示运动有帮助，但还没有说定究竟为什么。

  • Carter T, Pascoe M, Bastounis A, Morres ID, Callaghan P, Parker AG. The effect of physical activity on anxiety in children and young people: a systematic review and meta-analysis. J Affect Disord 2021;285:10–21. doi:10.1016/j.jad.2021.02.026 link链接

Activity 5 introduced prevention and intervention; this is one of the clearest examples. Activity 25 relaxes the same tight muscles from the inside, and Activity 38 helps you find time in a busy week. Activity 40 adds a twist: for anger, calming activities tend to work better than revving yourself up.

第 5 个练习介绍了预防和干预，这一章是最清楚的例子之一。第 25 个练习从内部放松同样紧绷的肌肉，第 38 个练习帮你在忙碌的一周里挤出时间。第 40 个练习会补一个转折：处理愤怒时，让自己平静的活动往往比让自己更兴奋的活动更有效。
