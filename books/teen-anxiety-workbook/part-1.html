Part 1 · Understanding anxiety第 1 部分 · 认识焦虑

Activities 1–6练习 1–6 · The book itself has no parts; this six-part grouping is ours, to give a map of the 46 activities.原书不分部分；这六部分是我们为了给 46 个练习画一张地图而做的分组。

1 · About Anxiety关于焦虑
Anxiety is a normal feeling with two broad causes — what happens around you and how your brain chemistry is set — and both can be turned down with therapy, self-help, medication, or a mix.焦虑是正常的感受，大体有两类来源——身边发生的事，和大脑化学的状态；两类都能靠心理治疗、自助练习、药物或它们的组合调低。
2 · Your Fight-Flight-Freeze Response战斗—逃跑—僵住反应
Your brain has an ancient alarm that gets the body ready to fight, run, or freeze; it can't always tell a real danger from a stressful moment, and knowing that lets you calm it on purpose.大脑里有一套古老的警报，会让身体准备好战斗、逃跑或僵住；它常常分不清真危险和只是让人紧张的场面——知道这一点，你就能有意识地让它安静下来。
3 · How You Experience Anxiety你是怎样经历焦虑的
Anxiety shows up differently in different people — in sleep, in the stomach, in restlessness — and learning your own signals helps you catch it early and pick a tool.焦虑在每个人身上表现不同——在睡眠里、在肚子里、在坐立不安里；认清自己的信号，就能早点发现它并挑一个合适的工具。
4 · Your Anxiety Patterns你的焦虑规律
Keeping a short log of when you feel anxious, what was happening, and what you were thinking reveals patterns you can't see from inside a single bad moment.把「什么时候焦虑、当时发生了什么、心里在想什么」简单记下来，就能看到身处某个难受时刻时看不见的规律。
5 · Prevention and Intervention预防与干预
Calming skills work best when you practice them on ordinary days (prevention), so they are ready to use when anxiety spikes (intervention).平静的本领要在平常日子里练（预防），焦虑冲高时才拿得出来（干预）。
6 · Peace Is Already Within You平静本来就在你心里
Calm is a natural state you already have; anxious thoughts cover it up like layers, and you can uncover it rather than having to create it.平静是你本来就有的状态；焦虑的念头像一层层东西把它盖住，你要做的是把它找回来，而不是从头造一个。
