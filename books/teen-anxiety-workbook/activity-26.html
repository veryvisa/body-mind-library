Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

When things pile up and you feel scattered, a few seconds of attention on your body's center of gravity can steady you enough to act instead of react.事情堆在一起、人觉得七零八落时，花几秒钟把注意力放在身体的重心上，能让你稳下来，去「做」而不是被推着「反应」。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Bringing your focus to the middle point of your body, its center of gravity, gives you a sense of balance and steadiness that makes anxiety easier to handle.把注意力和精力集中到身体的重心上，能带来平衡和稳定，从而帮你应对焦虑。

What the book says书里怎么说

Bringing your focus to the middle point of your body, its center of gravity, gives you a sense of balance and steadiness that makes anxiety easier to handle.

把注意力和精力集中到身体的重心上，能带来平衡和稳定，从而帮你应对焦虑。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Pulling my energy into my center is a scientific fact.' The center of gravity is a real physical idea, but 'drawing in scattered energy' is best read as a helpful picture to imagine, not a description of what the body literally does. Another misreading: 'If centering didn't calm me in a crisis, it doesn't work.' Like the other skills in this part, it is easier to use in a hard moment if you have practiced it on easy days.

「把能量收回重心，是科学事实。」重心是真实的物理概念，但「把分散的能量收回来」最好当作一个帮助想象的画面，而不是身体字面上在做的事。另一种误读是「危急时刻找重心没让我平静，说明它没用」。和这一部分的其他本领一样，平常日子里练过，难熬的时刻才更容易用上。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

We could not find any study that tests this exact centering exercise, so on the question 'does centering work?' there is no reliable source yet (暂无可靠来源). What scientists do study is interoception, the sense of what is going on inside your body; an expert review describes problems with it as increasingly seen as part of anxiety disorders (Khalsa et al., 2018). That makes body-focused attention a reasonable thing to explore, but it is not evidence for this exercise. Treat centering as a practice to try and judge by your own before-and-after ratings.

我们没有找到任何检验这个「找重心」练习本身的研究，所以在「它有没有效」这个问题上，暂无可靠来源。科学家确实在研究「内感受」——感知身体内部状况的能力；一篇专家综述说，内感受出问题日益被看作焦虑障碍的一部分（Khalsa 等，2018）。这让「把注意力放在身体上」成为一个值得探索的方向，但它不是这个练习有效的证据。把找重心当作一个可以试的练习，用你自己练习前后的打分来判断。

  • Khalsa SS, Adolphs R, Cameron OG, et al. Interoception and mental health: a roadmap. Biol Psychiatry Cogn Neurosci Neuroimaging 2018;3(6):501–513. doi:10.1016/j.bpsc.2017.12.004 link链接

How it connects和别处怎么连

It is a short cousin of progressive relaxation (25) and mindfulness (27), and the 'anchor yourself' item in grounding (28) works the same way through the feet. Its lesson of taking problems one at a time is the heart of Activity 35 (it's a cinch by the inch).

它是渐进式放松（25）和正念（27）的简短近亲；着地技巧（28）里的「给自己下锚」用的是同一个思路，只是从脚下入手。「一个一个处理问题」的道理，正是第 35 个练习（一寸一寸就容易）的核心。
