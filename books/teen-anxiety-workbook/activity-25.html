Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Anxiety tightens your muscles, so slowly moving your attention through your body and letting each area loosen is a learnable way to bring anxiety down.焦虑会让肌肉绷紧；所以让注意力慢慢走过全身、让每个部位依次松下来，是一种学得会的降低焦虑的办法。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Anxiety makes muscles tense, and deliberately releasing that tension helps relieve anxiety; progressive relaxation does this area by area, either as regular prevention or as an intervention on a hard day.焦虑让肌肉紧张，有意识地放掉这份紧张有助于缓解焦虑；渐进式放松就是一个部位一个部位地做，可以当规律的预防，也可以在难熬的日子里当干预。

What the book says书里怎么说

Anxiety makes muscles tense, and deliberately releasing that tension helps relieve anxiety; progressive relaxation does this area by area, either as regular prevention or as an intervention on a hard day.

焦虑让肌肉紧张，有意识地放掉这份紧张有助于缓解焦虑；渐进式放松就是一个部位一个部位地做，可以当规律的预防，也可以在难熬的日子里当干预。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'If I don't end up totally relaxed, I did it wrong.' Any loosening counts, and the first tries can feel strange. A useful detail: the book's version only asks you to release each area. Many classic versions first tense each muscle group for a few seconds and then let go, which makes the difference easier to feel; if releasing alone is hard, you can try that. Activity 43 includes a quick tense-and-release move of this kind.

「如果没有完全放松，就是我做错了。」任何一点松弛都算数，头几次感觉怪也很正常。一个有用的细节：书里的版本只要求你放松每个部位；很多经典版本会先把每组肌肉绷紧几秒钟再放开，这样更容易感觉到差别。如果单靠放松很难，可以试试那种做法。第 43 个练习里就有一个这样「先绷后松」的快速动作。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Relaxation training has a solid track record for anxiety. A meta-analysis of 27 studies found a medium-to-large effect on anxiety; it pooled several methods together (progressive muscle relaxation, autogenic training, applied relaxation, and meditation) and found larger effects with longer training (Manzoni et al., 2008). The studies were not specific to teens, and the review did not test the exact release-only version in this book.

放松训练在焦虑上的效果有扎实的记录。一篇汇总 27 项研究的元分析发现，它对焦虑有中等到较大的效果；这篇分析把几种方法合在一起算（渐进式肌肉放松、自生训练、应用放松和冥想），训练时间越长效果越大（Manzoni 等，2008）。这些研究并非专门针对青少年，也没有检验书里这种「只放松、不先绷紧」的版本。

  • Manzoni GM, Pagnini F, Castelnuovo G, Molinari E. Relaxation training for anxiety: a ten-years systematic review with meta-analysis. BMC Psychiatry 2008;8:41. doi:10.1186/1471-244X-8-41 link链接
