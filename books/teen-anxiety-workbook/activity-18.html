Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

The messages you repeat to yourself shape your anxiety; noticing them and rewriting the negative ones into realistic, kind ones helps you feel steadier.你反复对自己说的话会塑造你的焦虑；留意它们，把消极的改写成真实又友善的话，人会更稳。

We send ourselves messages all day. Negative affirmations raise anxiety and positive ones help us feel peaceful. By noticing your messages, tracking them, and rewriting the negative ones, you can gradually change how you feel.我们一整天都在对自己说话。消极的自我暗示推高焦虑，积极的让人平静。留意这些话、记录下来、把消极的改写，就能慢慢改变感受。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

We send ourselves messages all day. Negative affirmations raise anxiety and positive ones help us feel peaceful. By noticing your messages, tracking them, and rewriting the negative ones, you can gradually change how you feel.

我们一整天都在对自己说话。消极的自我暗示推高焦虑，积极的让人平静。留意这些话、记录下来、把消极的改写，就能慢慢改变感受。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Positive affirmations means repeating that I'm amazing.' Statements that are too far from what you believe can backfire, especially when you're feeling low. Notice that Josh's rewrites aren't slogans; they are realistic, specific sentences with evidence behind them. That is the version worth practicing.

「正面自我对话，就是反复说我很棒。」和你心里所信相差太远的话可能适得其反，尤其在情绪低落时。注意乔希改写后的话并不是口号，而是真实、具体、有证据撑着的句子。值得练的是这一种。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

One study is a useful caution here. When people with low self-esteem repeated the statement 'I'm a lovable person', they felt worse afterward, not better; people with high self-esteem benefited only a little (Wood, Perunovic & L., 2009). The likely lesson is that statements far from what you believe can backfire. Josh's rewrites work differently: they are realistic and specific, which is closer to the kind of balanced thought CBT teaches.

有一项研究值得在这里提个醒。自尊低的人反复念「我是个可爱的人」之后，感觉反而更差；自尊高的人也只得到一点好处（Wood、Perunovic 与 L.，2009）。可能的教训是：和自己所信相差太远的话会适得其反。乔希的改写不一样：它们真实、具体，更接近 CBT 教的那种平衡的念头。

  • Wood JV, Perunovic WQE, L. JW. Positive self-statements: power for some, peril for others. Psychol Sci 2009;20(7):860–866. doi:10.1111/j.1467-9280.2009.02370.x link链接

How it connects和别处怎么连

The tracking chart is the self-monitoring habit from Activity 4 again. Rewriting messages uses the same skills as all-or-nothing thinking (9) and overgeneralizing (10): Josh's 'everyone is more confident' is an overgeneralization. The calm thought in thought stopping (12) can be one of your rewritten messages.

记录表又是第 4 个练习的自我监测习惯。改写自我对话用的本领，和非黑即白（9）、以偏概全（10）一样：乔希那句「人人都比我自信」本身就是以偏概全。念头叫停（12）里的平静念头，也可以用你改写后的一句话。
