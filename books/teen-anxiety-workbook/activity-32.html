Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Meditation is practice in bringing your attention back to something peaceful each time it wanders; done a little each day, it builds a steadier mind.冥想就是一次次练习：每当注意力飘走，就把它带回一个平静的东西上；每天做一点，心会越来越稳。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Meditation trains your brain to let go of anxiety and return to a peaceful state; practiced regularly as prevention, you can keep your cool in places that once set you on edge, and it lowers your anxiety in general.冥想训练大脑放下焦虑、回到平静；作为预防规律地练，能让你在过去会紧张的场合保持平静，也让整体的焦虑降低。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Meditation trains your brain to let go of anxiety and return to a peaceful state; practiced regularly as prevention, you can keep your cool in places that once set you on edge, and it lowers your anxiety in general.

冥想训练大脑放下焦虑、回到平静；作为预防规律地练，能让你在过去会紧张的场合保持平静，也让整体的焦虑降低。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'A good meditation is one where my mind stays still.' The wandering is not a failure; each return is the exercise, like a single lift at the gym. Another misreading: 'If twenty minutes is the goal, thirty seconds is pointless.' Jake started at thirty seconds, and the book says it doesn't matter how long it takes to build up. The book's claim that meditation 'saves time' is encouragement rather than something measured, but the research does support modest benefits for anxiety.

「好的冥想就是心一动不动。」走神不是失败；每一次回来才是练习本身，就像在健身房里举一次重量。另一种误读是「目标是二十分钟，那三十秒没意义」。杰克就是从三十秒开始的，书里也说加到目标要多久都没关系。书中说冥想能「省时间」，是一种鼓励而不是测量出来的结论；不过研究确实支持它对焦虑有一定的益处。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

A large review of 47 randomized trials with adults found moderate evidence that mindfulness meditation programs improve anxiety, with small-to-moderate effects; it found no evidence that meditation beat other active treatments such as medication, exercise, or other therapies (Goyal et al., 2014). That fits the book's pitch: meditation is one useful tool among several, and it is not a replacement for other help. The studies were with adults, so results for teens may differ.

一篇汇总 47 项成人随机试验的大型综述发现，有中等强度的证据表明正念冥想课程能改善焦虑，效果小到中等；同时没有证据表明冥想优于其他积极的治疗方式，比如药物、运动或其他疗法（Goyal 等，2014）。这和书里的说法吻合：冥想是几种有用工具之一，不能取代其他帮助。研究对象是成年人，青少年的结果可能不同。

  • Goyal M, Singh S, Sibinga EM, et al. Meditation programs for psychological stress and well-being: a systematic review and meta-analysis. JAMA Intern Med 2014;174(3):357–368. doi:10.1001/jamainternmed.2013.13018 link链接

How it connects和别处怎么连

It is the daily, longer form of mindfulness (27) and uses the same 'notice and come back' move as thought stopping's gentler version (12). Breath, one of the focus choices, has its own toolkit in Activity 30, and Activity 33 explores the spiritual side some people bring to quiet practice.

它是正念（27）的日常加长版，用的「察觉再回来」动作，和念头叫停（12）那个更温和的版本相同。作为焦点之一的呼吸，在第 30 个练习有一整套工具；第 33 个练习则谈有些人带进安静练习里的灵性层面。
