Step 2 · The main line第 2 步 · 主线

In one sentence一句话

Trying to control what can't be controlled keeps anxiety high; sorting what's yours to steer from what isn't lets you put your energy where it works.想掌控掌控不了的东西，焦虑就降不下来；分清哪些归你管、哪些不归，才能把力气用在起作用的地方。

What the author is getting at作者想说的

Wanting control is normal, but many things can't be controlled no matter how hard we try, so trying to control everything and everyone raises anxiety. Letting go of that need, and focusing on what you can control, lowers it.想要掌控很正常，但很多事再怎么努力也控制不了；想掌控一切人和事，焦虑就会升高。放下这种需要，把注意力放在自己能管的事上，焦虑就会降低。

Step 5 · Four angles第 5 步 · 四个角度

Look at it from four sides从四面看它

What the book says书里怎么说

Wanting control is normal, but many things can't be controlled no matter how hard we try, so trying to control everything and everyone raises anxiety. Letting go of that need, and focusing on what you can control, lowers it.

想要掌控很正常，但很多事再怎么努力也控制不了；想掌控一切人和事，焦虑就会升高。放下这种需要，把注意力放在自己能管的事上，焦虑就会降低。

A common misreading一个常见的误读

'Letting go means not caring what happens.' Ethan still cared about his paper and his friends. Letting go meant moving his effort from what he couldn't change to what he could. Another misreading: 'I should be able to let go instantly.' The book says letting go often feels worse before it feels better.

「放下就是不在乎结果。」伊森照样在乎他的论文和朋友。放下的意思，是把力气从改变不了的事，挪到能改变的事上。另一个误读是「我应该一下子就放得下」。书里说，放下往往先难受一阵，然后才会好受。

Does it still hold today?今天还成立吗

What research says (open when you're curious)研究怎么说（想深入再打开）

Researchers study this under the name 'intolerance of uncertainty': finding it hard to bear not knowing how things will turn out. In an early test, it was the factor that best separated people with generalized anxiety disorder from those without (Dugas et al., 1998), and a later review calls fear of the unknown a core ingredient across many anxiety problems (Carleton, 2016). These findings show a strong link with worry, not proof that it is the single cause.

研究者把这个现象叫做「无法忍受不确定」：很难承受不知道事情会怎样。早期的一项检验发现，它是区分广泛性焦虑症患者和非患者最重要的因素（Dugas 等，1998）；后来的一篇综述称，对未知的恐惧是多种焦虑问题共有的核心成分（Carleton，2016）。这些发现显示它与担心关系密切，但不能证明它是唯一的原因。

  • Dugas MJ, Gagnon F, Ladouceur R, Freeston MH. Generalized anxiety disorder: a preliminary test of a conceptual model. Behav Res Ther 1998;36(2):215–226. link链接
  • Carleton RN. Into the unknown: a review and synthesis of contemporary models involving uncertainty. J Anxiety Disord 2016;39:30–43. doi:10.1016/j.janxdis.2016.02.007 link链接
