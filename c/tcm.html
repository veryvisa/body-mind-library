From取自 A History of Absurd Medicine: The Chinese Edition荒诞医学史·中国篇 · Guang Zi光子

Cues: test yourself提示栏：合上书自测

What is the core mechanism of Zhouyou art?祝由术的核心机制是什么？
It relies on ritualistic suggestion and psychological comfort rather than pharmacological action.它依赖仪式性的暗示和心理安慰，而非药理作用。
Why do people believe in 'like cures like'?为什么人们相信“以形补形”？
It stems from a natural but flawed imagination that shape implies function, which becomes entrenched over time.它源于一种自然但有缺陷的想象，即形状暗示功能，并随时间根深蒂固。
How does the book view modern 'master' healers?本书如何看待现代“大师”治疗者？
They are seen as exploiting public trust and fear with unproven methods, similar to historical charlatans.他们被视为利用公众信任和恐惧，使用未经验证的方法，类似于历史上的江湖骗子。

Notes笔记

This book reveals that many 'traditional' remedies are actually rooted in superstition, imagination, or social pressure rather than science. Understanding this history helps us distinguish between cultural habits and evidence-based health practices.本书揭示了许多“传统”疗法其实源于迷信、想象或社会压力，而非科学。了解这段历史有助于我们区分文化习惯与基于证据的健康实践。

  • Rituals as Medicine巫术与医学
    • Zhouyou art uses rituals and talismans to treat illness through psychological suggestion.祝由术通过仪式和符咒，利用心理暗示来治病。
    • Ancient practices like soul-calling were early forms of psychological comfort.招魂术等远古做法是早期的心理安慰形式。
  • Imagination in Diet药疗与食疗
    • The 'like cures like' logic leads to absurd practices like eating bones for fractures.“以形补形”的逻辑导致了如骨折喝骨头汤等荒诞行为。
    • Alchemical elixirs and detox myths often lack scientific basis and can be harmful.炼丹长生药和排毒神话往往缺乏科学依据，甚至有害。
  • Gendered Medical Myths女性与医疗
    • Suspicious practices like silk pulse diagnosis reflect historical gender biases.悬丝诊脉等可疑做法反映了历史上的性别偏见。
    • Blood-drop kinship tests show how superstition influenced family structures.滴血认亲显示了迷信如何影响家庭结构。
  • Modern Pseudoscience现代荒谬疗法
    • Contemporary fads like chicken blood injections persist due to lack of critical thinking.鸡血注射等当代时尚因缺乏批判性思维而持续存在。
    • Unproven 'master' therapies exploit trust and fear for profit.未经验证的“大师”疗法利用信任和恐惧牟利。
  • Historical First Aid古代急救术
    • Ancient remedies for choking or drowning often relied on folklore rather than physiology.古代针对噎食或溺水的疗法常依赖民间传说而非生理学。
    • Pinching the philtrum is a common reflex response, not a proven medical cure.掐人中是一种常见的反射反应，并非经过证实的医疗治愈法。

Summary in one breath一口气总结

The book traces how superstition, imagination, and social norms have shaped Chinese medical history, often leading to ineffective or harmful practices. It encourages readers to critically evaluate health claims by looking at their historical and logical roots.本书追溯了迷信、想象和社会规范如何塑造中国医学史，往往导致无效或有害的做法。它鼓励读者通过审视健康声明的历史和逻辑根源来批判性地评估它们。

🌱 Try it (small, optional)试一试（很小，随意） Next time you hear a 'miracle cure' tip, pause and ask: 'Is there any scientific evidence for this, or is it just a story?'下次听到“神奇疗法”小贴士时，停下来问自己：“这有科学证据吗，还是只是一个故事？”
