Why do 'obedient' children sometimes collapse quietly?为什么“懂事”的孩子会静悄悄崩溃？
They often suppress their own needs to please parents, leading to hidden emotional exhaustion.他们常压抑自身需求以取悦父母，导致隐藏的情绪耗竭。
How does parental behavior influence a child's habits?父母行为如何影响孩子习惯？
Children mimic what they see; parents' actions, like phone use, directly shape their behavior.孩子模仿所见；父母的行为（如看手机）直接塑造他们的行为。

Summary in one breath一口气总结

This book explores how family interactions, from childhood to marriage, impact our mental well-being. It emphasizes that healthy relationships require boundaries, independence, and mutual respect rather than control or sacrifice.本书探讨了从童年到婚姻的家庭互动如何影响我们的心理健康。它强调健康的关系需要边界、独立和相互尊重，而非控制或牺牲。
