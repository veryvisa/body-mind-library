Cues: test yourself提示栏：合上书自测

Why is intuition sometimes wrong?为什么直觉有时会是错的？
Because it relies on quick patterns and memories that can be biased or inaccurate.因为它依赖于快速的模式和记忆，而这些可能存在偏差或不准确。
How does expertise change intuition?专业知识如何改变直觉？
It turns random guesses into reliable pattern recognition through long-term practice.它通过长期练习将随机猜测转化为可靠的模式识别。
What is the 'dual processing' model?什么是“双重加工”模型？
It describes two tracks of thinking: a fast, automatic one and a slow, analytical one.它描述了两种思维轨道：一种是快速自动的，另一种是缓慢分析的。

Notes笔记

Your intuition is a powerful, fast, and often accurate tool for decision-making, but it works best when you gently check it against facts rather than ignoring it completely.直觉是一种强大、快速且通常准确的决策工具，但只有当你温和地用事实去检验它，而不是完全忽视它时，它才能发挥最大作用。

  • Unconscious Iceberg无意识冰山
    • Most of your thinking happens automatically below the surface of awareness.你大部分的思考都在意识水面之下自动进行。
    • You often know things without realizing you know them.你常常在没意识到的情况下就已经知道了某些事。
  • Social Intuition社会直觉
    • We rely on gut feelings to read other people's emotions and intentions.我们依靠直觉来读取他人的情绪和意图。
    • These quick social judgments help us navigate daily interactions smoothly.这些快速的社会判断帮助我们顺畅地应对日常互动。
  • Expert Intuition专家直觉
    • Intuition becomes reliable only after extensive practice and experience.直觉只有在经过大量练习和经验积累后才变得可靠。
    • Experts use pattern recognition to make fast, accurate decisions in their field.专家利用模式识别在其领域内做出快速且准确的决策。
  • Memory Traps记忆陷阱
    • Our memories are not perfect recordings; they can be easily distorted.我们的记忆不是完美的录像，很容易被扭曲。
    • Expectations can bias what we remember, making us feel certain about wrong facts.预期会扭曲我们的记忆，让我们对错误的事实感到确信。
  • Dual Processing双重加工
    • System 1 is fast and automatic; System 2 is slow and effortful.系统1快速且自动；系统2缓慢且费力。
    • Balancing both systems leads to better decisions than relying on just one.平衡这两个系统比只依赖其中一个能带来更好的决策。

Summary in one breath一口气总结

Intuition is a natural, powerful part of how we think, but it needs a gentle check from our slower, logical mind to avoid mistakes. We are not just rational animals; we are intuitive ones who can learn to trust our gut wisely.直觉是我们思维中自然且强大的部分，但它需要较慢的逻辑思维进行温和的核查以避免错误。我们不仅仅是理性的动物，更是直觉动物，可以学会明智地信任自己的直觉。
