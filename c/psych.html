Cues: test yourself提示栏：合上书自测

Why does high self-esteem correlate with good grades, but not necessarily cause them?为什么高自尊与好成绩相关，但不一定导致好成绩？
Success often leads to high self-esteem, rather than the other way around; the correlation may reflect achievement rather than a causal shield.成功往往导致高自尊，而非反之；这种相关可能反映的是成就，而非因果保护。
What is the difference between a fact and a theory in science?科学中事实与理论的区别是什么？
Facts are agreed-upon observations, while theories are explanations that organize and predict those facts.事实是达成共识的观察，而理论是组织和预测这些事实的解释。
How does the fundamental attribution error affect how we judge others?基本归因错误如何影响我们对他人的判断？
We tend to blame others' behavior on their character rather than situational pressures, leading to harsher judgments.我们倾向于将他人的行为归咎于其性格而非情境压力，导致更严厉的评判。

Summary in one breath一口气总结

Social psychology reveals that our minds are prone to systematic errors in judging ourselves and others, such as self-serving biases and attribution mistakes, which can be understood through rigorous research methods.社会心理学揭示，我们在判断自己和他人心智时容易犯系统性错误，如自我服务偏差和归因错误，这些可以通过严谨的研究方法来理解。
