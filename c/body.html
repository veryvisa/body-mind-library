From取自 The Emperor of All Maladies: A Biography of Cancer众病之王：癌症传 · Siddhartha MukherjeeSiddhartha Mukherjee（悉达多·穆克吉）

Cues: test yourself提示栏：合上书自测

Why is cancer called 'the emperor of all maladies'?为什么癌症被称为“众病之王”？
Because it is a diverse group of diseases that can affect almost any part of the body and has a profound cultural impact.因为它是一组可以影响身体几乎任何部位的疾病，且具有深远的文化影响。
What was the main idea behind the 'War on Cancer'?“抗癌战争”背后的主要想法是什么？
To treat cancer as a unified enemy that could be defeated through coordinated national effort and funding.将癌症视为可以通过协调全国努力和资金来击败的统一敌人。
How does the book describe the relationship between cancer and cell growth?书中如何描述癌症与细胞增长之间的关系？
Cancer is fundamentally defined by the abnormal, uncontrolled growth of cells, which is a common feature across all its types.癌症从根本上定义为细胞的异常、不受控制的增长，这是其所有类型的共同特征。

Notes笔记

Cancer is not a single enemy but a family of diseases defined by uncontrolled cell growth. Understanding its history helps us see it as a complex biological process rather than a mysterious curse.癌症不是一种单一的敌人，而是一组以细胞异常增长为特征的疾病家族。了解它的历史有助于我们将癌症视为复杂的生物过程，而非神秘的诅咒。

  • Ancient Humoral Theories古代体液理论
    • Early doctors linked cancer to stagnant black bile.早期医生将癌症与淤积的黑色体液联系起来。
    • Surgery was the primary, often brutal, treatment method.手术是主要且往往残酷的治疗手段。
  • The War on Cancer抗癌战争
    • Political figures framed cancer as a national enemy to fight.政治人物将癌症框定为需要对抗的国家敌人。
    • This framing mobilized resources but oversimplified the disease.这种框架调动了资源，但简化了疾病的复杂性。
  • Chemotherapy Origins化疗起源
    • Researchers found that certain drugs could kill fast-growing cells.研究人员发现某些药物可以杀死快速生长的细胞。
    • Early trials showed promise but also severe side effects.早期试验显示出希望，但也伴随严重副作用。
  • Cancer as Many Diseases癌症是多种疾病
    • Different cancers behave differently based on their cell type.不同癌症根据其细胞类型表现各异。
    • Treating them as one entity limits effective strategies.将它们视为单一实体限制了有效策略。
  • Prevention and Lifestyle预防与生活方式
    • Avoiding known carcinogens reduces risk significantly.避免已知致癌物可显著降低风险。
    • Lifestyle choices play a role in cancer development.生活方式选择在癌症发展中起作用。

Summary in one breath一口气总结

The book traces cancer from ancient misconceptions to modern science, highlighting that it is a collection of distinct diseases. It emphasizes the importance of understanding biology over viewing it as a simple war to be won.本书追溯了癌症从古代误解到现代科学的过程，强调它是一组不同的疾病。它强调理解生物学的重要性，而非将其视为一场简单的、可以赢得的战争。
