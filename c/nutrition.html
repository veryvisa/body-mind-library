Cues: test yourself提示栏：合上书自测

Why is 'eating what you need' often a myth?为什么“吃什么补什么”往往是谣言？
Because visual resemblance doesn't equal biological function, and the nutrient concentration is often too low to have a significant effect.因为外观相似不等于生物功能相同，且营养素浓度通常太低，无法产生显著效果。
Is overnight water actually dangerous?隔夜水真的危险吗？
No, the nitrite increase is minimal and would require drinking hundreds of liters to reach toxic levels.不，亚硝酸盐增加量微乎其微，需要喝几百升才能达到有毒水平。
Does eating spicy food cause acne?吃辣会导致长痘吗？
Not directly; while some people may react, it is not the main scientific cause of acne for most people.不直接导致；虽然个别人可能有反应，但对大多数人来说它不是长痘的主要科学原因。

Notes笔记

Most viral health tips rely on visual similarities or fear, ignoring the critical role of dosage and scientific context. Learning to question these myths helps reduce unnecessary anxiety about daily habits.大多数网络健康建议依赖视觉相似性或恐惧心理，忽略了剂量和科学背景的关键作用。学会质疑这些谣言有助于减少我们对日常习惯的不必要焦虑。

  • Water Safety饮水安全
    • Nitrite levels in old water are far below toxic thresholds.隔夜水中的亚硝酸盐含量远低于有毒阈值。
    • Boiling water multiple times does not create dangerous toxins.多次烧开的水不会产生危险毒素。
  • Food-Body Logic食物身体逻辑
    • Carrots help night vision but do not cure myopia.胡萝卜有助于夜视，但不能治愈近视。
    • Walnuts contain too little alpha-linolenic acid to significantly boost brain power.核桃中α-亚麻酸含量太低，无法显著补脑。
  • Skin and Diet皮肤与饮食
    • Spicy food is not the primary cause of acne.辛辣食物不是长痘的主要原因。
    • Bananas can sometimes cause constipation rather than relieve it.香蕉有时会导致便秘而非缓解。
  • Weight and Habits体重与习惯
    • Lying down after meals does not directly cause weight gain.饭后躺下不会直接导致发胖。
    • Skipping breakfast is not a guaranteed path to gallstones.不吃早餐并非必然导致胆结石。
  • Sugar and Health糖与健康
    • Excess sugar contributes to weight gain, which is a risk factor for diabetes.过量糖导致体重增加，这是糖尿病的风险因素。
    • Sugar itself does not directly cause diabetes in healthy individuals.糖本身不会直接让健康人得糖尿病。

Summary in one breath一口气总结

This book debunks common food and health myths by applying basic scientific principles like dosage and causality. It encourages readers to trust evidence over viral fear-mongering, helping them make calmer, more rational health decisions.本书通过应用剂量和因果关系等基本科学原理，驳斥了常见的饮食和健康谣言。它鼓励读者相信证据而非病毒式传播的恐慌，帮助他们做出更冷静、更理性的健康决策。
