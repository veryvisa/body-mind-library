From取自 Pregnancy Care Guide怀孕呵护指南 · Liu Ceng Lou Xiansheng六层楼先生

Cues: test yourself提示栏：合上书自测

Does morning sickness harm the baby?孕吐会伤害宝宝吗？
No, the baby gets nutrients from the mother's body, so vomiting usually doesn't affect the baby's growth.不会，宝宝从母体获取营养，所以呕吐通常不影响宝宝生长。
Is pregnancy just a personal choice?怀孕只是个人选择吗？
While it is a choice, the author argues it involves deep emotional and social connections, not just individual decision-making.虽然是个选择，但作者认为它涉及深层的情感和社会联系，而非仅个人决定。
What if morning sickness lasts longer than expected?如果孕吐持续比预期久怎么办？
It is normal for it to vary; sudden cessation for days might be a warning sign, but prolonged duration is often just individual variation.持续时间因人而异是正常的；若突然停止数天可能是警示，但持续较久通常只是个体差异。

Notes笔记

Pregnancy is a profound, shared human experience rather than just a medical event. It involves navigating physical changes, debunking myths, and managing the emotional weight of caring for a new life.怀孕不仅仅是一个医学事件，更是一种深刻且需要共同承担的人生体验。它涉及应对身体变化、破除迷信，以及管理照顾新生命时的情感重量。

Summary in one breath一口气总结

This guide helps expectant parents understand that physical symptoms and daily restrictions are often misunderstood. It emphasizes that pregnancy is a journey of shared care, requiring realistic expectations and emotional support rather than rigid rules.本指南帮助准父母理解，身体症状和日常限制常被误解。它强调怀孕是一段共同关怀的旅程，需要现实预期和情感支持，而非僵化规则。
