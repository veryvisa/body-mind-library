Cues: test yourself提示栏：合上书自测

Is feeling anxious a sign that something is wrong with you?会焦虑，说明我哪里出了问题吗？
No. Almost everyone feels anxious at times. It becomes something to work on when it is strong or frequent enough to get in the way of daily life.不是。几乎人人都会焦虑。只有当它强到、频繁到妨碍日常生活时，才需要专门去处理。
What are the two broad sources of anxiety the book describes?书里说焦虑大体有哪两类来源？
Situational (something in your life is stressful) and chemical (your brain chemistry is tuned toward anxiety). Many people have some of both.情境性（生活里有让人紧张的事）和化学性（大脑化学偏向焦虑）。很多人两样都有一点。
When a situation is making you anxious, what two things can you work on?某件事让你焦虑时，可以从哪两头下手？
The situation itself (e.g., get help, change how you prepare) and the story you tell yourself about it (e.g., 'last year is not this year').事情本身（比如找人帮忙、换个准备方式），以及你对这件事讲给自己的故事（比如「去年不等于今年」）。

Notes笔记

Anxiety is a normal feeling with two broad causes — what happens around you and how your brain chemistry is set — and both can be turned down with therapy, self-help, medication, or a mix.焦虑是正常的感受，大体有两类来源——身边发生的事，和大脑化学的状态；两类都能靠心理治疗、自助练习、药物或它们的组合调低。

Summary in one breath一口气总结

Anxiety is super common — sometimes it's about what's going on, sometimes it's how your brain is wired, and either way there are real ways to turn it down.焦虑真的很常见——有时是因为发生的事，有时是大脑本身的设定，不管哪种，都有实实在在的办法把它调低。

Any length is fine — a few dots on the line count.原书第 3–4 页：画一条简单的时间线，标出到现在为止焦虑出现过的时候；再想想它更像情境性、化学性还是两者都有，以及你对心理治疗、自助、药物各有什么想法。写多写少都行——线上点几个点也算。
