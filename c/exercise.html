  2. The Bible of Running: I Run, Therefore I Am跑步圣经：我跑故我在This book explores running training, competition, and healing from a runner's perspective, helping readers understand the significance of exercise in life.本书通过跑者视角探讨跑步训练、竞赛与身心治愈，帮助读者理解运动对生活的意义。
  3. What I Talk About When I Talk About Running当我谈跑步时，我谈些什么Explores endurance, discipline, and mind-body connection through the experience of running.通过跑步体验探讨耐力、自律与身心连接，适合跑步爱好者。
  4. Barefoot Contessa: The Art of Simple, Healthy Cooking无器械健身This book provides bodyweight training routines to help readers build strength and fitness at home.本书提供无器械自重训练方案，帮助读者在家通过推拉腿核心练习提升力量与体能。

  3. You Can Run Faster你可以跑得更快This book guides runners to optimize training intensity, form, and race strategy using data and science.本书通过量化数据与科学原理，指导跑者优化训练强度、跑姿及比赛策略，提升跑步表现。

Cues: test yourself提示栏：合上书自测

Why is extreme dieting often ineffective long-term?为什么极端节食长期往往无效？
It often leads to metabolic adaptation and significant weight regain after stopping.它常导致代谢适应，停止后体重显著反弹。
What is a key limitation of bodyweight training for beginners?自重训练对初学者有什么关键局限？
It is difficult to adjust resistance direction and load precisely for progressive overload.难以精确调整阻力方向和负荷以实现渐进超负荷。
How does strength training help with fat loss?力量训练如何帮助减脂？
It increases muscle mass, which boosts resting metabolic rate and burns calories.它增加肌肉量，从而提升静息代谢率并燃烧热量。

Notes笔记

Sustainable fitness relies on consistent strength training rather than extreme dieting, and you can build an effective routine in a small space using simple, affordable equipment.可持续的健身依赖规律的力量训练而非极端节食，你可以用简单、低成本的器材在小空间里建立有效的训练体系。

  • Dieting vs. Exercise节食与运动
    • Extreme dieting often leads to weight regain over time.极端节食长期来看往往导致体重反弹。
    • Strength training is effective for fat loss and body composition.力量训练对减脂和身体成分改善很有效。
  • Home Gym Setup居家健身房
    • You can create a functional gym with a small budget.你可以用少量预算打造功能性健身房。
    • Simple tools like dumbbells are versatile for beginners.哑铃等简单工具对初学者很通用。
  • Training Structure训练结构
    • Consistency in frequency matters more than intensity spikes.频率的规律性比强度爆发更重要。
    • Proper warm-up prevents injury and improves performance.适当热身能预防受伤并提升表现。
  • Muscle Group Focus肌群重点
    • Training back and core improves posture and stability.训练背部和核心能改善姿态和稳定性。
    • Balanced training prevents muscle imbalances and pain.均衡训练可预防肌肉失衡和疼痛。
  • Posture Correction姿态矫正
    • Common issues like rounded shoulders stem from sedentary habits.圆肩等常见问题源于久坐习惯。
    • Targeted exercises can help realign the body naturally.针对性练习可帮助身体自然复位。

Summary in one breath一口气总结

This book argues that for busy students, consistent strength training at home is more sustainable and effective for health than crash dieting or relying solely on cardio. It provides a practical framework for setting up a minimal home gym and structuring workouts to improve posture and body composition.本书认为，对忙碌的学生而言，居家规律的力量训练比突击节食或仅靠有氧运动更可持续且有效。它提供了建立极简居家健身房和结构化训练以改善姿态与身体成分的实用框架。
